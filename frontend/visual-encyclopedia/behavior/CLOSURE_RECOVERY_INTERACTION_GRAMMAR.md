# Closure and Recovery Interaction Grammar

Status: Active behavior canon

## Purpose

This file is the durable home for closure, interruption, blockage, stale source, and recovery language across Today and cross-surface surfaces.

## Canonical Interaction Labels

- interrupted
- blocked
- stale
- recoverable

## Visible User Labels

- Still counts
- Waiting
- Blocked
- Needs recovery
- Not needed
- Moved
- Shortened

## Grammar Rules

- interrupted means the user or reality paused the action.
- blocked means an external dependency or constraint prevents progress.
- stale means the source or recommendation may be outdated.
- recoverable means the day can narrow to one safe next step or one closure choice.

## Interaction Actions

- interrupted surfaces may offer resume, close as Still counts, move, or hold.
- blocked surfaces may offer a next control, a handoff, or a safe defer path.
- stale surfaces may offer retry, refresh, or a manual route.
- recoverable surfaces may offer one safe next step or one closure choice.

## Required Order

`Intent -> Preview -> Commit -> Receipt -> Undo / Recover`

## Required Behavior

- Preserve source, proof, and receipt continuity across every state transition.
- Keep one dominant action at rest.
- Keep one visible correction or recovery path.
- Keep recovery language calm, inspectable, and non-shaming.
- Use explicit labels instead of color-only or red-only meaning.
- Do not hide recovery controls.
- Do not use shame, overdue, failure, streak, score, or fake urgency language.

## Surface Use

This grammar governs Today closure surfaces, stale recommendation handling, blocked detail, waiting detail, preview confirmation, and any cross-surface recovery affordance that closes a loop without pretending the loop never broke.
