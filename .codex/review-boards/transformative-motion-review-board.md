# Transformative Motion Review Board

## Purpose
Review all state transformation motion.
## Review Roles
Transformative transition architect, state animation reviewer, Reduce Motion reviewer, performance reviewer.
## Required Evidence
Primitive spec, source/destination states, preview, fallback, validation.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Transformative Motion canon, matched geometry map, Reduce Motion equivalents.
## Green Criteria
State transformation is meaningful, accessible, and bounded.
## Yellow Criteria
Polish/profiling deferred with owner.
## Red Criteria
Decorative, infinite, unreadable, or inaccessible motion.
## Stop Conditions
Motion harms task completion.
## Claim Boundaries
No device/performance/accessibility claims without proof.
## Relation
Feeds DAV10, SIG12, and SIG15 closeout.
