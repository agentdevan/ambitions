# Premium Tactility Board

## Purpose
Review haptic, press, settle, and feedback intent.
## Review Roles
Premium tactility reviewer, accessibility/motor reviewer, iOS reviewer.
## Required Evidence
Haptic intent map, owner surface, native API notes, motor/accessibility notes.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
SIG principles, native iOS constraints, current source, validation.
## Green Criteria
Sparse native feedback clarifies state.
## Yellow Criteria
Human device feel deferred.
## Red Criteria
Gamified/noisy feedback, dependency, or inaccessible tactility.
## Stop Conditions
Reward loops or unsupported device proof claims.
## Claim Boundaries
No physical-device proof unless run.
## Relation
Supports Signature Experience without competing identity.
