## Red Conditions

Forbidden file touch, unsafe operationalization, professional advice claim, new top-level destination, user-data server, hosted AI, silent commitment mutation, fake proof, or completed-history rewrite.

## Evidence Checklist

Files changed, source truth read, validation commands/results, Yellow owners, Red repairs, claim boundaries, rollback path, and next safe batch.

## Rollback Expectations

Revert only the current batch's changed files. Preserve completed evidence and do not rewrite historical completion status.
