# Fluid Motion And Transition Board

## Purpose
Review transitions for state meaning and native fluidity.
## Review Roles
Transformative transition architect, Reduce Motion reviewer, VoiceOver reviewer, performance reviewer.
## Required Evidence
Primitive name, source/destination states, Reduce Motion equivalent, preview, validation.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Transformative Motion System, Reduce Motion equivalents, SIG principles, current code.
## Green Criteria
Motion clarifies state and fallback preserves meaning.
## Yellow Criteria
Manual device profiling deferred.
## Red Criteria
Decorative/infinite motion, missing fallback, readability harm.
## Stop Conditions
Motion delays task completion or hides content.
## Claim Boundaries
No accessibility/performance proof without evidence.
## Relation
Motion layer extends DAV/PXEQ/photo-matched experience.
