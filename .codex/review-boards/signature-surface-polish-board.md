# Signature Surface Polish Board

## Purpose
Review surface-specific SIG polish.
## Review Roles
Owner-surface reviewer, design-system guard, photo-matched visual director, accessibility reviewer.
## Required Evidence
Before/after summary, primary object, preview, scorecard, validation.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Owner surface canon, DAV/PXEQ, Signature Experience Layer, current source.
## Green Criteria
Surface feels more premium and remains useful.
## Yellow Criteria
Polish gaps with named owner.
## Red Criteria
Broad rewrite, card-stack drift, route/schema/dependency touch.
## Stop Conditions
Top-level IA or app behavior drift.
## Claim Boundaries
No readiness claims.
## Relation
Preserves DAV03-DAV09 surface work.
