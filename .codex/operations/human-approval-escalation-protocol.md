# Human Approval Escalation Protocol

Use when a stop condition or approval-required change is reached.

## Steps

1. Stop before the irreversible or high-risk change.
2. Summarize evidence already gathered.
3. State the approval decision needed.
4. Provide safest options and consequences.
5. Do not proceed until the user explicitly approves.

## Approval-Required Areas

Runtime dependencies, persistence migrations, privacy models, sync/auth/accounts, shell replacement, major navigation changes, signed release/TestFlight/App Store work, large historical doc deletion, repeated validation failure, and unresolved canon/reality ambiguity.
