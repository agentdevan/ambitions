# Manual Signoff Checklists

Use these checklists during batch closeout for premium surface work.

Do not claim a checklist item passed unless it was directly reviewed in runtime.

## Today

Confirm:

- first screenful answers what matters now
- hero is dominant
- primary action is singular and calm
- support modules are subordinate
- fixed vs flexible treatment is understandable
- overload still feels calm
- quick recovery / quick focus continuity feels intentional
- Goal Detail and Plan handoffs preserve context
- reduced-motion behavior preserves meaning
- practical readability and heading hierarchy are acceptable

## Goals

Confirm:

- the surface reads like a direction board, not inventory
- one dominant board truth is obvious quickly
- active / pressured / quieter goals are visually distinct
- week relationship is legible without opening Plan
- Horizon Ladder stays shallow and useful
- hero primary action works
- card-to-detail routing works
- create-goal remains non-regressive
- lower-priority disclosure behaves clearly
- reduced-motion behavior is acceptable
- practical readability and accessibility are acceptable

## Goal Intake / Strategy Composer

Confirm:

- the flow feels like composing a believable path, not filling a form
- ambiguity/clarification steps are understandable
- feasibility is readable during setup
- pacing choices feel understandable, not technical
- milestone/phase preview is believable and not noisy
- trust/source framing is calm and confidence-building
- capture-to-goal promotion feels coherent when relevant
- confirmation motion is helpful, not theatrical
- reduced-motion behavior is acceptable
- practical readability and accessibility are acceptable

## Goal Detail

Confirm:

- the first screenful explains the goal strategically
- path filmstrip or equivalent progression is legible
- trust whisper and correction surfaces feel supportive
- memory/audit layers do not bury the main action
- return routes preserve continuity
- reduced-motion behavior is acceptable
- practical readability and accessibility are acceptable
