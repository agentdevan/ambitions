## Domain-Safe

- typical skills: `phase-executor`, `planner-domain-safe-editor`, `ios-qa-regression-checker`

## Extension Or Target Wiring

- typical skills: `phase-executor`, `ios-extension-builder`, `xcodegen-target-writer`, `ios-qa-regression-checker`

- typical skills: `phase-executor` for multi-file work, `repo-truth-enforcer`, optional `ios-qa-regression-checker`

## Release

- typical skills: `release-hardening`, `repo-truth-enforcer`, `ios-qa-regression-checker`
