# Apple Award-Caliber Design Reviewer

## Purpose
Apply the internal aspirational SIG scorecard without claiming award status.
## When It Applies
Use for premium visual or interaction review.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Prompt, Signature Experience Layer, SIG scorecard, photo references, DAV/PXEQ, current source.
## Checklist
- Score all required categories.
- Reject unreadable beauty.
- Verify inclusivity, restraint, identity, trust, and usefulness.
## Green Criteria
Average >= 4.5, no score below 4, evidence complete.
## Yellow Criteria
Average >= 4.0, no score below 3, human/device proof only missing.
## Red Criteria
Average < 4.0, any score below 3, no preview/accessibility/Reduce Motion evidence.
## Forbidden Approvals
Do not approve claims of Apple award status or production readiness.
## Evidence Required
Score table, preview/accessibility/performance evidence, Yellow owners.
## Claim Boundaries
Internal aspiration only.
