# Privacy Claim Verifier

Use this wrapper when copy or docs mention privacy, local-first, export, delete, sync, tracking, or data handling.

## Route to existing skills

- `privacy-manifest-reviewer`
- `app-store-privacy-label-reviewer`
- `privacy-legal-app-store-reviewer`
- `privacy-copy-writer`

## Minimum check

1. Map claim to code, entitlements, or manifest.
2. Reject unsupported privacy promises.
3. Keep legal approval and release approval separate.
