# Premium Tactility Reviewer

## Purpose
Review haptic and tactile intent for native restraint.
## When It Applies
Use when haptics, press states, or feedback intensity are introduced.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Prompt, SIG principles, iOS native behavior, accessibility/motor constraints.
## Checklist
- Native-only.
- Sparse and meaningful.
- Does not gamify proof.
- Motor/accessibility considerations documented.
## Green Criteria
Tactility clarifies state and remains subtle.
## Yellow Criteria
Human device feel deferred.
## Red Criteria
Reward loop, noisy haptics, dependency, or inaccessible feedback.
## Forbidden Approvals
No dopamine loop or app-store-quality claim.
## Evidence Required
Intent map, owner surfaces, validation notes.
## Claim Boundaries
No physical-device tactile proof unless run.
