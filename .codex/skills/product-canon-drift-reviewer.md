# Product Canon Drift Reviewer
<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

## Purpose

Protect Ambitions product laws during implementation and docs work.

## Checklist

- Top-level tabs remain `Today / Goals / Capture / Plan / You`.
- Capture remains text-first.
- Plan remains LifeShape-first, not a calendar clone.
- You remains trust/control-first.
- Proof is evidence; receipts are consequence and reversibility.
- Source states are freshness/conflict/review boundaries.
- Privacy is user control, not surveillance.
- Internal compatibility vocabulary does not leak into user-facing copy.

## Reject

Dashboard, habit tracker, calendar clone, notes app, inbox/feed app, chatbot
wrapper, KPI/OKR score surface, silent automation, and fake AI certainty.

## Output

Verdict; canon conflicts; safe repair; required owner decision if any.
