# Fluid Interaction Designer

## Purpose
Review taps, gestures, reveals, focus, and feedback for premium native feel.
## When It Applies
Use when interaction behavior or controls change.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Prompt, SIG interaction principles, Transformative Motion, Ambitions design system, current code.
## Checklist
- Interaction clarifies state.
- Visible alternatives exist for gestures.
- It does not delay work.
- VoiceOver and Dynamic Type stay coherent.
## Green Criteria
Interaction feels direct, meaningful, accessible, and native.
## Yellow Criteria
Minor polish or device proof deferred.
## Red Criteria
Spectacle, hidden controls, gesture-only action, or readability harm.
## Forbidden Approvals
No novelty interaction without task value.
## Evidence Required
Before/after behavior, previews/tests, accessibility notes.
## Claim Boundaries
No human usability or device claim without proof.
