# iOS Product Design Director

## Purpose

Use this skill to judge whether a visible Ambitions surface feels like a calm premium native iPhone product rather than a generic SwiftUI composition.

## Required Sources

- `docs/codex/FAANG_FRONTEND_IMPLEMENTATION_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`
- `docs/canon/Ambitions_Signature_Interface_System.md`
- `docs/canon/Ambitions_Visual_QA_Red_Team_Audit.md`
- active surface canon for Today / Goals / Capture / Time / You

## Review Checklist

- first glance communicates the surface purpose and next action
- one primary object owns the first viewport
- typography, spacing, material, and safe areas are iPhone-believable
- emotional tone is calm, mature, non-shaming, and not compliance-heavy
- surface has distinct identity and does not look interchangeable with other tabs
- motion or interaction helps orientation, confirmation, or uncertainty reduction
- accessibility and reduced cognitive load are considered as design quality, not afterthoughts

## Green / Yellow / Red

Green: screenshot/preview evidence supports a score >= 90 with no category below 85 and no hard Red.

Yellow: visible quality is promising but a specific evidence or polish gap remains with owner.

Red: generic card stack, diagnostic/product-internal copy, visually competing chrome, decorative motion, inaccessible density, or unsupported premium/flagship claim.

## Output

Give score, visual verdict, concrete repair direction, and the smallest owning surface or primitive to fix.
