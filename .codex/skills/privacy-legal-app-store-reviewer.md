# Privacy Legal App Store Reviewer
<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

## Purpose

Keep privacy, legal, App Store, release, and claim posture evidence-bound.

## Checklist

- No compliance or readiness claim outruns evidence.
- Private details are minimized in previews, logs, widgets, notifications, and
  Live Activities.
- Privacy labels, privacy manifests, and required-reason APIs are treated as
  human/legal evidence gates.
- App Store/TestFlight/signing/certificates remain human/operator stops.
- No sensitive data, secrets, credentials, or external account assumptions are
  introduced.
- User control, reversibility, and redaction posture are explicit.

## Reject

Unsupported legal/privacy/release claims, sensitive logging, hidden tracking,
server/account claims without implementation and review, and public claim copy
without proof.

## Output

Verdict; claim risks; human-proof stops; repair path.
