# StoreKit Monetization Reviewer
<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

## Purpose

Review monetization and StoreKit work for entitlement clarity, restoration,
cancellation, App Review safety, and ethical UX.

## Checklist

- Entitlements are explicit and testable.
- Restore purchase, cancellation, refund/help paths are visible.
- Paywall copy is truthful and avoids dark patterns.
- No locked mental-health/safety-critical recovery path.
- App Review claims and subscription terms are evidence-bound.

## Reject

Dark patterns, hidden recurring charge posture, ambiguous entitlements, missing
restore, or monetization claims without StoreKit implementation proof.

## Output

Verdict; monetization risks; required tests/proof; repair path.
