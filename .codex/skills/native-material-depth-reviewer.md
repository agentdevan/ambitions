# Native Material Depth Reviewer

## Purpose
Protect premium graphite/glass depth from cheap gradients or noisy effects.
## When It Applies
Use for surface backgrounds, panels, chrome, and product-still visuals.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Prompt, photo references, Signature Experience Layer, Ambitions design system.
## Checklist
- Dark graphite base.
- Soft edge light.
- Refined material restraint.
- Readable text.
## Green Criteria
Material improves clarity and feels native.
## Yellow Criteria
Minor polish deferred.
## Red Criteria
Plastic gradient, neon, unreadable glass, or identity split.
## Forbidden Approvals
No decorative material without state or hierarchy purpose.
## Evidence Required
Changed files, preview/screenshot if available, contrast/accessibility notes.
## Claim Boundaries
No human visual approval without review.
