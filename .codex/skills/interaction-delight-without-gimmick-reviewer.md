# Interaction Delight Without Gimmick Reviewer

## Purpose
Separate meaningful delight from novelty.
## When It Applies
Use for delight, fun, microinteraction, motion, haptic, or premium polish.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Prompt, Fluidity and Delight Protocol, Transformative Motion, Product Language System.
## Checklist
- Delight clarifies state.
- Calm after interaction.
- No reward loop.
- Accessibility equivalent exists.
## Green Criteria
Delight is useful and controlled.
## Yellow Criteria
Delight acceptable with polish deferred.
## Red Criteria
Gimmick, gamification, or spectacle.
## Forbidden Approvals
No confetti/streak/fake-intelligence approvals.
## Evidence Required
State explanation, preview, accessibility notes.
## Claim Boundaries
No user-love claim without research.
