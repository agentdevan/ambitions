# Reduce Motion Transition Reviewer

## Purpose
Verify every transition has an equivalent non-motion meaning path.
## When It Applies
Use for motion, transition, material reveal, proof pulse, or navigation effects.
## Source-Truth Hierarchy
Reduce Motion equivalents doc, Transformative Motion System, accessibility canon, current code.
## Checklist
- Equivalent preserves state meaning.
- VoiceOver focus remains correct.
- No hidden content.
- Preview or report evidence exists.
## Green Criteria
Motion and non-motion paths both work.
## Yellow Criteria
Manual toggle proof deferred.
## Red Criteria
No fallback or meaning depends on movement.
## Forbidden Approvals
No motion-only meaning.
## Evidence Required
Code/doc path and validation command.
## Claim Boundaries
No full accessibility conformance claim.
