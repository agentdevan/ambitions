# World-Class Time Bar

## Five-point scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Structurally incompatible with Time law or unusable for the proving slice. |
| 2 | Recognizable intent with major authority, comprehension, accessibility, or category failure. |
| 3 | Plausible concept with unresolved first-viewport, truth-state, density, or native-feasibility weakness. |
| 4 | Strong enough for standalone rendering; material risks are explicit and testable. |
| 5 | World-class structural hypothesis: immediately comprehensible, truth-preserving, distinctive, accessible, operational, and natively credible. |

Scores are selection aids, not mathematical product truth. No average selects a winner.

## Required 5/5 standard

A 5/5 direction:

1. preserves immediate native calendar comprehension;
2. makes the active range and Now obvious;
3. distinguishes accepted, proposed, external, stale, and historical truth;
4. makes protection and fixed reality visible without color alone;
5. avoids treating open calendar space as recommendation;
6. makes conflict participants and consequence explicit;
7. preserves current accepted truth during preview;
8. retains exact temporal object identity through review and return;
9. communicates most of the first viewport before every label is read;
10. feels modern, expensive, rich, restrained, native, and distinctly Ambitions;
11. remains operational rather than infographic-like;
12. supports quiet, typical, dense, and very-dense periods;
13. provides a complete ordered semantic equivalent;
14. preserves native Back, focus, safe areas, and keyboard behavior;
15. avoids duplicating Today; and
16. avoids calendar-clone, dashboard, and AI-planner drift.

## Automatic rendering floor

Reject before rendering any direction below 4/5 on any of:

- calendar comprehension;
- truth-state clarity;
- first-viewport usefulness;
- native feasibility;
- accessibility;
- proposal-versus-accepted integrity;
- conflict clarity; or
- category avoidance.

## Gate questions

### Calendar comprehension

- Can a person identify the week, day relationship, duration, and temporal order without reading a tutorial?
- Do all-day, overlap, recurrence, and source have a credible path without replacing calendar grammar?
- Does the custom structure preserve time-range blocks rather than reduce the surface to cards or an agenda?

### Truth integrity

- Does accepted placement have a structurally different role from proposed placement?
- Does external observation retain its source and avoid native acceptance?
- Are stale, historical, and unknown states explicit without warning-card accumulation?
- Is pending entirely absent unless a durable contract exists?

### Protection and fit

- Is protection expressed by boundary, label, trait, and reading order—not color alone?
- Is Fixed different from Protected and Flexible?
- Does open space remain neutral until personal usability is established?
- Does over-capacity stay distinct from participant-level conflict?

### Conflict and review

- Are both valid participants named?
- Does consequence precede implementation metadata?
- Does current truth remain visible and authoritative?
- Can the person cancel or keep current time without losing context?

### First viewport

- Does it orient before the fold and continue naturally through scrolling?
- Are view switchers and controls subordinate to temporal reality?
- Is Wednesday directly enterable without building a duplicate Today timeline?

### Density and accessibility

- Can quiet weeks remain calm without looking empty or unfinished?
- Can dense weeks preserve identity, state, consequence, action, conflict, and return without tiny blocks?
- Does Accessibility Dynamic Type become an ordered passage or List rather than a shrunken grid?
- Do Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increased Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, RTL, VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, and Full Keyboard Access preserve meaning?

### Native feasibility and category

- Can ordinary depth use framework navigation, native Back, natural scrolling, and native presentation?
- Does every direct manipulation have a named alternative?
- Is the structure an operating calendar rather than a static temporal diagram?
- Would removing the Ambitions accent still leave an ownable structure?
- Does it avoid calendar-grid-first identity, agenda root, dashboard metrics, AI briefing, or Today execution anatomy?

## Required evidence before owner closure

Text selection is insufficient for closure. A later authorized direction must survive:

- standalone native rendering of Week, Wednesday, object detail, review, return, and accessibility equivalent;
- quiet, typical, dense, overlap, and long-title fixture variants;
- Light, Dark, Increased Contrast, Reduce Transparency, and Reduce Motion;
- VoiceOver order and focus-return inspection;
- named-action parity for spatial gestures;
- full-app NavigationStack, Back, sheet/review, safe-area, keyboard, and restoration proof; and
- explicit comparison against Apple Calendar comprehension and the Time anti-pattern register.
