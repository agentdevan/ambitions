# Time Pattern Atlas

These patterns are transferable structural mechanisms, not product expressions or directions.

## 1. Stable period spine

- **Problem:** Range changes can make the person lose where they are.
- **Mechanism:** Keep the exact active range and its relationship to Today stable while chronology changes beneath it.
- **Lineage:** Apple Calendar, BusyCal, Fantastical.
- **Ambitions use:** `This week` plus exact dates remains persistent through day focus and return.
- **Failure:** Range chrome becomes the product identity or a segmented-control toolbar.
- **Accessibility:** Range is a heading; Previous, Today, and Next are named actions.

## 2. Named current-period return

- **Problem:** Temporal exploration needs a reliable way home.
- **Mechanism:** A stable `Today` command returns to the current period without changing preferred supported scale.
- **Lineage:** Apple Calendar, BusyCal, Readdle, Timepage.
- **Ambitions use:** Preserve current week versus Now-in-day distinction.
- **Failure:** Every exit jumps to Today and destroys exact return.
- **Accessibility:** The action names its result, such as `Return to current week`.

## 3. Now witness

- **Problem:** Current position disappears inside dense chronology.
- **Mechanism:** A restrained current-time marker intersects only the current day and recedes from object identity.
- **Lineage:** Native calendars, Weather, Tide Guide, Flighty.
- **Ambitions use:** Now is positional evidence, not a recommendation or moving decorative beam.
- **Failure:** Pulsing, waveform, focus stealing, or constant announcements.
- **Accessibility:** Current relation is announced on entry or request, not every minute.

## 4. Adaptive density grammar

- **Problem:** Quiet, typical, and dense periods need different visual breadth.
- **Mechanism:** Vary display span, detail level, collision grouping, and progressive depth without changing the period owner.
- **Lineage:** Apple Calendar Compact/Stacked/Details; BusyCal variable span.
- **Ambitions use:** Week may foreground Wednesday without hiding the seven-day period.
- **Failure:** User must learn several unrelated mini-products.
- **Accessibility:** Columns reduce and content stacks at large sizes.

## 5. Ordered semantic transform

- **Problem:** Spatial Week geometry cannot survive maximum Dynamic Type or nonvisual reading.
- **Mechanism:** Transform the same range and objects into an ordered chronological passage/List.
- **Lineage:** Apple Calendar and BusyCal List.
- **Ambitions use:** Preserve exact time, authority, source, protection, consequence, actions, and return.
- **Failure:** The transform becomes agenda-as-root or duplicates Today.
- **Accessibility:** Full parity; no meaning is left only in position or duration height.

## 6. Exact-object depth

- **Problem:** Compact temporal blocks cannot carry complete identity and authority.
- **Mechanism:** Selecting the object opens focused detail while preserving period/day/object lineage.
- **Lineage:** Flighty exact flight/aircraft; BusyCal Info Panel; Health record detail.
- **Ambitions use:** Compact Time object detail carries subject, placement, source, and recurrence scope.
- **Failure:** Generic card detail loses the canonical identity or becomes another root.
- **Accessibility:** Opening focuses the object title; dismissal returns to the originating object.

## 7. Authoritative-plus-proposed rails

- **Problem:** Predictions and suggestions can visually replace accepted fact.
- **Mechanism:** Keep authoritative truth and predicted/proposed truth concurrently visible and explicitly named.
- **Lineage:** Flighty official versus predicted times; Morgen preview; Fantastical Proposals.
- **Ambitions use:** Current placement remains primary; proposed placement occupies a separate review rail.
- **Failure:** Dashed accepted-looking block in the same lane.
- **Accessibility:** `Current` is read before `Proposed · Not scheduled`.

## 8. Explicit stale state

- **Problem:** Old source facts look current after connectivity or freshness loss.
- **Mechanism:** Attach source freshness to the primary fact and avoid silent inference.
- **Lineage:** Flighty offline estimation; Weather feature/source availability.
- **Ambitions use:** `Stale Apple Calendar observation` or `Current truth unknown` where applicable.
- **Failure:** Muted color alone or a detached sync badge.
- **Accessibility:** Source and freshness appear in the object’s state value.

## 9. Inspectable provenance

- **Problem:** Unified views erase where a temporal fact came from.
- **Mechanism:** Keep primary view concise while object depth exposes source and lineage.
- **Lineage:** Apple Health Data Sources & Access; Weather source register; Flighty inbound identity.
- **Ambitions use:** External observation retains `Apple Calendar observation` without permanent marker clutter after native import.
- **Failure:** Source color becomes authority state.
- **Accessibility:** Human source name precedes technical identifiers.

## 10. Alternative before commitment

- **Problem:** A single suggested change feels forced or already applied.
- **Mechanism:** Show alternatives with consequences while retaining the current accepted state.
- **Lineage:** Apple Maps routes; Morgen preview; Fantastical Proposals.
- **Ambitions use:** Proposed launch-review move remains inspectable beside protected family time while its unspecified current placement stays explicitly unknown.
- **Failure:** AI recommendation or one visually dominant winner.
- **Accessibility:** Alternatives have unique names and consequences; decline remains valid.

## 11. Named settlement verb

- **Problem:** Gesture completion obscures the durable boundary.
- **Mechanism:** Use explicit `Go`, `Schedule`, or `Confirm`-like action only when settlement authority exists.
- **Lineage:** Maps, Morgen, Fantastical.
- **Ambitions use:** First fixture uses only Cancel/Keep current because commit is unproven.
- **Failure:** Fixture-only control sounds production-capable.
- **Accessibility:** Action announces exact object, change, and consequence.

## 12. Protection as named boundary

- **Problem:** Colored blocks cannot communicate why time must not move.
- **Mechanism:** Combine label, boundary, material weight, and consequence.
- **Lineage:** Fantastical unavailable; Opal sessions; Gentler break/sick/injured.
- **Ambitions use:** `Family time · Protected · No work` changes the local boundary.
- **Failure:** Category color, lock icon alone, or moralized rigidity.
- **Accessibility:** Protection meaning and valid outcomes are written.

## 13. Neutral opening

- **Problem:** Markets routinely turn empty calendar space into recommendation.
- **Mechanism:** State only the observed opening and preserve personal-usability uncertainty.
- **Lineage:** Counter-pattern derived from Fantastical Openings, Morgen availability, and Time canon.
- **Ambitions use:** `After 6:30 PM · Open calendar space · Personal usability unknown`.
- **Failure:** `Best time`, `You can fit`, or automatic Goal suggestion.
- **Accessibility:** The qualification is part of the value, not hidden help.

## 14. Humane interruption accommodation

- **Problem:** Plan divergence becomes failure or shame.
- **Mechanism:** Preserve what remains true, allow recovery state, and suspend pressure.
- **Lineage:** Tiimo Review Today; Gentler Sick/Injured/On a Break.
- **Ambitions use:** Recovery qualifies chronology without becoming a recovery dashboard.
- **Failure:** Streak loss, score, warning cascade, or automatic reflow.
- **Accessibility:** Recovery choices and consequences appear in stable order.

## 15. Direct manipulation plus named alternative

- **Problem:** Drag/resize is fast but inaccessible and can hide consequence.
- **Mechanism:** Keep drag as optional accelerator and expose Move, Change start, Change duration, Keep current, and Cancel.
- **Lineage:** Readdle explicit edit; Apple HIG drag guidance.
- **Ambitions use:** Later preview only after exact conflict and recurrence scope are known.
- **Failure:** Drop commits material change immediately.
- **Accessibility:** Full action parity for VoiceOver, keyboard, Switch Control, and Voice Control.

## 16. Progressive condition disclosure

- **Problem:** Every source, recurrence, warning, and conflict cannot fit in the Week.
- **Mechanism:** Lead with current consequence, then reveal exact detail at object/review depth.
- **Lineage:** Weather headline→hourly→daily; Flighty status→journey→provenance.
- **Ambitions use:** Week shows source/truth marker; detail reveals exact source and recurrence.
- **Failure:** Important authority is hidden as metadata.
- **Accessibility:** Primary state remains in the first semantic group.

## 17. Consequence before metadata

- **Problem:** Technical conflict facts do not explain lived impact.
- **Mechanism:** State what changes or is consumed before revision/source mechanics.
- **Lineage:** Maps ETA/traffic; Flighty delay cause.
- **Ambitions use:** `This would consume protected family time` precedes IDs and recurrence.
- **Failure:** Red overlap plus diagnostic text.
- **Accessibility:** Conflict entry lands on consequence or first participant.

## 18. Source distinct from truth status

- **Problem:** Calendar color is overloaded as source, category, acceptance, and warning.
- **Mechanism:** Treat source and authority as orthogonal semantic fields.
- **Lineage:** Cross-corpus finding.
- **Ambitions use:** `Apple Calendar observation` may be current or stale; Ambitions placement may be accepted or historical.
- **Failure:** One color legend attempts to encode both axes.
- **Accessibility:** Source and truth have separate labels/traits.
