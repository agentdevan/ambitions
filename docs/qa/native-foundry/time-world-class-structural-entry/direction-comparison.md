Scores use 1–5. For quality criteria, 5 is stronger. For risk and complexity, 5 is higher risk/complexity. Scores are not additive, and no average or automatic winner is calculated.

## D01 — Native Week Ledger

- **What it proves:** Whether exceptional truth/protection semantics inside the strongest familiar Week substrate are sufficient to make Time world-class.
- **What it sacrifices:** The greatest amount of ownable product identity; its Week still begins with conventional calendar geometry.
- **Research support:** Apple Calendar’s native grammar and List; BusyCal density/span; Readdle direct-edit parity; Apple HIG navigation/accessibility.
- **What would invalidate it:** Owner or usability review concludes it is merely a calendar clone; the adaptive portrait excerpt is mistaken for a three-day root; or proposals remain accepted-looking despite the open lane.

## D02 — Adaptive Period Passage

- **What it proves:** Whether a Week can retain cross-day orientation and duration without seven tiny columns by opening the focused day inside one continuous passage.
- **What it sacrifices:** Simultaneous spatial comparison across all seven days and some conventional overlap scanning.
- **Research support:** Timepage passage, Structured date-strip/flow, Weather progressive scales, Tide Guide temporal exploration, Wave 3 ordered-passage law.
- **What would invalidate it:** The root reads as an agenda, focused Wednesday duplicates Today, or nonfocused days lose calendar duration/overlap meaning.

## D03 — Interval Object Field

- **What it proves:** Whether stable temporal object identity can be the ownable structure across Week, detail, proposal review, and return while still preserving exact chronology.
- **What it sacrifices:** Some grid immediacy and layout simplicity; connector/label collision becomes a material implementation problem.
- **Research support:** Flighty exact-object/prediction/source lineage, BusyCal detail, Health provenance, Apple Calendar interval grammar, Moonlitt coherent object.
- **What would invalidate it:** The field reads as a graph/diagram, labels lose clear interval ownership, short objects become unreachable, or visual and semantic orders diverge.

## D04 — Protected Ground Week

- **What it proves:** Whether protection, fixed reality, and honest unknown usability can shape Time’s first viewport without capacity scoring or recommendation.
- **What it sacrifices:** Some object-led neutrality; the surface foregrounds constraints even in weeks where protection is not the primary user concern.
- **Research support:** Gentler recovery states, Opal named boundaries, Fantastical availability states, Time’s unequal-capacity canon, Maps consequence-first review.
- **What would invalidate it:** Empty ground reads as usable/recommended, the root resembles a heat map or capacity dashboard, or Protected and Fixed collapse into one visual category.

## D05 — Dual-Truth Week Ledger

- **What it proves:** Whether proposal-versus-accepted integrity and consequence review can become Time’s defining differentiator without hiding familiar chronology.
- **What it sacrifices:** Quiet-root purity and some visual restraint when several proposals exist; relies more heavily on explicit authority copy.
- **Research support:** Flighty official/predicted, Fantastical Proposals, Morgen preview/Schedule, Maps alternatives/Go, VC-09 dual truth.
- **What would invalidate it:** The proposal ledger becomes a planning queue/dashboard, persists empty, ranks suggestions, or makes proposals look installed in the calendar.

## D06 — Articulated Period Atlas

- **What it proves:** Whether one bound atlas of seven readable day leaves can make Week identity ownable while preserving conventional day chronology, exact objects, truth, review, and return.
- **What it sacrifices:** Simultaneous all-day comparison and some native simplicity; the active leaf can be mistaken for a Day/Today root or custom tab system.
- **Research support:** Active provisional Integrated Period Atlas and installed Adaptive Dual-Truth Period Passage, Flighty object identity, Tide Guide/Moonlitt temporal character, Articulated Native Grammar, Apple Calendar substrate, and Weather disclosure.
- **What would invalidate it:** The root reads as Day/Today, leaf edges read as top tabs or need a legend, Week identity disappears, the metaphor becomes decorative under quiet/dense stress, or ordered semantic parity fails.

## Neutral synthesis

- D01 prioritizes familiar comprehension and native feasibility while carrying calendar-clone risk.
- D02 tests continuous cross-day passage without tiny columns while carrying agenda/Today risk.
- D03 prioritizes exact temporal-object continuity while carrying connector and diagram risk.
- D04 prioritizes protection and honest capacity ground while carrying heat-map/dashboard risk.
- D05 prioritizes explicit dual truth while carrying proposal-queue/dashboard risk.
- D06 tests a bound Week atlas with readable day leaves while carrying Day/Today and custom-control risk.

The comparison intentionally leaves those tradeoffs unresolved for owner selection.
