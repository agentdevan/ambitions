# Time Intervention Synthesis

## Structural opportunity

World-class Time is not a prettier week grid. It is a native temporal operating surface in which exact chronology and authority can be understood together: what is accepted, what is merely proposed, what came from an external source, what is stale or historical, what is protected or fixed, what is only calendar-open, and what consequence a change would create.

The market divides those strengths across different products:

- native calendars provide immediate temporal grammar and density handling;
- day planners provide calm focus but drift toward Today;
- Flighty, Weather, Health, and Maps provide truth/source/alternative models but not schedule layout;
- Gentler Streak, Tiimo, and Opal provide humane protection/recovery signals but risk waveforms, AI planning, or scoring; and
- Tide Guide and Moonlitt provide pre-reading temporal identity but risk decorative infographics.

Ambitions must integrate the laws without importing the trade dress or category identity.

## Selected research principles

1. **Week is a place, not a mode.** The exact active range and its relationship to Now remain stable before object detail or view controls.
2. **Calendar grammar is substrate.** Time position, duration, all-day separation, overlap, and chronological order remain familiar.
3. **Authority is structural.** Accepted, proposed, external, stale, historical, and unknown truth differ in grouping, material, label, trait, and action availability.
4. **Current truth never disappears in preview.** Proposed geometry cannot replace the accepted placement until settlement.
5. **Protection changes the boundary.** `Family time · Protected · No work` alters the local temporal field; tint is only reinforcement.
6. **Open is observational.** Calendar-open space does not become personally usable or recommended without valid local authority.
7. **Conflict is participant-level.** It identifies what cannot coexist, why, and what remains accepted; over-capacity stays separate.
8. **Consequence precedes metadata.** Lived effect is understood before revision, source, recurrence, or operation detail.
9. **Objects carry exact identity through depth.** Week, day, detail, review, settlement/cancel, and return resolve the same subject/placement/source identities.
10. **Accessibility is a representation change.** Spatial Week becomes a full ordered chronological passage or List; it is not a shrunken grid or reduced product.
11. **Native navigation owns depth.** Framework Back, interactive Back, focus, safe areas, keyboard, and dismissal are preserved.
12. **Motion explains passage only.** Reduced Motion uses static origin/current/proposed states and deterministic focus.
13. **Quiet is allowed.** Empty space need not be filled, scored, or turned into a recommendation.
14. **Dense means deeper, not smaller.** Identity, truth, state, consequence, conflict, action, and return survive while secondary detail moves deeper.
15. **Capability remains visible in the evidence, not fictional in the UI.** The first fixture can inspect/cancel; it cannot commit, reflow, write externally, issue canonical Receipt, or Undo.

## The bounded flagship family

The first native calibration should eventually prove one coherent family with two identity-safe paths:

```text
Week / This week
→ Wednesday focus
→ Send the launch brief detail
→ Wednesday / Send the launch brief / originating focus

Wednesday focus
→ distinct launch-review conflict trigger
→ current family-time protection versus proposed 5:45–6:15 PM review
→ Keep current or Cancel
→ Wednesday / originating conflict trigger / focus
```

The fixture supplies no stable ID or current accepted time for `launch review`. The review must say that its current placement is unspecified, and must not alias it to `Send the launch brief` or invent a prior interval.

The same family must also expose Thursday’s external prenatal observation and proposed nursery Step without confusing either with accepted Ambitions work.

The Week first viewport must establish:

- exact active range;
- present relationship and Wednesday entry;
- one accepted fixed object;
- one protected boundary;
- one neutral opening or material pressure;
- the beginning of chronology; and
- enough cross-day context to remain Time rather than Today.

It does not need to expose every Event field, the full target scale family, external diff administration, all recurrence detail, or a complete settlement system.

## Distinction without copy dependence

Labels are mandatory but insufficient. The design must also make these relationships pre-readable:

- accepted objects occupy the authoritative chronology;
- proposals remain adjacent, open-ended, or review-bound rather than fully installed;
- external observations retain a source edge/annotation without becoming a colored native Event;
- protected time creates a closed semantic boundary and meaningful reserved space;
- current/proposed review has a stable before/after reading order;
- stale/unknown/history recede through explicit qualification, not illegibility; and
- conflict is a discontinuity between named participants, not ambient red.

## Why the six directions must differ

The core problem has at least six plausible structural centers:

1. familiar native chronology;
2. period passage through the week;
3. exact temporal objects as the primary anchors;
4. protection and usable-space boundaries;
5. current/proposed review as the defining operation; and
6. a restrained Ambitions-specific period atlas that integrates all five without spectacle.

Choosing among them is a genuine product decision. It cannot be reduced to accent, grid styling, card density, or typography. The comparison therefore records what each direction proves and sacrifices rather than calculating a winner.

## Unresolved tensions for owner review

- How much conventional Week grid must remain visible before Time reads like a calendar rather than an atlas?
- How can a portrait iPhone show seven-day identity without tiny columns or an agenda root?
- Should proposed placement live spatially beside accepted chronology or primarily enter through focused review?
- How strongly may protection shape empty space before the surface feels capacity-led or diagrammatic?
- Can one restrained Ambitions-specific spatial law remain operational under quiet and very-dense weeks?
- How much source/freshness state belongs in Week versus object detail?
- Which direction best preserves Time’s distinctness from Today when Wednesday is focused?
- How can List be a complete accessibility equivalent without becoming the product identity?

These tensions remain deliberately unresolved. The six directions test them; this synthesis does not select a winner.
