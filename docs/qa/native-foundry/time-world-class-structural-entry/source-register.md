## Interpretation boundaries

- Apple Design Award descriptions are Apple’s rationale, not proof that a product pattern belongs in Ambitions.
- Official marketing and help documentation are not direct interaction or accessibility proof.
- Forecast uncertainty is not the same as scheduling proposal authority.
- Health trends are not personal time capacity.
- Focus sessions are not automatically canonical protected placements.
- Free/busy availability is not personal usability.
- No screenshot, copy, visual asset, animation, or product trade dress is reused.
