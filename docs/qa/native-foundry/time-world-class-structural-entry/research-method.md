# Research Method

## Question

How can Ambitions Time preserve immediate native calendar comprehension while making accepted, proposed, external, stale, historical, protected, fixed, flexible, and open temporal truth operationally distinct—without becoming a calendar clone, agenda, dashboard, capacity score, AI planner, or duplicate Today?

## Scope

The study uses current public sources available on 2026-07-30 and the repository at `4ced31dc9a9bd2c9a987ad7649eedd7bb1569b72`. It is bounded to the structure necessary for an owner choice about the first Time calibration family.

The market corpus contains 16 distinct products across five archetypes:

1. native calendar comprehension;
2. modern day and schedule planning;
3. state and source distinction;
4. capacity, recovery, and humane planning; and
5. distinctive temporal and spatial information systems.

Some products contribute to more than one archetype. Cross-archetype membership does not increase the distinct-product count.

## Source order

Sources were preferred in this order:

1. Apple Human Interface Guidelines;
2. Apple Design Awards;
3. official Apple Support and App Store pages;
4. official product sites; and
5. first-party help documentation.

Dribbble, Behance, Pinterest, UI kits, template marketplaces, unsourced screenshot galleries, AI-generated concepts, “best calendar UI” listicles, and web dashboards shown inside phone frames were excluded.

Official marketing and help pages are treated as evidence of declared product structure, not proof of complete accessibility, reliability, or runtime behavior. Matrix accessibility scores are structural adaptability hypotheses, not verified claims about each product.

## Analysis procedure

Each relevant product was evaluated for:

- period identity and stable range orientation;
- Now/Today representation and return;
- temporal object grammar, including range, duration, overlap, all-day, source, and recurrence;
- current, proposed, forecast, external, stale, historical, and unknown truth treatment;
- protection, fixed reality, flexibility, open space, and recommendation risk;
- conflict participants, consequence, review, settlement, cancellation, and return;
- quiet, typical, dense, overlapping, long-copy, and Dynamic Type behavior;
- native controls, direct manipulation, and explicit named alternatives;
- emotional character and pressure/shame risk; and
- likely Ambitions category drift.

Findings were synthesized into a pattern atlas and anti-pattern register before the six directions were drafted. Each direction was then checked against the world-class Time bar and the capability ceiling. No aggregate score selected a winner.

## Apple platform floor

The current Apple evidence establishes a platform floor rather than a house style:

- purpose, hierarchy, consistency, and accessibility start the design decision;
- familiar navigation and controls should remain intact where custom behavior is unnecessary;
- interfaces must be perceivable and adaptable without relying on a single sensory channel;
- Dynamic Type expands and recomposes rather than shrinking required meaning;
- Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency require authored semantic equivalents; and
- 2026 Apple Design Award recognition favors distinctive, platform-fit interaction and inclusive information design: Structured was an Inclusivity finalist; The Outsiders: Athlete Tracker by Gentler Stories LLC was an Interaction finalist; Moonlitt won Interaction; and Tide Guide won Visuals and Graphics.

Exact citations and access notes are in [source-register.md](source-register.md).

## Repository reconciliation method

Repository evidence is separated into four tiers:

1. **Normative target:** Time, object, journey, navigation, visual, and accessibility canon.
2. **Installed provisional direction:** `AVF-TIME-S07-R01` and `VC09-TIME-S07-R00`.
3. **Current source evidence:** the week projection, TimeBlock graph/store, object-store manifest, and mutation registry cited by RP-04.
4. **Executable proof:** none produced in this packet; builds, tests, Xcode, Simulator, and screenshots were prohibited.

Type or file presence never upgrades target behavior to current capability. The older RP-04 audit is used as a required map and rechecked against the cited current source at the packet baseline.

## Direction-generation discipline

The lead author produced all six directions. Research lanes did not write competing directions. A valid direction had to:

- generate the bounded Week → Wednesday → object → review → return family;
- preserve a complete ordered accessibility equivalent;
- keep proposed Goal timing visibly non-accepted;
- show external observation as source-owned fact;
- preserve protected family time during conflict review;
- avoid unsupported mutation controls; and
- remain materially distinct from the other five in primary spatial model and first-viewport hierarchy.

## Research limitations

- No paid product accounts, private betas, or exhaustive direct interaction were used.
- `Current` means an official product page, App Store listing, or first-party help page was available at the 2026-07-30 research snapshot. It does not mean the latest installed binary or every per-product version was independently verified.
- Public product pages can omit edge cases and accessibility details.
- Apple Calendar’s iPhone week view remains orientation-dependent in Apple’s public guide and is evidence of familiar grammar, not a prescription for Ambitions portrait structure.
- Official product claims were not treated as independent usability proof.
- The six directions are text hypotheses. Native rendering is required to evaluate actual density, touch ergonomics, motion, focus, and material quality.

## Proof ceiling

This study supports a structural owner decision. It does not prove a winning direction, native rendering quality, SwiftUI implementation, direct-device accessibility, production capability, or runtime settlement.
