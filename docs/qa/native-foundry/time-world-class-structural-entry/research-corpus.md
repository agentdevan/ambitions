# Research Corpus

Corpus size: **16 distinct current products**

Archetype labels:

- **A** — Native calendar comprehension
- **B** — Modern day and schedule planning
- **C** — State and source distinction
- **D** — Capacity, recovery, and humane planning
- **E** — Distinctive temporal and spatial systems

## 1. Apple Calendar — A

- **Period identity:** Stable month/day context, density modes, multi-day and List views, and named return to current context preserve range orientation.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Conventional all-day region, time-range blocks, proportional duration, recurrence, source calendar, invitations, and exact edit grammar establish the baseline people already understand.
- **Truth/protection:** Calendar membership and invite status exist, but accepted/proposed/external/protected authority is not a complete semantic system.
- **Conflict/density:** Spatial overlap is visible; month density adapts through Compact, Stacked, and Details. Overlap alone does not explain conflict consequence.
- **Interaction/accessibility:** Detail, edit, drag, recurrence scope, and List demonstrate native depth and alternate chronology; Week’s iPhone public guide is partly landscape-dependent.
- **Emotional character:** Neutral, serious, reliable, and anonymous.
- **Ambitions failure:** Copying the grid, source colors, and view switching would create a calendar clone without dual-truth identity. [SRC-P01–P08](source-register.md)

## 2. Fantastical — A/C

- **Period identity:** Broad period family and calendar sets retain range context while changing visible sources.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Familiar calendar blocks and detailed object editing remain primary.
- **Truth/protection:** Busy, free, unavailable, and maybe-available states show more than binary occupancy. Proposals remain in a distinct proposal context before confirmation.
- **Conflict/review:** Proposal collection and confirmation provide useful pending-versus-accepted precedent.
- **Density:** Powerful views and sets scale to complex calendars but can become feature-heavy.
- **Interaction:** Explicit submission complements fast entry; availability tooling is operational.
- **Emotional character:** Polished, powerful, professional.
- **Ambitions failure:** Openings can equate unscheduled time with bookable availability; copying would make Time a premium calendar with Ambitions tint. [SRC-P15–P20](source-register.md)

## 3. BusyCal — A

- **Period identity:** Day, Week, Month, Year, and List with adjustable 2–7-day spans keep the active period explicit.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Familiar time columns, tasks, event detail, and Info Panel preserve exact object grammar.
- **Truth/protection:** Source and task/event distinctions exist, but Ambitions authority states would need a separate structure.
- **Conflict/density:** Alternative overlap layouts, zoom/span controls, and decluttering show that one compressed grid cannot serve all density.
- **Interaction:** Direct object detail and view controls remain practical.
- **Accessibility:** List and variable span are strong structural precedents; verified assistive behavior was not assessed.
- **Emotional character:** Dense, serious, expert-oriented.
- **Ambitions failure:** Bottom mode tabs, heat-map/statistical treatments, and control prominence risk dashboard and segmented-control identity. [SRC-P21–P26](source-register.md)

## 4. Calendars by Readdle — A/B

- **Period identity:** Day, Week, Month, and List retain recognizable chronology.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Events, tasks, recurrence, time ranges, and offline behavior remain familiar.
- **Truth/protection:** Task and Event are distinguished, but drag-scheduled tasks can appear committed immediately.
- **Conflict/review:** Drag and explicit edit coexist; Undo follows a drop. Consequence review is not Ambitions’ full dual-truth model.
- **Density:** Planner and calendar integration increase utility but also surface competition.
- **Interaction:** Fast drag scheduling plus named edit is useful evidence for gesture parity.
- **Emotional character:** Practical and productivity-forward.
- **Ambitions failure:** A planner beside a calendar would become a card/dashboard hybrid and could falsely accept Goal timing. [SRC-P27–P30](source-register.md)

## 5. Structured — B

- **Period identity:** A stable week date strip anchors a vertically flowing day.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Duration and sequence read clearly; Inbox keeps unscheduled work separate.
- **Truth/protection:** Scheduled versus unscheduled work is legible, but accepted/external/stale authority is not its primary problem.
- **Conflict/density:** Daily focus reduces overlap complexity by narrowing the active temporal field.
- **Interaction:** Horizontal date passage plus vertical schedule is immediate and calm.
- **Accessibility:** Linear structure is adaptable and Structured’s 2026 ADA finalist status is relevant, but this research did not verify the app.
- **Emotional character:** Calm, supportive, execution-oriented.
- **Ambitions failure:** Using its anatomy for Time would create a duplicate Today timeline or agenda root. [SRC-P32–P35, SRC-A21](source-register.md)

## 6. Timepage — A/B/E

- **Period identity:** Hourly/Grid Week, refreshed Day, Month, swiping, and explicit Today return create strong period passage.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Time position and event blocks remain legible while the product adds distinctive movement.
- **Truth/protection:** Heat-map and briefing layers do not solve Ambitions’ accepted/proposed/source distinction.
- **Conflict/density:** All-day compaction and view size controls manage density.
- **Interaction:** Gesture-rich period passage feels alive and premium.
- **Accessibility:** Multiple modes could help adaptation but can also increase control burden.
- **Emotional character:** Expressive, tactile, designed.
- **Ambitions failure:** Heat maps, customization, and daily briefings directly match the packet’s rejected categories. [SRC-P31](source-register.md)

## 7. Tiimo — B/D

- **Period identity:** Visual day passage, week/month planning, and time countdowns keep the current activity understandable.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Time is readable through blocks and sequence, with stronger focus than calendar breadth.
- **Truth/protection:** Flexible rescheduling and Review Today normalize change, but AI planning and colorful icon grammar can blur suggestion authority.
- **Conflict/density:** The product reduces overload through readable sequence rather than dense overlap.
- **Interaction:** Gentle nudges and flexible rearrangement support recovery.
- **Accessibility:** Neuroinclusive intent and 2025 iPhone App of the Year recognition are relevant; runtime modes were not verified here.
- **Emotional character:** Kind, supportive, forgiving.
- **Ambitions failure:** AI co-planning, icon color, and day-execution identity would duplicate Today and weaken restrained temporal authority. [SRC-P36–P38](source-register.md)

## 8. Morgen — B/C/D

- **Period identity:** Week default with previous/current/next controls keeps the range named.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Calendar, tasks, due dates, and scheduled work remain distinct.
- **Truth/protection:** AI recommendations remain in preview until `Schedule`; due date does not itself equal scheduled block; busy-calendar settings expose availability sources.
- **Conflict/review:** Preview, adjust, and explicit Schedule are useful current/proposed mechanics.
- **Density:** Multi-calendar planning is powerful but desktop-first.
- **Interaction:** Named settlement is stronger than drag-only acceptance.
- **Emotional character:** Efficient, optimized, work-oriented.
- **Ambitions failure:** AI-first planning and inferred capacity could dominate chronology and turn Time into enterprise scheduling software. [SRC-P39–P44](source-register.md)

## 9. Flighty — C/E

- **Period identity:** One exact flight remains stable across departure, inbound aircraft, delay, arrival, history, and Live Activity contexts.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes exact temporal object identity and movement rather than period layout.
- **Truth/protection:** Official times remain alongside predictions. Offline mode explicitly marks estimated data. Exact inbound-aircraft lineage makes the source of movement inspectable.
- **Conflict/review:** The product preserves accepted/official truth while presenting changed expectations and causes.
- **Density:** Current consequence leads; supporting timeline and provenance disclose progressively.
- **Interaction:** Object-first depth retains continuity.
- **Emotional character:** Precise, expensive, serious, alive.
- **Ambitions failure:** Aviation-specific prediction graphics cannot be copied; the transferable law is dual authority and exact identity. [SRC-P45–P48](source-register.md)

## 10. Apple Weather — C/E

- **Period identity:** Current condition, hourly sequence, ten-day forecast, alerts, and maps preserve nested temporal scales.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes clear present-versus-forecast hierarchy.
- **Truth/protection:** Source availability and regional limitations are explicit; current, forecast, alert, and historical averages remain different facts.
- **Conflict/review:** Consequence is foregrounded through severe alerts rather than generic warning-card accumulation.
- **Density:** Headline condition leads, then progressive hourly/daily/detail disclosure.
- **Interaction:** Stable current condition plus drilldown makes changing information understandable.
- **Emotional character:** Calm, spatial, information-rich.
- **Ambitions failure:** Forecast styling must not be reused as proposal authority; weather cards and charts would turn Time infographic-like. [SRC-P09–P10](source-register.md)

## 11. Apple Maps — C

- **Period identity:** Origin, destination, and route context remain stable while alternatives change.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes alternative-before-commit interaction.
- **Truth/protection:** Routes remain alternatives with ETA, distance, and traffic consequence until the person taps `Go`.
- **Conflict/review:** Multiple valid options coexist without one silently replacing current navigation.
- **Density:** Consequence leads; technical route details remain subordinate.
- **Interaction:** Named settlement and explicit end/resume provide a strong review boundary.
- **Emotional character:** Direct, consequential, native.
- **Ambitions failure:** Route selection cannot be mapped literally to scheduling; it supplies consequence-first choice and explicit settlement only. [SRC-P11–P12](source-register.md)

## 12. Apple Health — C/D

- **Period identity:** Day/week/month/year trends and exact records retain temporal context at several scales.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes source and history depth.
- **Truth/protection:** Device, app, provider, and manual sources remain inspectable through Data Sources & Access.
- **Conflict/review:** Source precedence and exact records help reconcile facts without collapsing provenance.
- **Density:** Highlights and trends compress large history into progressive detail.
- **Interaction:** Object/source detail remains native and exact.
- **Emotional character:** Clinical, trustworthy, data-heavy.
- **Ambitions failure:** Card metrics, trends, and health-style summaries would make Time a capacity dashboard. [SRC-P13–P14](source-register.md)

## 13. Gentler Streak — D/E

- **Period identity:** Activity Path situates the present inside recent history and a dynamic recommended range.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes humane capacity and recovery meaning.
- **Truth/protection:** Active, Sick, Injured, and On a Break are named states that change recommendations and notification behavior.
- **Conflict/review:** Recovery is treated as legitimate state rather than failure.
- **Density:** A compact visual field communicates trajectory before labels are read.
- **Interaction:** History-relative movement feels alive.
- **Emotional character:** Humane, nonjudgmental, personally responsive.
- **Ambitions failure:** The waveform and recommended range would become a decorative capacity score if transferred to Time. [SRC-P49–P51](source-register.md)

## 14. Opal — D

- **Period identity:** Named sessions define exact protection windows and current posture.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes operational boundaries and exception policies.
- **Truth/protection:** Block/allow lists, break policies, Hard Mode, and Vacation Mode make protection more than tint.
- **Conflict/review:** Rules expose consequence before a person weakens protection.
- **Density:** Focus sessions and reports scale over time.
- **Interaction:** Strong named boundaries are explicit and consequential.
- **Emotional character:** Determined and protective, sometimes coercive.
- **Ambitions failure:** Scores, streaks, leaderboards, comparisons, and hard-lock rhetoric create shame and dashboard drift. [SRC-P52–P54](source-register.md)

## 15. Tide Guide — E

- **Period identity:** Full-screen time-positioned chart, live condition, scrub inspection, and day/month/year passage make temporal position pre-readable.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes continuous position and changing-condition legibility.
- **Truth/protection:** Forecasted physical condition is not accepted/proposed schedule authority.
- **Conflict/review:** No applicable participant-level conflict model.
- **Density:** Minute-level detail and larger-scale passage coexist through progressive inspection.
- **Interaction:** Press-to-scrub and authored motion make time feel alive.
- **Emotional character:** Rich, atmospheric, premium, practical.
- **Ambitions failure:** A waveform, sweeping animation, or static chart would make Time a decorative temporal infographic. [SRC-P55–P56, SRC-A21](source-register.md)

## 16. Moonlitt — E

- **Period identity:** A coherent lunar object persists across changing phases, celestial events, and planning horizons.
- **Calendar comprehension:** Not a calendar; contributes ownable single-object temporal continuity.
- **Truth/protection:** Astronomical fact and forecast context differ from user-owned schedule authority.
- **Conflict/review:** No applicable placement conflict model.
- **Density:** A single strong object can carry time, movement, and detail without dashboard accumulation.
- **Interaction:** Apple’s 2026 Interaction award emphasizes intuitive, platform-fit exploration and restrained Liquid Glass integration.
- **Emotional character:** Distinctive, calm, alive.
- **Ambitions failure:** Scenic atmosphere or celestial metaphor would obscure exact chronology; only coherent-object continuity transfers. [SRC-P57, SRC-A21](source-register.md)

## Corpus conclusion

No reviewed official source established a product that combines familiar Week chronology, exact temporal object identity, accepted/proposed dual truth, source freshness, protected personal meaning, conflict participants, humane capacity uncertainty, truthful settlement, and exact return. Conventional calendars address comprehension but tend to flatten authority. Modern planners address immediacy but can drift toward Today. Information systems address state richness but risk infographic identity. Ambitions’ opportunity is the integration of those laws, not the imitation of one product.
