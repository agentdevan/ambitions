## Reading the matrix

- Calendar products set the comprehension and density floor but have high calendar-clone risk.
- Flighty, Maps, and Morgen provide the strongest transferable current/proposed or alternative-before-commit evidence.
- Flighty, Weather, and Health provide the strongest source/freshness/provenance evidence.
- Gentler Streak, Tiimo, and Opal show humane protection/recovery mechanisms, but each carries identity risks: waveform, day-planner/AI, or score/lock rhetoric.
- Tide Guide and Moonlitt show how temporal information can feel alive before every label is read, but their expression cannot become Time’s operating model.
- No row is a complete Ambitions solution; high relevance and high copy risk often coincide.
