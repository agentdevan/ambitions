# World-Class You Bar

A 5/5 You direction must:

1. communicate personal control and local ownership in the first viewport;
2. preserve the nine canonical domains in exact order;
3. reach into Notifications & Attention without compressing all domains;
4. remain immediately comprehensible as a native iPhone control surface;
5. avoid account, profile, cloud, subscription, and device-management implications;
6. distinguish person-entered, confirmed, suggested, inferred, uncertain, historical, superseded, and removed truth;
7. keep current truth authoritative over suggestion and history;
8. disclose sensitive source, purpose, and consequence before technical metadata;
9. keep unsupported commands and permissions absent;
10. present Appearance as finite choices with one controlled specimen;
11. preserve native Back, interactive Back, focus, safe areas, keyboard behavior, and exact return;
12. communicate most of the first viewport before every summary is read;
13. support sparse, typical, verbose, and localized states without container accumulation;
14. transform structurally at Accessibility Dynamic Type;
15. preserve non-color equivalents, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, and Increased Contrast;
16. feel modern, expensive, rich, restrained, native, local, trustworthy, personal, calm, and operationally clear;
17. avoid Settings-clone, account-page, privacy-dashboard, AI-profile, and generic-list identity; and
18. remain a selectable structural hypothesis rather than a static infographic.

## Pre-render rejection floor

Reject before rendering any direction below 4/5 on:

- native comprehension;
- canonical-domain integrity;
- local/no-account truth;
- personalization-state integrity;
- first-viewport usefulness;
- accessibility;
- capability honesty;
- native feasibility; or
- category avoidance.

No aggregate score selects a winner. A direction may be strong in one dimension and invalid in another.
