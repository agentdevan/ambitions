# Research Method

## Question

How can You feel like a precise, private, personal control surface rather than Settings, an account page, a privacy dashboard, an AI memory profile, or a generic grouped list?

## Boundary

Research was limited to information needed to select a structural direction for the bounded You journey. It did not audit unrelated repository roots, research backend architecture, produce component APIs, or form a production roadmap.

## Authority order

1. Current You canon and installed visual closures
2. Current capability audit reconciled with cited live source
3. Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Apple Support
4. Official product and help documentation
5. Structural synthesis by the Foundry lead

Historical or broader target inventories yield to the current You specification and migration blueprint when they conflict.

## Corpus construction

The corpus contains 15 distinct current products across five archetypes:

- native settings and control comprehension;
- privacy and source boundaries;
- local ownership and data control;
- personalization and appearance;
- calm, distinctive personal systems.

Only Apple guidance/support and official product/help sources were used. Dribbble, Behance, Pinterest, UI kits, template marketplaces, screenshot galleries, listicles, and generated concepts were excluded.

## Questions applied

For each product, the research examined:

- how the root communicates scope, current state, and ownership;
- how depth is entered and exact return is preserved;
- how permissions disclose source and purpose;
- how local, synced, account, historical, uncertain, and removed states differ;
- whether appearance remains controlled or expands into a theme market;
- what survives at large Dynamic Type without becoming a dashboard;
- where account identity, recommendations, scores, or disabled rows create drift; and
- what would be structurally useful without copying product expression.

## Direction synthesis

The six directions were written by one synthesis owner. They are different spatial and semantic models, not styling variants. No direction was selected, ranked into a winner, or promoted to implementation.

## Review method

Two separate final passes were performed:

1. research integrity: current official sources, attribution, corpus diversity, citation completeness, and no predetermined winner;
2. flagship authority and accessibility: exact domain law, capability honesty, local/no-account truth, structural Dynamic Type, category avoidance, and no implementation authorization.
