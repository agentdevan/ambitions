# Research Corpus

Corpus size: **15 distinct current products**.

## A. Native control comprehension

### 1. Apple Settings

Grouped rows, stable hierarchy, concise current-state summaries, system-owned permissions, and predictable disclosure provide the strongest native comprehension baseline. Its weakness for Ambitions is generic identity: copying the form unchanged would make You indistinguishable from Settings.

### 2. Apple Health

HealthKit guidance keeps sensitive authorization contextual, explains purpose, and leaves permission control with the system. This supports precise source/purpose disclosure. Health dashboards and trend summaries are inappropriate root models for You.

### 3. Apple Passwords

Security-sensitive categories, authenticated depth, history, and deleted-state separation demonstrate calm object control. Account continuity, security recommendations, and vault/category identity would overstate Ambitions capability.

### 4. Apple Journal

Journal Suggestions separates on-device suggestion sources into explicit categories and system privacy control. It is useful lineage for suggested-versus-person-entered truth, but the OS suggestion broker itself is not an Ambitions model.

## B. Privacy and source boundaries

### 5. 1Password

Official privacy settings map each permission to an exact purpose. Deep security organization is excellent; account, vault, team, and admin structures are disallowed drift for a no-account local app.

### 6. Signal

Privacy controls explain visible consequence in plain language and use native appearance choices. Profile and account identity remain central to Signal and must not migrate into You.

### 7. Proton Pass

Exact item history and current-versus-prior snapshots clarify temporal state. The profile-to-settings route, subscription suite, vaults, account, and security-product anatomy are not valid root identity for You.

## C. Local ownership and data control

### 8. Day One

Official guidance exposes settings, privacy, and export as explicit depths. Its broad account, sync, subscription, and export surface shows why You must capability-gate rows instead of building an everything menu.

### 9. Bear

Bear communicates private note ownership, safety, export, and appearance with a calm native posture. Its theme catalogue and broad settings inventory would turn Appearance into a gallery.

### 10. Obsidian

Obsidian makes local files and powerful configuration legible, but extensive plugins, themes, and advanced options demonstrate the cost of turning personal control into a control-panel product.

### 11. Anytype

Anytype states local-first storage, encryption, deletion, local-only mode, and data location explicitly. Its vault, identity, membership, key, channels, API, and sync model is substantially broader than Ambitions and must not be implied.

### 12. Standard Notes

Standard Notes clearly separates encrypted and decrypted backups, history, protection, archive, and permanent deletion. Its account/encryption/backup capability makes strong controls truthful there; Ambitions must omit analogous commands until supported.

## D. Personalization and appearance

### 13. Tiimo

Tiimo frames personalization as reducing friction and includes system appearance and accessibility choices. Profile, icon, font, and broader theme customization show how humane options can still become a personalization suite.

### 14. Things 3

Things uses restrained preferences, platform-standard routes, explicit integration settings, and limited appearance choices. Its clarity supports progressive disclosure; cloud sync and independent widget themes are outside Ambitions flagship scope.

## E. Humane personal posture

### 15. Gentler Streak

Gentler Streak uses nonjudgmental language and locally processes Apple Health data for core health experiences. Its profile, wellness dashboard, recommendations, metrics, subscription, and character customization are useful anti-pattern boundaries for You.
