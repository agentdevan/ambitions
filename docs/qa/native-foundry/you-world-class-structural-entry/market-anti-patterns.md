# Market Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it is common | Why it first appears useful | Why it fails Ambitions | Native alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account hero | Consumer apps organize ownership around identity | Gives a strong personal anchor | Flagship You has no account and would fabricate capability | Plain local ownership summary in Identity & Local Data |
| Avatar profile | Profiles personalize an otherwise generic root | Feels human and recognizable | Implies social/account identity and crowds out control | Text-led You crown and domain passage |
| Settings clone | Grouped lists are familiar and inexpensive | Immediate usability | Produces no Ambitions identity beyond labels and accent | Native rows with local ownership and truth-state continuity |
| Privacy dashboard | Security products summarize many signals | Appears reassuring at a glance | Turns privacy into monitoring, grades, or alarm | Exact domain state and purpose-bound depths |
| Privacy or security grade | Scores compress complexity | Feels decisive | Creates judgment, false precision, and optimization pressure | Named current state and actionable consequence |
| AI memory dossier | AI products expose stored personalization | Appears transparent | Recasts the person as generated content and blurs inference with truth | Provenance-labelled Personalization passage |
| “What Ambitions Knows” | Knowledge lists promise explainability | Makes hidden state visible | Suggests totalizing, surveillance-like understanding | Bounded preferences and provenance by domain |
| Profile completion | Onboarding patterns encourage setup | Shows progress | Creates pressure and invents a finish state for personal control | No completion metric; absent unsupported rows |
| Recommendation above controls | Optimization products prioritize advice | Offers an apparent next step | Displaces ownership and turns You into coaching | Current state first; actions only inside owning depth |
| All domains in one viewport | Dashboards maximize overview | Feels efficient | Makes rows tiny, hides hierarchy, and prevents calm scanning | Natural scroll reaching Notifications in viewport one |
| Card stack settings | Cards signal premium visual design | Separates topics visibly | Adds container noise and dashboard identity | Grouped material planes and native disclosure |
| Disabled future controls | Roadmaps expose coming capability | Makes the product feel expansive | Misrepresents support and creates dead ends | Unsupported controls absent |
| Permission switch duplication | In-app toggles look direct | Keeps control inside the app | Can lie about system authority and permission status | State row plus named system-settings route |
| Broad data-management center | Mature services offer export/reset/delete | Signals control and compliance | Current Ambitions lacks the durable command surface | Capability-gated inspection; commands absent |
| Theme gallery | Appearance is marketable and expressive | Feels personal and rich | Turns Appearance into browsing and fragments root identity | Finite System/Light/Dark plus approved accents |
| Swatch wall | Many accents are easy to visualize | Shows variety immediately | Makes color collection the product and weakens hierarchy | Labelled finite choices and one live specimen |
| Root-specific themes | Per-surface customization feels powerful | Promises tailored contexts | Breaks global hierarchy and multiplies state | One app-wide appearance posture |
| Color-only personalization state | Color makes provenance compact | Preserves visual density | Fails accessibility and makes uncertainty easy to miss | State label, ordering, shape, and source |
| Diagnostics console | Technical transparency looks trustworthy | Offers detailed evidence | Exposes implementation rather than personal consequence | Concise status with focused diagnostic depth only if supported |
| Help-center root | Settings often accumulates support links | Makes every answer reachable | Turns You into an everything drawer | About Ambitions remains bounded to supported identity |

## Banned direction drift

Any direction is automatically invalid if its root depends on an account hero, avatar, dashboard, score, recommendation feed, AI dossier, all-nine-domain compression, card-stack identity, theme gallery, disabled unsupported rows, or duplicated system permission switches.
