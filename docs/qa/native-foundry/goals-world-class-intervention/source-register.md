## Interpretation boundaries

- ADA descriptions are Apple’s award rationale, not evidence that every interaction is suitable for Ambitions.
- Official marketing and help pages describe product behavior but do not prove accessibility completeness. Matrix accessibility scores are researcher adaptability hypotheses, not verified product support claims.
- No screenshots, copy, assets, animations, or product trade dress are reused.
- Product-specific observations in the pattern atlas are structural interpretations grounded in the sources above.
