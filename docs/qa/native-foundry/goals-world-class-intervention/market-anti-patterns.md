# Market Anti-Patterns

## Register

| Anti-pattern | Why the market uses it | Initial appeal | Why it fails Ambitions | Harm or drift | Prefer native default when… |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generic project stepper | Makes ordered work obvious | Familiar current/next markers | A living pursuit is not a predetermined delivery plan | False certainty and project-management identity | The truth is genuinely a short, fixed, transactional sequence |
| Horizontal roadmap | Fits many milestones into a dramatic line | Implies momentum and horizon | It invents proportional space and invites clipped geometry | Future Steps look committed and measurable | A native list better expresses uncertain order |
| Checklist masquerading as Proof | Reuses task completion controls | Easy evidence scanning | Proof substantiates truth; it is not remaining work | Trust becomes compliance | Evidence is a disclosure or history list |
| Giant-title document | Gives immediate hierarchy | Looks calm and editorial | Title carries identity while the pursuit has no anatomy | Copy burden and dead space | Native navigation title plus content identity suffices |
| Repeated inset information cards | Makes grouping mechanically easy | Consistent and shippable | Every concept receives equal object weight | Dashboard/card-stack drift | Grouped content can remain on the open plane |
| Repeated colored edge rules | Adds inexpensive brand accent | Visually coherent | Color substitutes for structure and recurs without semantic change | Template feel and color dependence | A separator or spacing change communicates grouping |
| Ontology inspector | Makes relationships exact | Looks rigorous | Exposes internal nouns, owners, and edges instead of lived consequence | Database/architecture language reaches the person | A native detail list is needed for secondary technical evidence |
| Relationship graph | Shows many connections | Visually impressive | Imposes graph literacy and visual clutter | Goals becomes a knowledge tool | The person’s task is truly network exploration |
| Architecture terminology | Preserves implementation precision | Seems trustworthy | Explains system mechanics instead of personal reality | Cognitive burden and loss of warmth | Terminology is confined to diagnostics or evidence |
| Four-equal-button jump bar | Provides fast section access | Symmetric and compact | Gives every Path phase equal commitment and priority | Generic toolbar and false fixed stages | A segmented control represents actual mutually exclusive views |
| Repeated state labels | Makes semantics explicit | Easy accessibility fix | Labels carry all meaning while geometry says nothing | Copy-heavy prototype appearance | Labels supplement, not replace, spatial meaning |
| Decorative node bullets | Adds continuity motif | Small, ownable detail | Nodes become ornaments rather than states | Faux system and color dependence | Bullets are simply list markers |
| Dashboard lens | Puts related information in one place | Efficient glanceability | The Goal becomes a collection of modules and metrics | Productivity template drift | Multiple independent metrics genuinely require comparison |
| Progress percentage | Quantifies completion | Clear and motivating | Persistent pursuits are not linearly divisible | False precision and optimization pressure | Work is a bounded transfer with measurable completion |
| Completion ring | Makes progress glanceable | Familiar Apple-adjacent visual | Imports fitness/habit compliance grammar | Gamification and category confusion | A system activity state has a real normalized measure |
| Category color system | Differentiates many objects | Scans quickly | Suggests Life Area rank/category meaning through hue | Decorative taxonomy and accessibility burden | Color is redundant and user-authored |
| “AI-generated plan” presentation | Signals automation and novelty | Feels helpful and modern | Weakens person-owned truth and exposes provisional output as plan | Assistant/productivity-tool drift | AI output is explicitly provisional and reviewable |
| Quest or game journey | Sustains motivation | Feels alive and rewarding | Substitutes rewards for personal meaning | Infantilization, streak anxiety, external motivation | The product is explicitly a game |
| Card accordion | Combines disclosure with modules | Easy responsive implementation | Selected Life Area becomes another expanding tile | Generic settings/dashboard feel | A simple settings hierarchy is the actual product |
| Static infographic posing as interaction | Communicates an idea quickly | Visually distinctive in screenshots | Spatial symbols have no operational or navigational truth | Memorability without usability | The content is intentionally noninteractive explanatory media |

## Classification of the rejected spike

### Goal Path — `REPLACE`

The horizontal node line, clipped right edge, current/next labels, four-equal-button jump bar, selected-detail inset, and Proof checklist jointly match:

- generic project stepper;
- horizontal roadmap;
- checklist masquerading as Proof;
- four-equal-button jump bar;
- decorative node bullets;
- repeated state labels;
- repeated inset information cards; and
- static infographic posing as interaction.

The semantic order, identities, current/next/future distinctions, Proof lineage, and return behavior are preserved. The entire spatial model is replaced.

### Focused Goal — `REPLACE`

The giant title, sequential labels, explanatory paragraphs, violet edge panel, and recorded-moments checklist match:

- giant-title document;
- checklist masquerading as Proof;
- repeated inset information cards;
- repeated colored edge rules;
- repeated state labels; and
- dashboard lens in miniature.

The Goal identity, Life Area ownership, current truth, active thread, consequence, and routes are preserved. Their presentation is replaced.

### Consequential relationship — `REPLACE`

The two object labels connected by a line, “What connects them,” owner declaration, and explanatory body match:

- ontology inspector;
- relationship graph;
- architecture terminology;
- repeated colored edge rules;
- repeated state labels; and
- static infographic posing as interaction.

Both object identities, owner lineages, relationship identity, decision ownership, and non-mutating behavior are preserved. The connector/inspector model is removed.

## Boundary conclusion

The native default is preferable whenever custom geometry cannot express a stable product truth, improve orientation, or preserve context. Novelty without a semantic interaction contract is not an Ambitions signature.
