# Six Structural Directions

Exactly six text directions follow. They are hypotheses for selection and later rendering, not implementations or preferences.

## AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D01-R00 — Native Pursuit Ledger

- **Research lineage:** Things’ calm hierarchy, Bear/iA focus isolation, Apple Health’s change-first depth, Reminders’ adaptive disclosure, and standard Apple navigation.
- **Thesis:** A Goal becomes world-class by making native hierarchy exceptionally exact: one persistent identity margin, one accepted truth, and a progressively disclosed ledger of meaningful movement—not by inventing a custom diagram.
- **User outcome:** A person can orient, inspect the current pursuit, see what supports it, and return without learning a new interaction model.
- **Spatial model:** A vertically flowing native ledger organized around a persistent leading identity margin. The margin is structural whitespace and alignment—not a colored rule—and holds the selected Goal's owner, current-state marker, and exact return position while semantic chapters open beside it.
- **Primary canonical object:** The selected Goal, anchored by the identity margin as its title contracts into a compact native scroll-edge identity. Removing section copy still leaves one Goal-shaped origin and one current-state position.
- **Life Area relationship:** The Life Area is the enclosing section and native return context; expanding one Area increases its rhythm and detail while peers compress.
- **Goal identity anatomy:** Name and Life Area origin occupy the identity margin; one current-truth line crosses from that margin into the reading field. No hero title, full perimeter, decorative edge rule, or repeated state icon.
- **Linked Goal Lens anatomy:** An inline “why this matters now” chapter that unfolds from the Goal’s current truth and reveals one consequential relationship.
- **Goal Path model:** A sequence of semantic chapters—`Established`, `Current`, `Near movement`, `Open questions`, `Held for later`—with no connecting line or equal milestone geometry.
- **Past / current / next / future expression:** Past compresses into recorded truths; current is open and dominant; next is one concrete movement; future is a disclosure grouped by confidence rather than order alone.
- **Proof expression:** Compact provenance immediately under the accepted truth, expandable into exact recorded moments.
- **Uncertainty and Future Step expression:** “Not decided yet” and “possible later” groups are visually open and textually explicit, never faded into ambiguity.
- **Consequential relationship expression:** A native relationship row begins with the practical effect, then reveals the other Goal and owner on selection.
- **First-viewport composition:** Life Area origin, selected Goal identity margin/current truth, attached Lens summary, and one next movement; other Goals remain compact native rows aligned outside the selected margin.
- **Focused Goal composition:** Compact identity band, decisive current truth, current thread, one consequence, Path chapter preview, Proof disclosure, and native navigation actions.
- **Selected-detail behavior:** Tapping a Path chapter expands it into the reading field while the identity margin and current position remain fixed; deeper evidence uses native navigation and returns to that exact chapter.
- **Navigation and return:** NavigationStack with stable typed destinations and system back restoring Area expansion, Goal selection, and scroll anchor.
- **Density strategy:** Nonselected Areas and Goals compress to identity plus one state; dense roots use lazy native sections; no tile grid.
- **Material strategy:** Matte plane; separators and grouping carry most structure; standard material only for transient controls.
- **Typography character:** Editorially restrained system hierarchy with medium-weight Goal identity and highly readable state text.
- **Interaction model:** Tap-first selection, disclosure, navigation, context menus only for source-supported secondary actions.
- **Motion character:** System navigation and small inline disclosure transitions; the title contracts into the native scroll edge while the identity margin preserves origin. Identity never morphs theatrically.
- **Accessibility transformation:** The visual composition is already close to its semantic list equivalent; large type opens every chapter vertically and preserves headings/actions.
- **Light character:** Mineral background, neutral separators, restrained violet selection marker.
- **Dark character:** Deep graphite plane with high-contrast text and opaque content grouping.
- **Strongest quality:** Highest native familiarity, feasibility, accessibility, and density resilience, with a restrained Goal-specific identity behavior.
- **Principal risk:** The identity margin may still read as editorial layout rather than a living pursuit if current state and return behavior are not decisive.
- **Native SwiftUI feasibility:** High using NavigationStack, Section, DisclosureGroup-like authored compositions, lazy stacks, and stable focus IDs.
- **Market resemblance risk:** Medium-high resemblance to Things, Bear, and Apple system lists if copied superficially.
- **Why it is not a task manager:** It contains accepted truth, consequence, uncertainty, and Proof rather than checkable work inventory.
- **Why it is not a project manager:** Future movement is grouped by knowledge and relevance, not schedule, status, assignee, or completion.
- **Why it is unmistakably Ambitions:** The persistent identity margin binds Life Area ownership, accepted truth, open future, and truthful return into one Goal-specific structure rather than relying on headings.
- **Architecture-sensitive assumptions:** Existing fixtures can supply chapter classification without views inferring it.
- **What must be tested in a native prototype:** Whether the identity margin remains recognizable without copy, whether the first viewport feels alive rather than textual, whether inline Lens depth is intrinsic, and whether chapter expansion stays calm under dense data.

## AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D02-R00 — Open Horizon Passage

- **Research lineage:** Tide Guide’s coherent field and direct exploration, Weather’s forecast horizon, Gentler Streak’s history-relative context, and Flighty’s state continuity.
- **Thesis:** A pursuit is a passage from supported reality into decreasing certainty; the present is a cross-viewport horizon rather than a node in a stepper.
- **User outcome:** A person immediately perceives what is behind them, what is true now, what comes next, and where the future remains open.
- **Spatial model:** A vertical or gently diagonal field divided by one present horizon: support/history below or behind, current truth on the seam, and possibilities opening beyond it.
- **Primary canonical object:** The Goal is the stable horizon anchor; its identity sits at the origin of the field rather than above a document.
- **Life Area relationship:** The Life Area supplies the field’s origin plane and remains visible as a quiet boundary label through depth.
- **Goal identity anatomy:** Compact anchored title, owner origin, current position, and one material consequence aligned to the horizon.
- **Linked Goal Lens anatomy:** Activating the Lens changes the field’s visible depth: a supporting or constraining Goal appears as an adjacent horizon layer, not a card.
- **Goal Path model:** Past support occupies compressed strata; current truth spans the main horizon; the next movement is one near landmark; Future Steps occupy successively more open regions.
- **Past / current / next / future expression:** Past is stable and denser; current is crisp; next is singular and reachable; future uses wider spacing and explicit certainty labels rather than equally spaced milestones.
- **Proof expression:** Proof is embedded in past strata and can be selected where it supports the current horizon.
- **Uncertainty and Future Step expression:** An open boundary changes shape and labels when the route is unknown; possible alternatives coexist without becoming branches of a graph.
- **Consequential relationship expression:** The second Goal bends, narrows, or protects part of the current horizon; the practical consequence appears at the shared boundary.
- **First-viewport composition:** Selected Life Area origin, Goal anchor, current horizon, current truth, one near movement, and an edge glimpse of open future.
- **Focused Goal composition:** The same horizon expands to full width; current truth and consequence stay on the seam while Proof and near movement occupy opposing semantic regions.
- **Selected-detail behavior:** Tap a landmark to lift its exact detail from the field in place; dismiss returns it to the same position.
- **Navigation and return:** Native navigation preserves the selected horizon location; back restores exact field offset and selection.
- **Density strategy:** Only one Goal owns the expanded field; other Goals reduce to horizon summaries. Very dense future states group into explicit regions, not more landmarks.
- **Material strategy:** Matte plane with extremely restrained tonal difference across semantic regions; functional glass only for transient navigation/action chrome.
- **Typography character:** Wide breathing room, decisive state text on the horizon, compact owner and certainty language.
- **Interaction model:** Tap to focus, drag only where source-supported history inspection has meaningful discrete positions, native scroll for depth.
- **Motion character:** The horizon maintains position while surrounding regions expand or contract; Reduce Motion uses stable cuts and focus changes.
- **Accessibility transformation:** Ordered sections—Goal, current truth, supporting past, next movement, possible future—with explicit certainty and adjustable actions only if direct manipulation exists.
- **Light character:** Mineral plane with temperature and separators differentiating known/current/open regions.
- **Dark character:** Deep graphite with clear seams and no glow; openness comes from space, not opacity loss.
- **Strongest quality:** Most immediate qualitative continuity and strongest replacement for the stepper.
- **Principal risk:** Could imply a continuous measurable axis or become a static infographic.
- **Native SwiftUI feasibility:** Medium-high with adaptive stacks, Layout, scroll position restoration, and matched identity; no custom drawing should carry accessibility alone.
- **Market resemblance risk:** High if it resembles Tide Guide or Gentler Streak charts.
- **Why it is not a task manager:** It shows state, support, uncertainty, and possibility rather than executable rows.
- **Why it is not a project manager:** Distance represents epistemic certainty, never schedule or percent complete.
- **Why it is unmistakably Ambitions:** The horizon expresses a uniquely Ambitions distinction between accepted truth and still-open future movement.
- **Architecture-sensitive assumptions:** Fixtures can explicitly classify evidence, near movement, and uncertain future without inventing spatial quantities.
- **What must be tested in a native prototype:** Whether the horizon communicates without onboarding, whether detail emergence feels interactive, and whether the semantic equivalent is truly complete.

## AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D03-R00 — Consequence Weave

- **Research lineage:** Apple Photos’ relationship-by-grouping, Craft/Bear contextual backlinks, Journal’s event grouping, and Flighty’s cause-and-impact clarity.
- **Thesis:** A living pursuit is understood through the few relationships that materially support, protect, or constrain it; these relationships weave around one Goal without becoming a graph.
- **User outcome:** A person understands not only what the Goal is, but how it belongs in their life and what another pursuit changes about it.
- **Spatial model:** One primary vertical pursuit band intersected by a small number of horizontal consequence bands, each appearing only where it affects current truth.
- **Primary canonical object:** The selected Goal is the uninterrupted primary band; relationships are subordinate crossings, never peer nodes.
- **Life Area relationship:** The Life Area is the persistent heading/origin of the primary band and determines its return context, not its color.
- **Goal identity anatomy:** Goal identity and accepted truth sit on the primary band; current thread and next movement follow its reading direction.
- **Linked Goal Lens anatomy:** The Lens temporarily reveals one crossing band labeled first by consequence—supports, protects, constrains—then by the related Goal and Life Area.
- **Goal Path model:** The primary pursuit band has its own qualitative grammar independent of relationships: recorded support compresses behind a current-position seam, one next movement occupies the near continuation, and an explicitly unjoined future remains open. Relationship bands may touch only the current or next region where they have a material consequence.
- **Past / current / next / future expression:** Past support compresses into provenance, current truth has a stable seam, next is a single continuation, and future stays visibly unjoined. Band width, position, and length never encode quantity, dependency, duration, or flow.
- **Proof expression:** Proof is a small stitched provenance seam where recorded evidence joins accepted truth, with full detail disclosed on selection.
- **Uncertainty and Future Step expression:** The primary band remains intentionally unjoined beyond the next movement, labeled as open rather than decorated with placeholder nodes. A relationship cannot bridge or resolve that uncertainty unless fixture truth explicitly says it does.
- **Consequential relationship expression:** This is the core: a crossing makes the practical consequence spatially primary, with object metadata secondary.
- **First-viewport composition:** One expanded Life Area, one selected Goal band, current truth, one material crossing, and one next movement; other Goals remain compact identity bands.
- **Focused Goal composition:** The primary band occupies the viewport; current thread, Proof seam, and one relationship crossing create structure without section cards.
- **Selected-detail behavior:** Selecting a crossing widens it enough to reveal consequence and the related Goal; deeper inspection navigates natively and returns to that crossing.
- **Navigation and return:** System navigation maintains the primary Goal as the return anchor; relationship depth never makes the secondary Goal appear to own the route.
- **Density strategy:** Show one material relationship at a time in the Lens; additional relationships live in progressive disclosure sorted by consequence, not graph degree.
- **Material strategy:** Matte open plane; narrow opaque tonal bands only where states intersect; no glow, threads, or literal textile decoration.
- **Typography character:** Strong identity line, concise consequence statements at crossings, quiet owner metadata.
- **Interaction model:** Tap to reveal a crossing; swipe or picker may change the single inspected relationship if native and accessible.
- **Motion character:** A relationship band enters from its owning side and settles at the consequence; Reduce Motion inserts it without translation.
- **Accessibility transformation:** Goal reading group first, then ordered relationship groups phrased by effect; no reliance on crossing position.
- **Light character:** Mineral layers separated by line weight and spacing, with minimal semantic tint.
- **Dark character:** Graphite plane with restrained opaque band differences and high-contrast junction labels.
- **Strongest quality:** Best non-graph model for consequential relationships and Life Area context.
- **Principal risk:** Band crossings can still look like a graph, Sankey diagram, or decorative weave; the prototype must reject any encoding of quantity, dependency, duration, or flow.
- **Native SwiftUI feasibility:** Medium using adaptive layout and aligned regions; interaction must avoid Canvas-only semantics.
- **Market resemblance risk:** Medium; less direct product resemblance, but high information-visualization risk.
- **Why it is not a task manager:** The primary structure is consequence and ownership, not action inventory.
- **Why it is not a project manager:** Relationships do not encode dependencies, critical paths, status, or schedules.
- **Why it is unmistakably Ambitions:** It makes “what this pursuit protects or affects in a life” the visual signature rather than progress mechanics.
- **Architecture-sensitive assumptions:** Canon and fixtures can identify one material relationship and its precise consequence without view inference.
- **What must be tested in a native prototype:** Whether the weave reads as lived consequence rather than a diagram, how many crossings density permits, and whether return ownership remains obvious.

## AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D04-R00 — Pursuit Aperture

- **Research lineage:** iA Writer’s focused aperture, Apple Journal’s moment-to-object transition, Photos’ identity collections, Moonlitt’s coherent primary object, and Apple’s hierarchy guidance.
- **Thesis:** The Goal is a persistent aperture into a changing personal reality; expanding it increases the resolution of the same object instead of opening a document.
- **User outcome:** A person feels that one Goal is a stable living object whose present truth, support, and possible movement can be inspected at different depths.
- **Spatial model:** A bounded but not card-like aperture—created by partial edges, alignment, and controlled clipping—through which the Goal’s semantic layers appear.
- **Primary canonical object:** The aperture itself represents the Goal’s stable identity across root, Lens, focused depth, and Path.
- **Life Area relationship:** The aperture is cut from the Life Area plane; its origin edge and return behavior preserve ownership.
- **Goal identity anatomy:** A compact identity lockup on the aperture edge, accepted truth centered in the opening, and consequence/next movement positioned beyond the current boundary.
- **Linked Goal Lens anatomy:** The Lens changes what layer is visible through the aperture—relationship, Path, or Proof—without adding an inset module.
- **Goal Path model:** Depth planes rather than milestones: recorded foundation, accepted current plane, near movement, uncertain outer plane, recovery/closure overlay.
- **Past / current / next / future expression:** Past supports from behind; current occupies the clear plane; next sits just beyond the aperture; future remains partially occluded and explicitly provisional.
- **Proof expression:** Proof appears as the foundation visible at the aperture’s edge and opens into exact provenance.
- **Uncertainty and Future Step expression:** The outer opening changes from defined to unresolved geometry while labels state what is known and not decided.
- **Consequential relationship expression:** A related Goal shifts the visible boundary or protected area of the aperture; selecting it reveals the exact consequence and identity.
- **First-viewport composition:** Expanded Life Area plane with one Goal aperture, visible current truth, one consequence, one near movement, and a restrained Lens affordance.
- **Focused Goal composition:** The aperture enlarges rather than the title; current truth, active thread, Proof foundation, and open future occupy distinct depth planes.
- **Selected-detail behavior:** Tapping a plane brings it to the reading surface while the aperture edge and Goal identity remain fixed.
- **Navigation and return:** Native depth maintains the aperture’s identity; back contracts to the selected root aperture and restores focus.
- **Density strategy:** Only the selected Goal has an open aperture; other Goals show closed identity notches with one state line. Dense data becomes a list within the active plane.
- **Material strategy:** Opaque matte planes with partial geometry and tonal elevation; glass limited to transient controls.
- **Typography character:** Object identity is compact and stable; state truth can be larger but never functions as a hero title.
- **Interaction model:** Tap planes or use a native picker/toolbar to select Path, Proof, or relationship lens; scrolling deepens content naturally.
- **Motion character:** Depth change is low-amplitude scale/position continuity; Reduce Motion swaps planes with focus-preserving crossfade.
- **Accessibility transformation:** The aperture becomes an ordered object group with named layers and direct actions; no meaning relies on occlusion.
- **Light character:** Mineral paper-like depth without page or card metaphor; subtle temperature separates layers.
- **Dark character:** Graphite depth through opaque tonal planes and clean partial edges, never glow.
- **Strongest quality:** Strongest persistent-object anatomy and a potentially ownable Ambitions signature.
- **Principal risk:** Could become a sculpted sci-fi container, carousel, or inaccessible z-stack.
- **Native SwiftUI feasibility:** Medium using custom Layout, matched geometry sparingly, safe clipping, and explicit accessibility representations.
- **Market resemblance risk:** Low-to-medium if kept abstract and semantic; high if given celestial or skeuomorphic styling.
- **Why it is not a task manager:** The aperture reveals truth layers and relationships, not work items.
- **Why it is not a project manager:** Depth represents semantic resolution, not stages, dependencies, or execution status.
- **Why it is unmistakably Ambitions:** Accepted truth is literally the clear view through a personally owned canonical object, with possibility kept visibly open.
- **Architecture-sensitive assumptions:** Snapshot models can define layer roles and selection without views deriving semantics from z-order.
- **What must be tested in a native prototype:** Whether partial geometry reads as one object rather than a card, how it scales at large type, and whether plane selection remains obvious and native.

## AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D05-R00 — Evolving Passage

- **Research lineage:** Day One’s longitudinal entry corpus, Calendar’s density transforms, Gentler Streak’s recent-history context, Flighty’s phase continuity, and Photos Memories’ narrative grouping.
- **Thesis:** A pursuit is an evolving passage composed of meaningful eras, not a plan; each era gathers accepted truth and Proof while the current era opens toward an uncertain next.
- **User outcome:** A person can understand how a Goal has changed over time and what matters now without reading a history log or project roadmap.
- **Spatial model:** A series of vertically stacked, variable-depth eras with the current era occupying the main viewport and adjacent eras peeking as context.
- **Primary canonical object:** The Goal is the passage owner; eras are states of that object, never separate projects.
- **Life Area relationship:** The Life Area is the persistent passage origin and native return context; eras do not detach from it.
- **Goal identity anatomy:** Compact persistent identity, current era name or truth, and one decisive transition statement.
- **Linked Goal Lens anatomy:** The Lens reveals how another Goal influenced the transition into the current era, attached to that transition rather than displayed globally.
- **Goal Path model:** Qualitative eras such as `Foundation`, `Making room`, `Ready for arrival`, with one current era and a noncommittal next opening; names come only from source-backed fixture truth.
- **Past / current / next / future expression:** Past eras compress but remain inspectable; current expands; next is a possible transition; distant future is omitted until it has meaning.
- **Proof expression:** Proof is embedded as the recorded basis for each era transition, not checkboxes within the era.
- **Uncertainty and Future Step expression:** Future appears as an unnamed or provisionally named opening with explicit unknowns; it is not rendered as a promised era.
- **Consequential relationship expression:** A related Goal appears at the transition it affected, with the lived consequence first.
- **First-viewport composition:** Life Area origin, selected Goal identity, current era/truth, one relationship or protection, one near transition, and compressed prior continuity.
- **Focused Goal composition:** Current era dominates while prior support and next opening remain spatially visible; exact history and Proof disclose naturally.
- **Selected-detail behavior:** Selecting an era expands its accepted truth and Proof in place; navigating deeper returns to that era.
- **Navigation and return:** Native navigation preserves selected era and scroll position; root restoration returns to selected Goal in its Life Area.
- **Density strategy:** Older eras collapse into a summarized history line; very dense history uses a native chronological list after deliberate navigation, not on the primary field.
- **Material strategy:** Open plane with restrained era boundaries created by rhythm and one transition seam; no timeline cards.
- **Typography character:** Era truth is the main landmark; dates and evidence are tertiary; Goal identity remains stable and compact.
- **Interaction model:** Natural vertical scroll, tap to open an era, native history navigation, no horizontal road map or scrub without exact temporal meaning.
- **Motion character:** Current era settles into place while prior context compresses; Reduce Motion uses scroll/focus restoration only.
- **Accessibility transformation:** Ordered era headings with current state trait, evidence disclosures, and explicit possible-future language.
- **Light character:** Mineral-neutral field with subtle tonal shifts between eras, never scrapbook or journal paper.
- **Dark character:** Deep graphite with firm transition seams and readable historical text.
- **Strongest quality:** Best longitudinal emotional continuity and recovery/closure potential.
- **Principal risk:** Could become a journal, life timeline, or renamed project phases.
- **Native SwiftUI feasibility:** High-medium using lazy vertical composition, scroll targets, sections, and stateful expansion.
- **Market resemblance risk:** Medium resemblance to Day One or narrative timelines.
- **Why it is not a task manager:** Eras are accepted states of a pursuit, not work containers.
- **Why it is not a project manager:** Transitions are discovered from lived truth and Proof, not planned stage gates.
- **Why it is unmistakably Ambitions:** It treats personal progression as changes in accepted reality with explicit uncertainty and consequence.
- **Architecture-sensitive assumptions:** Fixture/canon can support meaningful state eras; the UI may not invent narrative labels from chronological activity.
- **What must be tested in a native prototype:** Whether eras feel like lived progression rather than a timeline, whether the current era is decisive, and how sparse Goals avoid emptiness.

## AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D06-R00 — Living Fold

- **Research lineage:** Freeform scenes’ preserved viewpoints, Craft’s attached depth, Photos’ identity grouping, Play’s direct native manipulation, and Apple’s adaptive hierarchy/material guidance.
- **Thesis:** A Life Area folds open to reveal one Goal; the same fold then reveals current truth, Path, Proof, or relationship consequence while preserving a visible origin and exact return.
- **User outcome:** A person understands depth as opening more of the same pursuit, not navigating among screens or modules.
- **Spatial model:** One directional fold or hinge created through alignment and partial plane transformation—not literal paper—that successively exposes semantic depth.
- **Primary canonical object:** The selected Goal lives on the revealed surface and stays attached to the Life Area hinge.
- **Life Area relationship:** The Life Area is the stable outer plane; only one Area can open a Goal fold at a time, without implying importance.
- **Goal identity anatomy:** Goal name and identity marker live at the hinge; current truth occupies the revealed surface; consequence and action align with the opening direction.
- **Linked Goal Lens anatomy:** A secondary fold reveals the consequence shared with another Goal while keeping the primary hinge and owner visible.
- **Goal Path model:** Path is generated by successive semantic folds: accepted foundation, current living surface, next revealed edge, and unopened future—not a row of nodes.
- **Past / current / next / future expression:** Past remains beneath the current surface as inspectable support; current is fully open; next appears at the exposed edge; future remains closed and explicitly unknown.
- **Proof expression:** Proof is visible at the underside/support of the current fold and opens as exact provenance.
- **Uncertainty and Future Step expression:** An unopened edge can be labeled with what is unresolved; opening is only available when fixture truth supports a destination.
- **Consequential relationship expression:** The secondary Goal does not become a peer node; its effect appears on a fold that shares the relevant boundary.
- **First-viewport composition:** Several Life Area identities on the open plane; one selected Area unfolds into a Goal identity/current truth and a glimpse of the attached Lens.
- **Focused Goal composition:** The Goal fold occupies most of the viewport, with hinge identity, current truth, active thread, next edge, and Proof support sharing one geometry.
- **Selected-detail behavior:** Selecting a semantic edge opens that fold; closing restores the exact preceding surface and focus target.
- **Navigation and return:** Native navigation remains authoritative; fold continuity supplements, never replaces, system back and typed restoration.
- **Density strategy:** Only the active fold is articulated; dense children become a native vertical list on the revealed surface. Multiple folds never fan out simultaneously.
- **Material strategy:** Opaque matte planes and partial edges; restrained functional glass for controls; no shadows or perspective that imitate paper craft.
- **Typography character:** Identity at the hinge, state truth on the surface, metadata at the edge; three roles maximum in compact form.
- **Interaction model:** Tap-first native disclosure is the complete interaction. No drag is required. A native button or row opens each authorized layer, and system back remains the only depth-exit contract.
- **Motion character:** A short native content transition suggests directional reveal while preserving origin; no perspective or custom gesture is required. Reduce Motion uses instantaneous plane replacement with hinge identity and focus unchanged.
- **Accessibility transformation:** The fold becomes an ordered hierarchical passage—Life Area, Goal, selected layer—with expand/collapse semantics and native back.
- **Light character:** Mineral planes with edge definition from spacing and tone, avoiding paper simulation.
- **Dark character:** Graphite planes with clean occlusion boundaries, no glow or 3D spectacle.
- **Strongest quality:** Most restrained but ownable cross-state interaction signature.
- **Principal risk:** Could become a gimmicky accordion, card peel, or inaccessible custom navigation mechanism.
- **Native SwiftUI feasibility:** Medium-high using explicit tap destinations, adaptive stacks, native navigation, and a modest content transition. The fold is a composition metaphor, not custom navigation or gesture infrastructure.
- **Market resemblance risk:** Low if it avoids Craft cards and physical-paper metaphor.
- **Why it is not a task manager:** Opening reveals semantic truth and relationship depth, not a nested task hierarchy.
- **Why it is not a project manager:** The fold expresses inspection and epistemic openness, not work breakdown or stage progression.
- **Why it is unmistakably Ambitions:** The person opens a persistent pursuit from its Life Area and carries its truth through every depth without losing ownership.
- **Architecture-sensitive assumptions:** Typed routes map each authorized fold to an existing semantic depth; the view never infers new routes or commands, and no drag state becomes route ownership.
- **What must be tested in a native prototype:** Whether a tap-first fold feels inevitable rather than novel, whether root density remains calm, whether large type preserves the hinge, and whether system navigation remains unmistakably primary.

## Direction integrity statement

- D01 is native-led and hierarchical.
- D02 is spatial and horizon-led.
- D03 is relational and consequence-led.
- D04 is object-centric and depth-led.
- D05 is longitudinal and era-led.
- D06 is Ambitions-specific and interaction-led.

None is a color, typography, card, or node variant of another. No winner is identified.
