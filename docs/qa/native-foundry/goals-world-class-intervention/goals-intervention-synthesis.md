# Goals Intervention Synthesis

## What makes a persistent pursuit visually different from a project?

A project is conventionally defined by a planned sequence and finish condition. A persistent pursuit is defined by identity, accepted truth, evolving direction, consequence, and the ability to recover when the route changes. Its visual model must therefore privilege the canonical Goal and current relationship to it—not a list of remaining work. The past provides trust, the next movement provides orientation, and the future remains intentionally less determined.

Things and Reminders show why project syntax is effective but dangerous. Gentler Streak shows how history can contextualize a present without a completion percentage, while Flighty shows the power of stable identity across changing states. Ambitions needs the stability of the latter without pretending a Goal has flight phases or measurable exertion.

## Strongest non-stepper progression models

1. **Current-position seam:** A qualitative boundary between accepted past/current reality and unresolved future.
2. **Future-state horizon:** Future Steps lose specificity with distance rather than lining up as equally committed nodes.
3. **History-relative field:** Past evidence establishes a band or texture of continuity without scoring the person.
4. **Identity-preserving passage:** The Goal stays fixed while contextual layers move through present, next, and possible future.
5. **Selected-detail emergence:** Detail grows from a selected location instead of appearing in a detached card.

Tide Guide and Weather demonstrate spatial continuity; Gentler Streak demonstrates history-relative context; Calendar demonstrates density transformation. None should be copied literally because their axes are physically or chronologically precise. Goals requires qualitative semantic distance.

## Strongest non-graph relationship models

1. **Consequence adjacency:** Place the affected pursuit close to the consequence it creates or protects.
2. **Shared owner depth:** Reveal how two Goals occupy different Life Areas while sharing one practical boundary.
3. **Supporting layer:** Let one Goal appear as a quiet supporting or constraining plane behind the selected Goal.
4. **Contextual lens:** The relationship changes what can be perceived about the Goal, then recedes on return.
5. **Narrative grouping:** Use proximity and sequence, as Photos and Journal do, rather than connectors and edge labels.

The relationship should first answer “What changes for me?” Metadata can follow in disclosure.

## How Proof can exist without a checklist

Proof is evidence that an accepted truth is warranted. It should attach directly to that truth as provenance: a recorded moment, observation, Receipt, or history seam. A compact count or preview may show that support exists, but inspection reveals the exact evidence. Checkmarks are reserved for actual selection or completion actions; Proof itself should not impersonate completed work.

## How the Goal Path can show uncertainty and Future Steps

The Path should use increasing openness as knowledge decreases. The current state is exact, next movement is concrete, future steps are provisional, and unresolved territory is visibly uncommitted. This can be achieved through space, grouping, or boundary treatment, but never by fading required text below legibility. The semantic equivalent must explicitly distinguish `current`, `next`, `possible later`, `unknown`, `interrupted`, and `closed`.

## How a selected Goal remains attached to its Life Area

Life Area ownership must be more than a small metadata row. The owner can persist as the plane, origin edge, or return context from which the Goal emerges. The Goal’s identity should align to that owner in root and focused depth, and system back must restore the exact Life Area expansion and selected Goal.

## How the Linked Goal Lens feels intrinsic

The Lens must alter the perceptual frame around the selected Goal rather than arrive as an inset module. It might reveal a consequence layer, change spatial emphasis, or expose an attached relationship field. It remains read-only and clearly belongs to the Goal. Hiding it returns to the same Goal state rather than closing a dashboard widget.

## How one spatial grammar generates every depth

The chosen grammar needs three invariant elements:

- a persistent Goal anchor;
- a semantic boundary for current accepted truth; and
- a controlled way for context to emerge from or around that anchor.

Root uses the compact form. Focused depth increases semantic resolution. Path extends the current boundary into past and possible future. Relationship adds an adjacent consequence layer. Recovery bends or interrupts the boundary while preserving identity. Closure resolves the current state without deleting the pursuit’s history.

## What Goals can inherit from R14

- matte opaque semantic content plane;
- system typography;
- identity before state, chronology, metrics, and action;
- restrained semantic relief rather than generic cards;
- truth-layer distinctions;
- parent-owner lineage;
- concise material consequence;
- native action-role hierarchy;
- natural scrolling and progressive disclosure;
- minimum interaction targets;
- explicit recovery and truthful return;
- meaning independent of color, transparency, and motion; and
- accessibility recomposition rather than shrinking.

## What Goals must invent

- a persistent-pursuit anchor distinct from Today’s Start Here anatomy;
- a qualitative Path that is neither temporal rail nor project stepper;
- an intrinsic Linked Goal Lens;
- Proof-in-place;
- an uncertainty model for Future Steps;
- a non-graph consequential relationship; and
- a Goals-specific moment of visual authority.

## The unowned Ambitions opportunity

No benchmark product combines a personally owned canonical pursuit, qualitative current truth, provisional future movement, trustable Proof, consequential cross-pursuit relationships, and humane recovery in one native spatial system. The opportunity is not a prettier goal tracker. It is a **pursuit field whose shape reflects what is known, supported, protected, and still open**.

## Ten selected research principles

1. Anchor the canonical Goal before presenting progress.
2. Make current accepted truth the second landmark.
3. Express future distance as decreasing certainty, not milestone count.
4. Attach Proof to the truth it substantiates.
5. Express relationships through practical consequence before metadata.
6. Keep Life Area ownership spatially persistent through depth.
7. Let selected detail emerge from its context.
8. Transform density and accessibility composition instead of shrinking.
9. Reserve material and motion for functional hierarchy and state change.
10. Restore exact object and owner context after every inspection.

## Ten rejected market patterns

1. Project stepper.
2. Horizontal roadmap.
3. Completion percentage or ring.
4. Proof checklist.
5. Giant-title document.
6. Repeated inset cards and colored edge rules.
7. Ontology inspector or relationship graph.
8. Dashboard lens.
9. Quest, streak, score, or reward journey.
10. Static spatial infographic without operational behavior.

## Five unresolved design tensions

1. **Stability vs vitality:** How much can the Goal’s spatial field change while identity remains unmistakable?
2. **Uncertainty vs clarity:** How can Future Steps feel open without seeming vague or unfinished?
3. **Proof visibility vs calm:** How much evidence belongs in the first viewport before trust turns into documentation?
4. **Relationship depth vs graph drift:** How can multiple consequences remain inspectable without introducing node-edge grammar?
5. **Ownability vs native default:** How distinctive can interaction become before accessibility, discoverability, or platform coherence suffers?

## Three candidate ownable Ambitions signatures

These are candidates, not selections.

1. **Pursuit Aperture:** The selected Goal opens a bounded view through current truth into what remains possible; uncertainty changes the aperture rather than adding status labels.
2. **Living Fold:** Life Area ownership folds open into a Goal, and subsequent folds reveal Path, Proof, and relationship depth while retaining the same origin.
3. **Continuity Field:** Accepted truth forms a stable seam while past support, current movement, and future possibility occupy different semantic regions around it.
