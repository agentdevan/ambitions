# World-Class Goals Bar

A 5/5 Goals direction is not merely polished. It must establish a coherent product model that remains recognizable across root, Lens, focused depth, Path, relationship, recovery, and closure.

## Required 5/5 qualities

1. **Ownable identity:** Recognizable as Ambitions without wordmark, violet, or fixture copy.
2. **Living pursuit:** One Goal feels persistent, changing, and personally owned—not like a deliverable.
3. **Identity without hero dependence:** The Goal remains recognizable without a huge repeated title.
4. **Qualitative continuity:** Past, present, next, possible future, uncertainty, Proof, and recovery are understandable without a conventional stepper.
5. **Consequential relationship:** Related Goals are understood through support, protection, constraint, or effect—not graph or database anatomy.
6. **Pre-reading comprehension:** Most of the first viewport’s hierarchy is apparent before every sentence is read.
7. **Copy in support:** Copy clarifies spatial meaning rather than carrying the design.
8. **Calm authority:** The experience is restrained without being timid, empty, or generic.
9. **Decisive moment:** At least one state transition or selection has unmistakable visual authority.
10. **Native feasibility:** The structure is feasible with adaptive SwiftUI, system navigation, natural scrolling, and standard accessibility semantics.
11. **Density resilience:** Typical, dense, very dense, and Accessibility Dynamic Type states recompose without losing meaning.
12. **Independent semantics:** Identity, truth, consequence, action, and position survive without color, transparency, or motion.
13. **Category avoidance:** The structure does not resemble project management, task tracking, dashboards, roadmaps, graphs, or journal documents.
14. **One system through depth:** Focused detail emerges from the same spatial grammar as the root rather than switching to a Form.
15. **Transferable signature:** The direction has a distinctive Goals signature that can recur across Goals states without being copied into Today or Time.

## Five-point assessment scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Distinctive, coherent, native, accessible, semantically exact, and robust across the full bounded journey |
| 4 | Strong and credible, with bounded questions suitable for native prototyping |
| 3 | Understandable but generic, overly derivative, structurally incomplete, or dependent on copy |
| 2 | Material product drift, accessibility fragility, or visual-model contradiction |
| 1 | Incompatible with Goals ownership or the Foundry boundary |

## Automatic rejection floor

A direction is rejected before rendering if it scores below 4/5 on any of:

- product identity;
- persistent-object anatomy;
- progression model;
- native feasibility;
- accessibility; or
- project-management avoidance.

## Review questions

### At the root

- Can one Life Area expand materially without looking ranked, tiled, or accordion-like?
- Is the selected Goal a persistent object rather than a selected row?
- Does the Linked Goal Lens appear intrinsic to that Goal?

### At focused depth

- Does current pursuit truth have a spatial role distinct from title and metadata?
- Can current thread, next movement, Proof, protection, and uncertainty be understood before reading a document?
- Does the Goal stay visibly attached to its Life Area?

### In the Path

- Is the present a meaningful boundary rather than a dot on a line?
- Does the future become less determined instead of repeating identical milestones?
- Does Proof substantiate the pursuit rather than look completed?
- Can interruption and recovery bend the same model without creating an error screen?

### In relationships

- Is practical consequence apparent before relationship metadata?
- Do both objects retain identity without nodes and edges?
- Does return restore the exact Goal context?

### Under accessibility transformation

- Is there an ordered semantic equivalent, not merely a scaled spatial layout?
- Are all actions at least 44 points and reachable?
- Do native heading, group, state, and adjustable-action semantics preserve the model?

No textual direction can satisfy this bar by assertion. The bar becomes a prototype gate only after owner selection.
