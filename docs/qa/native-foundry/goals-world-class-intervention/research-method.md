# Research Method

## Question

How can Ambitions make a persistent personal pursuit feel alive, spatially coherent, emotionally intelligent, operationally clear, and unmistakably its own—without becoming a project manager, task tracker, roadmap, graph, dashboard, or copy-heavy document?

## Method

The study used current public sources available on 2026-07-28. Primary sources were preferred in this order:

1. Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Apple developer material;
2. Apple Design Awards, 2024–2026;
3. official App Store pages;
4. official product sites and first-party help documentation; and
5. direct inspection of the committed native Goals spike.

Secondary commentary was not used as product fact. Dribbble, Behance, Pinterest, UI kits, theme marketplaces, AI concept feeds, unsourced listicles, and web dashboards in phone frames were excluded.

The corpus intentionally mixes products that are good references with products that expose important copying risks. A high score does not mean “copy this product.” It means a product is useful evidence for a particular structural problem. `Copying risk` is scored in the opposite direction from quality metrics: 5 means very high risk. Accessibility adaptability scores are researcher hypotheses about how well the observed structure could transform; they are not verified claims about each product's accessibility implementation.

## Apple platform constraints

Apple’s current design guidance establishes the platform floor:

- begin with purpose, maintain clear hierarchy, and make the experience understandable and adaptable ([Design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles));
- use layout, negative space, grouping, and adaptive hierarchy to show relationships ([Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout));
- keep Liquid Glass in the functional control/navigation layer rather than primary content ([Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials));
- minimize complexity and provide simple, intuitive, system-consistent interactions ([Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility)); and
- treat discovery, prototyping, validation, and iteration as a cycle rather than defending an early idea ([Explore the app design cycle](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/develop-in-swift/explore-the-app-design-cycle)).

The 2024–2026 Apple Design Awards were reviewed as evidence of the current quality bar: distinctive systems, strong interaction, clarity, accessibility, and practical value—not ornament alone ([2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/), [2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/), [2026](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)).

## Analysis procedure

Each product was evaluated only where materially relevant against:

- primary-object identity;
- spatial position and continuity;
- progression and uncertainty;
- relationship expression;
- first-viewport hierarchy and density;
- content/chrome/material separation;
- interaction and contextual navigation;
- functional motion;
- accessibility transformation;
- emotional character; and
- likely failure modes if borrowed by Ambitions.

Findings were synthesized into patterns before any direction was written. Directions were then checked for structural difference, project-management avoidance, accessibility, and native feasibility. No score was averaged into an automatic winner.

## Availability and substitution notes

- Play remains relevant as a 2025 Apple Design Award interaction benchmark. Because its current official product site was unavailable during final link validation, it is treated only as historical award-era interaction evidence, not a current availability claim.
- Apple Journal is retained as a first-party benchmark because it provides a current example of suggestion-to-object capture.
- Moonlitt and Primary: News in Depth supplement the initial list because the 2026 Apple Design Awards provide current primary-source evidence for distinctive visualization and content-first restraint.
- Finch is included to study persistent-avatar continuity and to expose the limits of game/quest grammar for Ambitions.

## Proof ceiling

This study proves that the six directions have traceable research lineages and credible native design hypotheses. It does not prove visual quality, interaction quality, SwiftUI performance, direct-device usability, production runtime fit, or owner acceptance.
