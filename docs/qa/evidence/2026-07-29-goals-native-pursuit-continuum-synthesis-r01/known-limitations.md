# Known limitations

- This is a three-screen Foundry synthesis, not production Goals
  implementation.
- The fixture is synthetic and non-mutating; persistence, replay, runtime
  ownership, migration, and production restoration are not proven.
- Peer Home Goals do not expose fabricated supporting routes.
- Goal Path and relationship destinations retain inherited bounded fixture
  presentations; they are interaction proof, not accepted deeper visual
  calibration.
- Exact Goal seam geometry, Life Area signatures, future presentation,
  relationship emphasis, motion, haptics, Light neutral, and shared APIs remain
  open.
- Physical-device accessibility, edge gestures, dock reach, haptics, and
  prolonged use remain unproven.
- Direct Xcode preview was unavailable under the active Ambitions scheme;
  final Simulator automation used direct `xcodebuild` and `simctl` tooling.
- The Crowned Edge Dock remains `PROVISIONAL_HIGH_RISK_HYPOTHESIS` and is not
  production-approved.
- Screenshots are evaluation evidence with `production_baseline = false`.
- `APPROVED FOR SWIFTUI` remains false.
