# Screen-by-screen review

## Goals root

The root is now a strict Life Area index. Home, Relationships, and Career carry
stable identity, area-level direction, differentiated neutral posture, and
row-wide native disclosure without leaking child Goal names. The compact crown
and provisional dock remain subordinate.

The native result is more operational than the generated R02 frame and removes
internal posture labels. Its Life Area signatures remain provisional; direct
device use and later cross-root synthesis must determine whether they are
distinctive enough without becoming category ornament.

## Home Life Area

Home is a separate native depth with compact scroll-edge navigation. All three
Goals use a related seam and truth anatomy. `Welcome our baby home` has greater
semantic resolution because the fixture supports Proof and current movement,
not because it receives an unrelated card or rank treatment. The entire selected
Goal passage is the native navigation target.

Peer Goals intentionally remain inspection-only in this bounded fixture.

## Focused Goal

Home ownership, stable Goal identity, accepted truth, Proof, current movement,
future posture, and relationship consequence share one open composition. The
accepted truth dominates; Goal Path is entered through the current movement
rather than a broad CTA. Proof and future states expand in place, while
relationship inspection uses native push navigation and truthful return.

The native screen resolves the generated frame's ambiguous hit regions and
demonstrates real selection feedback and scrolling. Exact future geometry,
relationship emphasis, motion, and haptics remain deliberately unfrozen.

## Cross-scale review

The three depths remain distinguishable at full-device and contact-sheet scale.
Light and Dark retain equivalent hierarchy. Accessibility Dynamic Type becomes
a natural scrolling composition rather than shrinking the Goal object.

Status: no blocking semantic, navigation, clipping, or Simulator-testable
accessibility defect remains in the bounded three-screen prototype.
