# Known limitations and proof ceiling

- Owner visual review is pending. A successful build and self-review are not
  visual approval.
- Screenshots and recordings are Simulator evaluation references, not production
  baselines or direct-device proof.
- The fixture-host state machine and pause durations are synthetic. They do not
  prove persistence, replay, database mutation, network behavior, or production
  latency.
- No runtime adapter or production mutation implementation exists in this
  branch.
- VoiceOver semantic order/actions were inspected through accessibility-backed
  UI queries; direct-device spoken output and focus restoration remain open.
- Switch Control, Full Keyboard Access, Voice Control, physical one-handed
  reach, system-edge competition, low-brightness Dark, and physical Reduce
  Transparency/Reduce Motion behavior remain direct-device obligations.
- Increased Contrast was exercised in Simulator. Differentiate Without Color is
  supported structurally by labels, symbols, values, and selection traits, but
  a physical assistive-input pass remains open.
- Long-content RTL is a fixture stress projection, not a translated locale.
- No editable input exists in this slice, so keyboard-present behavior is not
  applicable. Future editors require their own proof.
- A stale or rejected runtime settlement was not naturally supported by the
  authorized fixture contract and was deliberately not fabricated.
- No generic inverse action is present; exact inverse eligibility remains a
  production command-contract responsibility.
- Exact crown collapse thresholds, restoration animation, device-size safe
  areas, and native edge-gesture coexistence remain unfrozen/direct-device work.
- The deterministic recordings show semantic state walkthroughs, not a claim of
  production input, persistence, or assistive-focus timing.

`APPROVED FOR SWIFTUI` remains `false`. Broad reconstruction, runtime
integration, app-entry change, legacy replacement, and cutover remain
unauthorized.
