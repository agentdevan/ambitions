## Supporting state proof

- `TFCS-S01`: transitional saving keeps object identity and accepted truth while
  duplicate commitment is disabled.
- `TFCS-S02`: cancellation returns to the unchanged focused Step.
- `TFCS-S03`: interruption preserves the last-saved note without false success.
- `TFCS-S04`: dense Today retains protected/fixed reality without manufactured
  urgency or silent movement.
- `TFCS-S05`: long copy and RTL recompose within native scrolling.
- `TFCS-S06`: Increased Contrast preserves the current/proposed distinction.

Stale or rejected settlement is not naturally supported by this fixture and was
not fabricated. The interrupted projection is the supported local/offline
retained-truth evidence; it is not a live-runtime or persistence claim.

## Gate questions

- **Product identity:** the journey remains object-, relationship-, and
  consequence-led. It does not become a task list, agenda, dashboard, feed, or
  assistant.
- **Hierarchy:** Start Here dominates without a container card; identity
  precedes truth, fit, consequence, action, and supporting time.
- **Review:** current and proposed truth are named, symbolically distinct, and
  spatially separated. Cancel is explicitly non-mutating.
- **Settlement:** accepted truth is primary; evidence is compact; return is
  explicit; no score, reward, confetti, or automatic dismissal appears.
- **Return:** stable IDs preserve the settled object, reveal the next eligible
  Start Here, and avoid restoring stale hierarchy.
- **Recovery:** the interruption seam is object-scoped, saved progress is
  retained, choices are filtered, and dismissal preserves the Step.
- **Native behavior:** one native scroll owner, `NavigationStack`, native Back,
  native full-screen review, native recovery sheet, native progress, and native
  controls remain in use.

The inspected captures show no text or required control obscured by the crown,
dock, safe area, or another control. Content that extends beyond a viewport is
reachable through natural scrolling; the recordings and UI tests exercise that
continuation. Physical-device safe areas, gestures, focus, and reach remain
open as listed in `known-limitations.md`.
