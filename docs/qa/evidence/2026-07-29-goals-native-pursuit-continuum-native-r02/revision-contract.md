- Goals → Life Area → Goal hierarchy and framework-owned Back behavior.
- Root-local restoration, fixture identities, accepted truth, and non-mutation.

## Change

- Author Life Area posture without exposing child Goals.
- Establish one compact and focused canonical Goal anchor.
- Give all Home Goals accepted-truth anatomy.
- Replace the focused truth card with an open truth foundation.
- Embed Proof beneath accepted truth.
- Make current movement the strongest operational object.
- Express future certainty materially and let relationship qualify its boundary.
- Increase readable hierarchy and give Light an equivalent mineral character.
- Preserve Goal identity at Accessibility Dynamic Type.

## Proof ceiling

This revision proves only native fixture-driven visual and interaction
calibration across the existing three-screen family. It does not prove runtime
ownership, persistence, production restoration, device shell viability, final
tokens or APIs, production cutover, or `APPROVED_FOR_SWIFTUI`.
