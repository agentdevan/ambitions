# Open Vitality Field Contract

- One canonical Step and one coherent Now → Next → Protected → Open day field.
- Stable identities from `today-flagship/preparing-for-baby/still-counts/v1`.
- Current, proposed, operational, and settled truth distinctions.
- Still counts, explicit review, saving, settlement, History, return, and
  object-scoped recovery.

  dock-owned actions.

  scrolling, functional glass only, and opaque Reduce Transparency equivalents.

## Translate from the references

- Strong object identity and a compact open truth field.
- One continuity rail with shape-based nodes.
- Leading, scannable temporal labels.
- Anchored but distinct continuation, commitment, cancellation, navigation,
  and disclosure controls.
- Distinct silhouettes for root, focus, review, saving, settlement, return,
  Full Day, and recovery.

## Deliberate reference deviations

- Device frames, gradients, halos, tiny metadata, and fixed coordinates are
  rendering artifacts and will not be copied.
- The dock remains root-owned and does not persist through focused or
  consequential depth, consistent with canon.
- The revealed returned Start Here remains read-only because no truthful route
  exists for it in this fixture.
- The focused outcome label remains `Still counts`, not `Choose Still Counts`.
- Exact temporal mutation remains Time-owned; an unavailable transfer posture
  is shown when no source-backed route exists.
- Undo is shown only in a dedicated fixture state with the exact supported
  inverse preconditions.
