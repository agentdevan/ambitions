# Known limitations

- Direct-device proof remains required and incomplete.
- R13 is English-only by owner direction; production localization and RTL are
  neither implemented nor claimed.
- The iPhone Air narrow/resizable evidence frame is incomplete after launchd and
  data-migration failures. Compact iPhone 17e and Pro Max proofs succeeded.
- Physical VoiceOver spoken order, Switch Control, Full Keyboard Access, Voice
  Control, haptics, left/right one-handed reach, edge gestures, low-brightness
  Dark, Dynamic Island variations, Reduce Motion, and Reduce Transparency remain
  device obligations.
- Fixture saving latency is evaluation-only and makes no persistence guarantee.
- Time transfer is fixture-only unless a source-backed runtime route is later
  authorized.
- Undo appears only for the exact supported fixture inverse. It is not a generic
  product-wide rollback contract.
- Blocked consequence (`R13-D10`) is absent because the fixture does not support
  it. No fake capability was added.
- No R13 recording evidence exists by owner direction.
- Screenshots are evaluation references and are not production baselines.
