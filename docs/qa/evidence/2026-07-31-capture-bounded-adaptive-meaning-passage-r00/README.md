- Capture is a temporary full-screen global non-root and is not a tab.
- The neutral origin and initiating Capture control remain the exact in-session return target.
- Origin chrome is hidden while Capture is active; the editor receives initial focus and the native keyboard remains contained.
- Original expression remains visible and recoverable while interpretation stays provisional.
- The ambiguous fixture asks exactly one targeted clarification in native focused depth; Capture does not become a conversation transcript.
- Consequential review preserves proposed Goals meaning, original words, related Time context, unchanged current state, consequence, and next action.
- Capture prepares a fixture-only Goals handoff and performs zero canonical mutations.
- Framework Back preserves Capture context. Global Cancel and `Discard and Close` dismiss to the exact origin and restore Capture-trigger focus.
- `Keep Editing`, `Edit words`, `Edit proposal`, and recovery preserve the in-session draft and appropriate editor focus.
- Accessibility Dynamic Type recomposes the ordered review through native scrolling without removing meaning.
- Search implementation, behavior, screenshots, and evidence remain untouched.
- Capture Routing Calibration remains inactive.
- `APPROVED_FOR_SWIFTUI = false`.

## Changed

- Replaced the R00 form-like composer with an open editorial writing plane integrated into the matte graphite/mineral surface.
- Recast bounded meaning as one expression-to-proposal fold with original words primary and provisional meaning subordinate.
- Moved clarification into framework navigation with `Clarify`, Back, Cancel, one focused response, and one keyboard-safe action hierarchy.
- Consolidated review into a proposal-first consequence passage rather than five equal-weight form sections.
- Recast recovery as a localized retained-draft seam instead of a separate dossier.
- Limited automatic accessibility focus restoration to active VoiceOver sessions so ordinary native chrome and orientation remain stable.

## Removed

- The giant rounded composer card, detached `Destination` block, `Proposed, not added`, and `What this could mean` label.
- Five equally weighted review sections, universal full-width divider rhythm, and stacked filled/capsule actions.
- Recovery-as-dossier anatomy and redundant prototype or implementation language.
- No accepted product behavior was removed.
