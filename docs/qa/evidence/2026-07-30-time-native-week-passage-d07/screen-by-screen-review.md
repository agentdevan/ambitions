selected Wednesday, Now, readable adjacent Thursday context, and one measured
primary chronology. Fixed and protected accepted truth do not depend on color:
fixed uses a pin and leading rule; protected uses a lock and enclosing border.

annotation rather than filling remaining space like an Event.

separate, outlined, and explicitly not scheduled. Named participants and the
