# Architecture-sensitive assumptions

- The fixture is immutable synthetic evidence. It is not a domain repository,
  Event model, or Schedule Placement contract.
- Exact local minute geometry assumes a single visible civil day and does not
  prove timezone changes, daylight-saving transitions, all-day items, or
  multi-day occurrences.
- Proposed placement remains separate from accepted chronology until a future
  durable settlement contract exists.
- External observations remain source-backed inspection and do not imply
  replacement, write-back, or canonical Ambitions Event identity.
- Open calendar space carries no inference about energy, cognition, protection,
  personal usability, or recommendation.
- The host omits production shell and dock composition; shell posture remains
  provisional.
- Framework navigation and typed focus anchors are proving architecture, not an
  authorization to install production routes.
