# B02 provisional color and material system

Status: `PROPOSED`

These roles are local dynamic values in the Foundry package. They are not final
design tokens and do not modify the production design system.

## Palette

- `canvas` and `canvasElevated`: mineral-neutral Light and deep-graphite Dark.
- `objectRelief` and `objectInset`: restrained opaque local elevation.
- `labelPrimary`, `labelSecondary`, and `labelTertiary`: readable neutral type
  hierarchy.
- `ambitionsAccent` and `ambitionsAccentMuted`: sparse violet-indigo for
  selection, proposal, and primary interaction.
- `currentTruth`: neutral inset.
- `proposedTruth`: restrained violet relief plus paired-node structure.
- `settledTruth`: muted mineral/moss seam plus resolved-node structure.
- `interruptedTruth`: restrained amber seam plus broken-node structure.
- `protectedState`, `fixedState`, and `externalState`: narrow semantic accents
  with non-color equivalents.

## Material law

- Primary content is matte and opaque.
- Relief comes from tonal relationships, spacing, asymmetric shape, seams, and
  minimal shadow—not blur or glow.
- Liquid Glass is limited to functional Crowned Edge Dock chrome.
- Reduce Transparency uses the explicit opaque dock branch.
- Increased Contrast adds authored structural outlines and stronger separators.
- Low-brightness Dark retains distinguishable planes in Simulator evidence;
  physical OLED inspection remains incomplete.

## Rejected treatments

No glass content cards, large saturated panels, broad category colors, glow,
featureless black, pure-white card stack, gold/luxury cliché, or hue-only state
distinction was introduced.

## Proof ceiling

This is a calibration palette for the Foundry slice only. It is not final token
authority, production approval, or direct-device material proof.
