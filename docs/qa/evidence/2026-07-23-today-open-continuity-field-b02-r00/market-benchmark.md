# Restrained market benchmark

Research date: 2026-07-23. Sources are official Apple editorial and App Store
materials. This is not a moodboard and does not authorize copied identity or IA.

| Experience | Useful principle | B02 adaptation | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Structured](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/) | Scanable time and open capacity | Now, next fixed, protected, and open anchors in one overview; complete chronology in Full Day | planner IA and agenda-first root |
| [Tide Guide](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/) | One organizing geometry makes dense time legible | Continuity Spine and temporal alignment carry density | chart spectacle and decorative motion |
| [Flighty](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=970ncww4) | Stable identity and degraded connectivity remain obvious | Keep the material present truth first; scope offline/stale seams to the object | airport styling or alarmist error state |
| [Mela](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/) | Active-step emphasis changes without losing the whole object | Focus, saving, and settlement change local emphasis while Step identity persists | disconnected state screens |
| [Gentler Streak](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s) | Humane context without relentless performance | Still Counts remains matter-of-fact, personal, and non-gamified | scores, streaks, shame, ceremony |
| [Moonlitt](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/) | Simple elegance and disciplined functional material | Native glass stays in crown/dock/transient chrome | glass content and brand spectacle |

## Applied rules

- Use one strong organizing geometry and layered emphasis, not equal-weight
  cards.
- Put the consequential current object first while retaining useful supporting
  reality.
- Preserve identity as emphasis changes through focus, proposal, saving,
  settlement, return, and recovery.
- Let system navigation, sheets, buttons, disclosures, progress, and haptics
  carry familiar behavior.
- Build brand from rhythm, type, object geometry, and the Continuity Spine, not
  from a custom font, mascot, glow, or omnipresent wordmark.
- Treat quiet and degraded reality honestly; never fill it with fabricated
  urgency or generic error ceremony.
