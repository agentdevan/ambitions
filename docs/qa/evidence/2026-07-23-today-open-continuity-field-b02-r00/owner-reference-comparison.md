detailed compatibility decision; the final comparison contact sheet binds

## Interpretation rule

B02 targets the board's spatial confidence, density, object articulation,
temporal anatomy, and distinct screen silhouettes. It makes no pixel-match or
design-ownership claim. All B02 screenshots remain native Foundry evaluation
references with `production_baseline = false`.
