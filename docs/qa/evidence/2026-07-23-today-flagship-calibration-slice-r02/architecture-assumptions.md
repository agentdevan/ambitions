# Architecture-sensitive assumptions

- Views continue to consume small immutable snapshots. Fixtures do not own
  product policy or persistence.
- The calibration object is a stable Step because the existing command contract
  supports its lifecycle. This does not define every future Start Here object.
- Today initiates the fixture journey; durable mutation ownership remains
  external to the Foundry view.
- Full-screen consequential review and the recovery sheet remain the inspected
  R01 native presentation choices.
- Collapsed review `Details` and settlement `View history` prove disclosure
  hierarchy, not final Receipt/History information architecture.
- Synthetic timing proves operational posture only and makes no persistence or
  latency guarantee.
- No inverse action appears because the fixture does not satisfy every exact
  inverse-command prerequisite.
- Alternative outcomes remain canonical possibilities, but R02 omits them
  because no executable, exact fixture review paths already existed.
- `step.send-launch-brief` de-duplication is a returned-Today fixture projection
  decision, not a universal eligibility law.
- The semantic return anchor remains the settled nursery Step; the new Start
  Here does not steal focus.
- `ar-SA` content is evaluation localization only. It neither creates nor
  authorizes production localization infrastructure.
- Simulator evidence cannot close direct-device focus, safe-area, gesture,
  assistive-input, motion, transparency, reach, or low-brightness obligations.
- No runtime adapter, app-entry integration, legacy import, or dependency was
  added.
