# Material, color, typography, and density review

- Primary content remains matte and opaque on mineral-neutral Light and deep
  graphite Dark planes.
- Opaque tonal elevation, semantic spines, spacing, and separators create
  localized relief. There are no glass content cards, glow, or decorative
  gradients.
- Native material is limited to the functional Crowned Edge Dock; Reduce
  Transparency retains the authored opaque equivalent.
- Violet-indigo is reserved for primary action, selected shell state, and the
  proposed truth seam. Settlement and interruption use restrained semantic
  cues that are also labelled and shaped.
- System typography supplies decisive screen, object, state, and relationship
  landmarks. Metadata sizing is limited to secondary context.
- Root, focused, review, settlement, returned, and recovery compositions remain
  distinguishable at contact-sheet scale without relying only on their titles.
- Useful object relationships replace R02's explanatory prose and inert space.
  The result remains naturally scrollable instead of compressing the complete
  model into one viewport.

The owner graphite board informs spatial confidence, object articulation,
density, and action anchoring only. Its obsolete navigation and category
grammar are explicitly rejected.
