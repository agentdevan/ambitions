# B01 visual anatomy

## Root

The compact Today crown, continuous matte plane, and right-edge shell remain.
One plane-attached Start Here field carries Step identity, parent pursuit,
current truth, fit, consequence, time, and one native primary action. A narrow
semantic spine and internal state field provide local relief without turning
the viewport into a card dashboard.

## Timeline

Supporting objects share a temporal rail: time is a compact anchor, identity
is primary, relationship and state follow. Protected and fixed posture use
labels and symbols as well as restrained color. Start Here is not repeated in
the timeline while it owns the primary field.

## Focused Step

The stable Step header persists from Today depth. Parent pursuit sits directly
under identity. Current truth is a neutral formed field; fit, consequence, and
time are relationship rows inside the same object anatomy. `Still counts` is
the only displayed executable outcome.

## Review and saving

The review aligns the neutral current field with a proposed violet-indigo
field through a directional transition seam. Consequence, pursuit, and local
history remain compact within the proposed region. Native safe-area actions
anchor commit and cancel. Saving preserves the same comparison and adds a
proportionate native progress seam without implying settlement.

## Settlement, return, and recovery

Settlement replaces proposal relief with restrained green-neutral settled
relief, keeps pursuit continuity, and subordinates history. Returned Today
keeps the nursery Step in its settled location while the launch brief becomes
the sole Start Here object. Recovery uses the same object-field grammar in the
existing native sheet and preserves saved progress beneath truthful actions.

These units are Foundry-local calibration composition, not final design-system
components or tokens.
