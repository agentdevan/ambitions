# Design contract

## Thesis

Goals presents persistent living pursuits as an identity-led field. One Life Area may open to reveal Goals without implying rank. A selected Goal may reveal an attached lens that connects accepted truth, consequence, active thread, next movement, and Proof before deeper inspection.

## Required journey

Goals root → select Home → select Welcome our baby home → reveal Linked Goal Lens → open focused Goal → inspect consequential relationship → inspect Goal Path and history → return through the same Goal and Life Area context.

The complete journey is inspection-only. No canonical state changes, Receipt creation, Goal mutation, Path mutation, activation, or schedule editing occurs.

## Spatial law

- Open matte plane is the default.
- Identity precedes state, consequence, Proof, and action.
- Local relief appears only around the attached lens and selected Path detail.
- One Life Area expands; unselected Life Areas remain compact and equal.
- The lens remains structurally attached to its selected Goal.
- Focused depth expresses pursuit continuity, not project management.
- Standard Goal Path is horizontal and native; accessibility sizes use an ordered list.

## Prohibited transfer

No Start Here, Later Today, Today temporal rail, View Full Day, Still Counts, Today settlement placement, or returned-Today focus composition appears in Goals.
