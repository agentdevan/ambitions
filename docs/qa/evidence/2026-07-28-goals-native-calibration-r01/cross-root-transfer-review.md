# Cross-root transfer review

## Transferred from Today R14

- Matte opaque graphite/mineral planes.
- System typography and identity-first hierarchy.
- Stable canonical object identity and owner lineage.
- Accepted truth, material consequence, native action roles, progressive disclosure, and natural scrolling.
- Meaning that survives color, transparency, motion, and type-size changes.
- Crowned Edge Dock ownership without declaring the shell production-ready.

## Adapted for Goals

- Life Areas form an open hierarchy rather than a temporal field.
- One expanded Life Area contains stable Goal identities without rankings.
- The Linked Goal Lens is attached to one selected Goal instead of becoming a dashboard module.
- Focused depth expresses direction, active thread, next movement, Proof, and consequence.
- Goal Path communicates pursuit continuity through ordered semantic nodes, not chronology or percentage.

## Deliberately not propagated

- Start Here, Later Today, Today’s temporal rail, View Full Day, Still Counts, settlement placement, and returned-object focus composition.
- Today-specific root title scale, time posture, and current-to-later narrative.

## Finding

The provisional grammar transfers at the level of identity, lineage, truth, consequence, action, and accessibility. Goals requires its own hierarchy and Path anatomy; copying Today’s composition would have violated the transfer gate.
