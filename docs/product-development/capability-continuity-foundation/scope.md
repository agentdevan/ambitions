## Outcome

After completing meaningful work, reviewing or closing a Goal, or changing
direction, the user can see the capabilities that work may have developed and
decide which derived capability interpretation Ambitions may retain. Truthful
source Goals, Steps, Proof, Receipts, and History remain governed by their own
lifecycle and are not erased by capability controls.

The result is an inspectable, user-owned Life Capital record of skills,
knowledge, and useful methods that can be reused in another context. Experience
is supporting evidence or context, not itself a capability. Each capability
explains what it means, what evidence supports it, what remains uncertain, and
whether it may influence future planning. The user can add, confirm, correct,
archive, move to Trash, restore, or permanently delete it without changing the
historical Goal, Step, Proof, Receipt, or History Event that records what
actually happened.

This Scope establishes capability continuity only. It does not yet turn those
capabilities into career, education, or hobby recommendations; generate or
select a new destination; compare routes; or place work into Time. Those
behaviors belong to the named focused initiatives in the approved portfolio.
This Scope makes the foundation trustworthy before any of those consumers can
request purpose-specific permission.

## In scope

- A persistent, inspectable capability collection within existing You/Life
  Capital depth, without a new top-level destination or standalone dashboard.
- Manual creation of a capability as a user-stated Life Capital claim.
- Bounded capability proposals derived locally from accepted, non-sensitive
  completion/closure observations, explicit user-confirmed practice facts, and
  user-approved Proof evidence.
- Proposal review at calm review moments, including Goal review, completion,
  ending, or deliberate destination change, rather than interrupting execution.
- Independent, non-ranked provenance facets for user-stated, practiced, and
  Proof-linked evidence.
- Clear evidence lineage back to the relevant Goal, milestone, Step, closure,
  accepted completion/closure History Event, user-confirmed practice fact, or
  Proof, while keeping Receipts distinct from evidence.
- User confirmation, correction, `Not now`, `Not this`, future-use
  permission, archive, Move to Trash, restore, and permanent-deletion controls
  with explicit consequences.
- User-controlled accumulation of evidence from multiple Goals under one
  capability without silently merging distinct capability meanings.
- Preservation of confirmed capabilities when the originating Goal completes,
  ends, archives, or changes direction.
- An explicit, default-off per-capability future-use permission, plus disclosure
  of every current consumer or an honest statement that there is no current
  consumer. This eligibility setting is not advance consent for a later feature.
- Local-first, offline, private, accessible, non-judgmental behavior with no
  capability, proficiency, compatibility, employability, or Life Capital score.
- Honest handling of uncertainty, contradiction, possible duplicates, and
  insufficient evidence.

## Out of scope

- Career, education, hobby, or other destination recommendations.
- O*NET, BLS, ESCO, CTDL, CASE, CIP, CareerOneStop, employer, school, licensing,
  or other public pathway ingestion and crosswalking.
- Alternative-destination generation, career ladders, promotion sequences,
  requirement-gap analysis, eligibility adjudication, or formal prior-learning
  equivalency.
- Multiple-route comparison, option-preserving bridge selection, success
  prediction, or Goal Path adoption.
- Event-relative time-quality learning, schedule placement, schedule reflow, or
  changes to Time.
- LinkedIn archives, Open Badges, credential wallets, imports, exports, or
  external profile write-back.
- Issuer verification or an `issuer-credentialed` provenance claim created by
  this increment.
- Automatic creation or mutation of a Goal, North Star, Goal Path, Life Branch,
  Step, Proof, schedule, or destination.
- Capability inference from health, disability, religion, relationships,
  finances, citizenship, age, medical information, or other sensitive context.
- Medical, legal, licensing, hiring, admissions, accreditation, or other
  professional eligibility determinations.
- A universal person model, capability dossier, social profile, public resume,
  global skill graph, personality model, or hidden behavioral authority.
- Capability levels, XP, badges-as-rewards, streaks, ranks, scores, forced
  comparison, or automatic capability decay.

## Requirements

### REQ-001 — User-owned capability continuity

Ambitions must provide an inspectable Life Capital capability collection. A
capability is a reusable ability, body of knowledge, or useful method; an
experience is evidence or context for it, not the capability itself. A
capability becomes user-owned only when the user creates it directly or
confirms a proposal. The collection must live within existing You/Life Capital
depth and must not become a new root surface or dominant dashboard.

### REQ-002 — Manual entry remains valid without system evidence

The user must be able to add a capability directly. A manually added capability
is clearly identified as user-stated and does not require completion, Proof, a
credential, or Ambitions confirmation to remain valid as the user's own claim.

### REQ-003 — Proposals require relevant accepted evidence

Ambitions must create one reviewable proposal for the next eligible calm moment
when all of these conditions are true:

- a completed Goal, milestone, or Step, an explicit user-confirmed reflection,
  or a user-approved Proof describes the ability, body of knowledge, or method
  the user actually practiced;
- explicit user-authored wording in that same capability-bearing activity or
  reflection—or an already confirmed relationship—supports the proposed name
  and meaning;
- the completion/practice observation or Proof relationship has been accepted
  by the user, the source remains available, and the evidence is neither
  sensitive nor contradicted; and
- that exact evidence basis and proposed meaning is not already confirmed,
  pending review, or covered by a retained Not-this dismissal.

The next eligible moment is the next Goal review, closure, ending, or known
pivot associated with the evidence. If none occurs, the proposal appears at the
next user-initiated Life Capital inspection. Ambitions must present the same
evidence basis and meaning at most once unless the user explicitly asks to see
it again or materially new evidence changes the basis.

Goal or resource wording that merely names an aspiration or subject is not
practice evidence. For example, completing "Buy a leadership book" under a
Goal named "Learn leadership" does not establish practiced leadership. Ambitions
may normalize presentation but must not infer an unstated trait such as
leadership, discipline, aptitude, or personality from completion behavior. When
no explicit wording supports a name, Ambitions may offer a neutral "What did
you practice or learn?" reflection prompt and let the user supply the
capability.

Accepted completion/closure observations or explicit user-confirmed practice
facts may support a practiced facet; unrelated History mutations such as
rename, archive, scheduling, rejection, or correction may not. User-approved
Proof may additionally support a Proof-linked facet. A Receipt supplies
mutation provenance and must never be treated as practice evidence by itself.
An ended or incomplete Goal, missed work, or lack of Proof must not be treated
as evidence of inability.

When evidence is absent, too weak, conflicting, or sensitive, Ambitions must
remain quiet or invite manual entry without suggesting a capability as fact.

### REQ-004 — Proposals are reviewable and non-authoritative until confirmed

Proposals may appear only at a calm Goal review, closure, ending, known pivot,
or Life Capital inspection moment; they must not interrupt Step completion or
create a new approval chore for every action. Every proposal must show the
proposed capability meaning, why it appeared, the supporting evidence,
uncertainty, the default-off future-use eligibility, and the absence of a
current consumer before confirmation. The user can edit,
confirm, choose **Not now**, or choose **Not this**. Not now keeps the proposal
available for later review without creating a capability or influence. Not
this records a content-minimized dismissal of that exact evidence basis and
keeps it quiet unless materially new evidence changes the basis. Materially new
evidence means a later, separately accepted capability-bearing activity, an
explicit user-confirmed practice reflection, newly user-approved Proof, or a
substantive user-authored source correction that changes the capability's
meaning or applicability. Elapsed time, relaunch, repeated presentation,
unrelated metadata, or the same unchanged evidence is not materially new.

Before confirmation, the proposal is neither a user-owned capability nor a
planning influence. Confirmation makes its user-approved meaning user-stated,
alongside any separately supported practiced or Proof-linked facets. If an edit
is no longer supported by the cited evidence, Ambitions must re-evaluate the
relationship: the user may save the edited meaning as user-stated, choose
different evidence, or cancel, but unsupported practiced or Proof-linked facets
must not carry over.

### REQ-005 — Evidence facets are independent and non-ranked

A capability must present user-stated, practiced, and Proof-linked provenance
as independent facets that may coexist. The product must not arrange them as a
strength ladder or convert them into mastery, proficiency, worth, compatibility,
or employability. Evidence source, relevant context, date, uncertainty,
freshness, and contradiction state must remain inspectable where applicable.
These are product semantics; Design may express them in plain language such as
"You practiced this in…" and "Evidence you saved" rather than exposing
internal category names.

### REQ-006 — Capability meaning and evidence remain correctable and cumulative

The user must be able to correct the capability's name, plain-language meaning,
relevant context, and whether cited evidence applies. Evidence from later Goals
may be added to an existing capability only after the user chooses between
adding it to that capability, keeping it separate, or creating another
capability. The user may remove a capability-evidence relationship without
rewriting or deleting either source. A correction must retain truthful lineage,
must not rewrite the source Goal or evidence, and must affect only consumers
that used the corrected capability or evidence relationship.

An evidence relationship must follow the source object's lifecycle. Archive of
a still-available source does not remove its facet. While a source is in Trash,
the relationship becomes unavailable/recoverable and cannot newly support a
proposal. Permanent deletion, governed redaction, unlinking, or correction of a
source removes or redacts the capability-side relationship and marks the
affected facet missing, redacted, contradicted, or needing review as appropriate.
The user-owned capability remains unless the user separately changes its
lifecycle. Any retained lineage must be content-minimized and must not reveal
source content that its owner deleted or redacted.

### REQ-007 — Lifecycle and learning controls have distinct consequences

The product must support the following user-understandable consequences:

- **Not now / Not this:** follow the proposal consequences in REQ-004.
- **Reset suggestions:** clear pending proposals and remembered Not-this
  dismissals without changing confirmed user-owned capabilities, their approved
  evidence relationships, explicit settings, or source history. The same
  proposal may appear again only after the user explicitly requests
  reconsideration during a later Life Capital inspection or after materially
  new evidence. Individually removing a Not-this dismissal has the same
  consequence; it never causes an interruptive or immediate presentation
  elsewhere.
- **Archive:** remove it from the active capability collection, preserve its
  history, exclude it from planning, and allow restoration.
- **Move to Trash:** remove it from regular collection views and planning,
  preserve a recoverable capability record and relationships, and allow restore
  to the prior valid state.
- **Permanently delete:** after explicit consequence review, remove the
  capability content, derived interpretation, capability-evidence relationships,
  and all future influence. Source Goals, Steps, Proof, Receipts, and History
  remain governed by their own lifecycle. Capability mutation history retains
  only a disclosed content-free deletion tombstone needed for deterministic
  integrity; it must not retain the capability name, meaning, or evidence
  context in searchable or reconstructable form.

Pending proposals and remembered Not-this dismissals are learned influences,
not hidden internal flags. They must be inspectable in learning controls,
individually reversible or removable, clearable through Reset suggestions, and
covered by truthful History, Receipt, and replay behavior when durable.

### REQ-008 — Originating Goal changes do not erase capability truth

A confirmed capability must survive completion, ending, archive, or a deliberate
destination change of its originating Goal. The product must describe it
positively as retained Life Capital. If a later comparison finds it irrelevant
to another destination, that result must not invalidate, downgrade, or erase
the capability's original evidence.

### REQ-009 — Capabilities do not decay automatically

Ambitions must not silently lower or remove a capability because time passed.
Evidence may be marked stale, contradicted, or needing review independently of
the capability. Changes in an external requirement must not imply that the
user's capability decayed.

### REQ-010 — Future influence is explicit and inspectable

Every confirmed capability must expose one default-off **future-use
permission**. Turning it on makes the capability eligible for consideration by
a later planning feature; it does not itself authorize any feature to read or
use the capability. Every later consumer requires its own approved Scope and
fresh, consumer-specific disclosure and consent before first use. The user must
be able to turn future-use permission on or off at any time. The product must
list a capability's current consumers when any exist. Until this increment has
a consumer, the collection states once—rather than on every record—that no
current feature uses this capability collection to change planning yet.
Future-use-off, archived, Trashed, or permanently deleted capabilities must not
influence planning. This Scope adds
no capability consumer, so capability changes affect no Goal Path and trigger
no path mutation or simulation in this increment. Any later consumer requires
its own approved Scope and must preserve canon's requirement to resimulate
affected paths and show impact without silently committing material change.

### REQ-011 — Related concepts remain distinct

Ambitions must not collapse a capability into a credential, Proof, experience,
resource, eligibility fact, requirement, personality trait, interest, value, or
health condition. Similar capability names may be offered for user review, but
the product must never silently merge records or treat label similarity as
equivalence.

### REQ-012 — Privacy includes derived capability meaning

Capability records, proposals, evidence links, corrections, and influence
history must remain private local data and work offline without an account.
They must not be sent to Account, R2, Source Atlas, hosted AI, telemetry, or an
external profile. Ambitions must not derive proposals from protected context.
Every proposed label, derived meaning, evidence relationship, and manual record
must have an inspectable privacy/handling classification based on the output and
linked context, not only the source input. If explicit wording, derived meaning,
or cited context would reveal a protected category—or classification is
uncertain—Ambitions must remain quiet rather than proposing it. A user may
manually enter such a capability, but it remains excluded from future
use in this Scope and an attempted re-enable must explain that separate
privacy research and product scope are required. The user can correct a false
positive when the capability and linked context do not actually reveal a
protected category. A genuine protected-category record cannot be opted into
planning use in this increment.

### REQ-013 — No scoring, judgment, or forced identity

Capability presentation must use calm, specific language and must not expose a
score, rank, level invented by Ambitions, streak, XP system, comparative label,
personality conclusion, or judgment of the user's potential. "Ended," "not
currently relevant," and "needs review" must never be presented as failure,
waste, or proof of inability.

### REQ-014 — Capability actions cannot mutate adjacent canonical objects

Creating, confirming, correcting, changing future-use permission,
archiving, moving to Trash, restoring, or permanently deleting a capability
must not itself create or change a Goal, North Star, Goal Path, Life Branch,
Step, Proof, schedule, destination, or completion state. Adding or removing an
evidence relationship changes the capability record only; it does not mutate
the source object, auto-complete work, or convert a candidate idea into a
canonical object.

### REQ-015 — Mutations are truthful and recoverable

Capability creation and control changes must follow existing local mutation,
History, Receipt, replay, archive/restore, Trash/restore, and permanent-deletion
laws. The user must be told exactly what changed, what remains, what future
influence was removed or restored, what content-free tombstone remains after
permanent deletion, and whether undo or restoration is available.

### REQ-016 — The experience is accessible without losing meaning

All capability evidence, provenance, uncertainty, privacy, lifecycle, influence
state, and actions must be understandable with VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch
Control, Full Keyboard Access, Dynamic Type, increased contrast, reduced
motion, and non-color indicators. Confirmation,
correction, Not now, Not this, future-use permission, Reset suggestions,
archive, Move to Trash, restore, and permanent deletion must have named controls
and must not depend on gesture, spatial layout, color, or visual-only
relationships.

After a successful, failed, or cancelled mutation, focus must return to the
affected capability or the exact result/recovery action, and status or error
changes must be announced without relying on visual observation. Direct
assistive-technology verification is required for the supported actions.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-001 (REQ-001, REQ-002):** A user can manually add a capability, find it
   again within You/Life Capital, and see it labeled as user-stated without
   attaching Proof or completing a Goal. The collection is reachable through
   You/Life Capital depth and does not add a root destination or dominate the
   You landing surface.
2. **AC-002 (REQ-003, REQ-005):** When explicit user-authored wording names a
   capability practiced in the same completed activity or user-confirmed
   reflection, Ambitions presents exactly one reviewable proposal at the next
   associated calm moment or subsequent user-initiated Life Capital inspection.
   Its exact supporting objects are visible. Adding user-approved Proof adds a
   distinct Proof-linked facet rather than a higher score.
3. **AC-003 (REQ-003):** When no explicit user-authored wording or approved
   relationship supports a capability name, Ambitions offers at most a neutral
   reflection prompt and does not invent one. A Receipt alone, ended Goal, or
   missed Step produces neither a practiced proposal nor a negative capability
   claim. Absence of Proof neither supports nor blocks a proposal that has
   independently eligible practiced evidence. Completing "Buy a leadership
   book" under "Learn leadership" does not establish practiced leadership.
4. **AC-004 (REQ-003, REQ-012):** Sensitive, contradictory, or insufficient
   evidence produces no behavioral capability inference and no hidden planning
   influence.
5. **AC-005 (REQ-004):** At a calm review or Life Capital inspection moment,
   the user sees the proposed meaning, why it appeared, exact evidence,
   uncertainty, and the fact that no current feature uses it. The user can edit
   it, confirm it, choose Not now, or choose Not this; Step completion is never
   interrupted. Before confirmation it is neither user-owned nor influential.
   Not now keeps it pending without influence. Not this keeps that exact basis
   quiet until a separately accepted capability-bearing activity, explicit
   practice reflection, newly approved Proof, or substantive source correction
   changes the basis; elapsed time, relaunch, metadata, and repeated unchanged
   evidence do not.
6. **AC-006 (REQ-004):** If the user edits a proposal to a meaning unsupported
   by its cited evidence, Ambitions removes or declines the unsupported
   practiced and Proof-linked facets and offers save-as-user-stated, choose
   different evidence, or cancel. Confirmation never transfers unsupported
   provenance to the edited meaning.
7. **AC-007 (REQ-005, REQ-013):** A confirmed capability uses calm, specific,
   non-shaming language and shows its independent
   provenance facets, evidence context, uncertainty, and relevant dates without
   a strength, proficiency, compatibility, employability, rank, badge, XP, or
   Life Capital score.
8. **AC-008 (REQ-006, REQ-011, REQ-015):** Evidence from two different Goals
   can accumulate under one user-chosen capability after explicit review. The
   user can add the evidence to the existing capability, keep it separate, or
   create another capability; adding or removing the relationship leaves both
   source Goals and their History unchanged.
9. **AC-009 (REQ-006, REQ-011, REQ-015):** Correcting a capability or declaring
   cited evidence irrelevant records the change, preserves source truth, avoids
   silently merging similar labels, and re-evaluates only declared consumers.
10. **AC-010 (REQ-007):** The user can inspect, individually reverse, or remove
    retained pending proposals and Not-this dismissals. Reset suggestions
    clears both classes of retained influence without changing confirmed
    capabilities, approved evidence relationships, explicit settings, or
    source history. After individual removal or reset, unchanged evidence can
    appear again only after the user explicitly requests reconsideration during
    a later Life Capital inspection; it does not immediately interrupt another
    surface. Durable changes produce truthful History, Receipt, and replay
    results.
11. **AC-011 (REQ-007, REQ-010, REQ-015):** Archive removes a capability from
    the active collection and planning influence while preserving restorable
    content and History.
12. **AC-012 (REQ-007, REQ-015):** Move to Trash removes a capability from
    regular views and planning but permits exact restoration to its prior valid
    state. Permanent deletion separately shows consequences, removes capability
    content, derived meaning, evidence relationships, and future influence, and
    retains only a disclosed content-free deletion tombstone; original Goals,
    Steps, Proof, Receipts, and History remain unchanged under their own
    lifecycle.
13. **AC-013 (REQ-008, REQ-009, REQ-013):** Completing, ending, archiving, or
    deliberately changing the originating Goal leaves the confirmed capability
    intact. Stale or contradicted evidence is marked separately for review, and
    a changed external requirement never decays or downgrades the capability.
14. **AC-014 (REQ-010):** Each capability starts with future-use permission off,
    lets the user turn it on or off, and remains excluded from planning while it
    is off. Turning it on makes the capability only eligible for a later
    consumer; it grants no advance consent. A later consumer cannot use it until
    fresh consumer-specific disclosure and consent. Current consumers are listed
    only when they exist. In this increment the collection states once that no
    current feature uses this capability collection to change planning yet.
15. **AC-015 (REQ-011):** A credential, Proof, experience record, eligibility
    fact, resource, requirement, interest, and capability with related wording
    remain separately inspectable and are never silently treated as the same
    thing.
16. **AC-016 (REQ-012):** Manual creation, proposal review, correction,
    permission changes, archive, Trash/restore, permanent deletion, inspection,
    History, Receipt, and replay work offline without account or network access;
    privacy-egress tests show no private capability payload reaches a prohibited
    destination.
17. **AC-017 (REQ-012):** The user can inspect each record's handling
    classification and correct a false positive when neither the capability nor
    linked context is protected. Wording, derived meaning, or cited context that
    actually exposes a protected category produces no proposal. A user-entered
    protected capability remains visible but locked out of future use in this
    increment, and Ambitions makes no medical, legal, licensing, hiring,
    admissions, or similar determination.
18. **AC-018 (REQ-014):** Exercising every capability action leaves Goal, North
    Star, Goal Path, Life Branch, Step, Proof, schedule, destination, and
    completion state unchanged. Adding or removing evidence affects only the
    capability-side relationship and never mutates the source object.
19. **AC-019 (REQ-015):** Every durable capability mutation produces truthful
    History/Receipt/replay evidence and an exact result explanation; archive,
    restore, Move to Trash, restore from Trash, permission changes, and
    Reset suggestions recover deterministically where their semantics permit.
20. **AC-020 (REQ-016):** VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, and Full
    Keyboard Access expose capability meaning, provenance, evidence links,
    uncertainty, privacy, influence state, and every action in a stable semantic
    order. Every action has a named, non-gesture control; lifecycle and influence
    states never rely on color. Dynamic Type, increased contrast, and reduced
    motion preserve the same content and controls. Focus returns to the exact
    result or recovery action, status changes are announced, and the supported
    actions receive direct assistive-technology verification.
21. **AC-021 (REQ-006):** Archiving a cited source preserves its available
    relationship. Moving the source to Trash makes the relationship unavailable
    but recoverable and prevents it from supporting a new proposal. Restoring
    the source restores the valid relationship. Permanent deletion, redaction,
    unlinking, or correction removes or updates the capability-side relationship
    without exposing deleted source content or deleting the user-owned
    capability.
22. **AC-022 (REQ-003, REQ-004, REQ-006, REQ-008, REQ-010):** A completed,
    capability-bearing activity produces one calm proposal; confirmation creates
    a user-owned capability with future-use permission off; later eligible
    evidence from another Goal can be attached only by user choice; and ending
    or changing the original Goal leaves the accumulated capability inspectable
    without changing either Goal.

Implementation of this Scope requires a new canonical Capability object contract
because no current object specification owns durable, user-correctable
capability identity or evidence accumulated across multiple Goals. That object
does not create a new product center or root surface. Canon impact is:

- A new `docs/canon/specifications/objects/capability.md` should own capability
  identity and meaning, independent evidence facets, multi-source relationships,
  creation and mutation, confirmation settlement, future-use permission,
  lifecycle, user correction, non-scoring, non-decay, evidence-relationship

  credential, requirement, or trait. Local Learning may request a confirmed

  collection's relationship to Life Capital and preserve Life Capital
  editability and anti-gamification laws.
- `docs/canon/specifications/systems/local-learning.md` should own bounded
  proposal eligibility, pending proposal state, Not now and Not this behavior,
  suggestion reset, learned-influence inspection, and affected-consumer
  re-evaluation.
- `docs/canon/specifications/systems/privacy-and-data-classification.md` should
  own the derived-output and linked-context handling boundary, local-only
  retention, protected-category exclusion, deletion minimization, and prohibited
  egress.
- `docs/canon/specifications/objects/proof.md` should own its narrower evidence
  relationship while preserving Proof/Receipt separation and non-grading.
- Each cited source object's existing owner continues to own its lifecycle. The
  Capability object owns the resulting relationship state.
  `docs/canon/specifications/objects/history-event.md` and Receipt canon should
  record the resulting immutable mutation or governed-redaction facts without
  reconstructing deleted source content.
- `docs/canon/specifications/surfaces/you.md` should own Life Capital inspection

  own Goal review, ending, and known-pivot presentation.
  `docs/canon/specifications/journeys/closure-and-proof.md` should own only its
  closure-specific integration. Local Learning decides whether a proposal is
  eligible and quiet; these surfaces decide where the eligible proposal appears.
- `docs/canon/standards/accessibility.md`, including `A11Y-PROOF-MATRIX-001`,
  remains applicable to every new state and action; this Scope does not narrow
  its requirements.

This Scope does not require new career/pathway, Goal Path, Life Branch, or Time
behavior. Current canon continues to govern until implementation deliberately
updates the relevant owning sources.

## Risks and open decisions

The following product decisions are resolved by this Scope so Design does not
invent them:

- The product provides both a persistent Life Capital capability collection and
  calm contextual proposals; it is not proposal-only and not a standalone
  skills dashboard.
- Manual claims remain valid as user-stated. System proposals require relevant
  accepted evidence and explicit confirmation.
- Provenance facets coexist and are not a ladder. This increment does not claim
  issuer verification, proficiency, mastery, or employability.
- Destination recommendation, path comparison, scheduling, profile import,
  credential import, and export belong to their named focused initiatives in
  the approved portfolio; they are not hidden parts of this Scope.
- Future-use eligibility is default-off and inspectable even when no consumer
  exists yet; the product must say so honestly rather than simulate value. A
  each named consumer still requires fresh, consumer-specific consent.
- Evidence may compound across Goals only through an explicit user choice; label
  similarity never silently merges capability identity.
- Not now postpones a proposal. Not this retains a content-minimized dismissal
  of its exact evidence basis until the user removes it, resets suggestions, or
  materially new evidence changes that basis.
- Archive, Move to Trash, restore, and permanent deletion are distinct. Reset
  suggestions affects only pending proposals and Not-this dismissals.
- This increment creates no planning consumer. Later consumer work must have a
  separately approved Scope and preserve resimulation and impact disclosure.

Remaining delivery risks and Design-level questions are:

- **Overclaim risk:** users may read "practiced" or "Proof-linked" as mastery.
  Design must keep provenance and uncertainty more prominent than celebratory
  language.
- **Noise risk:** even eligible proposals could feel repetitive. Design must
  express the Scope's at-most-once rule and calm presentation moments clearly;
  grooming must prove deduplication and the materially-new-evidence boundary.
  Neither may broaden proposal evidence or infer unnamed traits.
- **Sensitive-output risk:** an ordinary-looking capability label or evidence
  context can reveal protected information. Design must make sensitivity and
  default exclusion inspectable without forcing the user to expose why.
- **Duplicate-language risk:** capabilities from different contexts may share a
  label while meaning different things, or different labels may describe the
  same practice. Design must support user review without silent deduplication or
  a universal taxonomy.
- **Representation risk:** current canon and source do not yet establish the
  smallest durable Capability representation or receiving service. Design must
  choose the minimum architecture that realizes the new canonical Capability
  contract without duplicating identity or treating current Proof-capital and
  Source Atlas models as automatic authority.
- **Usability risk:** external user research has not established whether people
  will regularly inspect Life Capital. Design should validate comprehension of
  the persistent collection, contextual proposal, provenance facets, and
  controls before implementation grooming.
