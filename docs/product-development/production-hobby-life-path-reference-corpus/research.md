## Idea and user problem

The approved v1 portfolio can recommend from a synthetic two-family hobby pack
and can deliver narrow public packs for federal recreation safety, creative
references, and civic statistics. The eventual intelligence platform needs a
maintained public corpus broad enough to discover non-career, non-education
possibilities—creative practices, collecting and inquiry, recreation, service,
community participation, and other user-chosen pursuits—without inventing a
universal taxonomy or turning leisure into productivity.

The user problem differs from career and education. There is no single public
authority over “hobbies” or the meaning of a good life. An activity may be:

- an informal private practice with no governing body;
- a source of play, rest, expression, curiosity, connection, contribution, or
  challenge whose value only the user can define;
- an organized game or program with publisher/association rules;
- a local provider or community offering with fast-changing access/cost;
- a physical activity with site, equipment, weather, legal, certification, or
  safety authorities; or
- a practice that overlaps career, education, health, religion, relationships,
  civic life, or identity without belonging to those owners.

A universal `Hobby` row containing category, skills, cost, time, equipment,
safety, providers, and popularity would erase those boundaries. It could treat
a community label as authority, a park guide as general emergency advice, a
program progression as universal proficiency, or an observed capability as a
reason the person should optimize their free time.

The product value is a maintained offline-capable possibility catalog with
source-native identity, aliases, source-published relationships, domain/risk
review, starter references, rights, freshness, coverage, and explicit claim
ceilings. It supports calm discovery and future local comparison. It does not
own personal meaning, recommendation, current opportunities/providers,
safety/legal decisions, Goal/path creation, scheduling, participation, social
publishing, or external action.

“Life path” in this initiative means a discoverable non-career/non-formal-
education pursuit such as service or sustained creative/community practice. It
does not mean the canonical `Life Branch` object or a simulated alternative
private graph; generalized Life Branch simulation remains a separate owner.

### Governing canon

- Ambitions preserves private life continuity and rejects a universal person
  score, hidden personality model, pressure mechanics, or forced interpretation.
  Recreation cannot be reframed automatically as career capital, self-
  improvement, or public achievement.
- Source Atlas may deliver only public/reference facts through fixed public
  identities. Private interests, capabilities, schedule, location, health,
  relationships, selections, rejections, and corrections cannot shape a remote
  request, cache key, log, or source feedback.
- Private matching belongs to local Planning. A public corpus cannot receive
  private context or learn from which possibility the user views or chooses.
- Models and sources may propose; typed owners validate accepted mutations. A
  public activity record never creates a Goal, Life Area, Capability, Proof,
  schedule entry, membership, account, submission, or Life Branch.
- Unknown, stale, conflicting, unavailable, unsupported, rights-blocked, and
  unsafe-to-generalize remain explicit product states.

### Approved v1 boundary

`hobby-destination-recommendations` approves opt-in, ephemeral local exploration
for creative/making and knowledge/collecting using a synthetic eligible corpus.
It explicitly says no live hobby corpus is approved and excludes social/
community and physical/safety-bearing results until stricter corpus evidence
exists.

`public-reference-knowledge-foundation` supplies source-preserving claims,
rights/freshness/conflict axes, offline/inspection, and the public-only boundary.
It does not establish a hobby taxonomy.

Other v1 owners remain independent:

- Capability continuity may explain optional prior-progress overlap but cannot
  define an activity or make it personally valuable.
- Goal Path generation may propose steps only after an adopted Goal and
  purpose-eligible source requirements exist.
- Context-quality scheduling owns Time placement and energy/context fit.
- Destination adoption/pivot owns explicit Goal creation and preservation of
  progress.
- Life Branch reconciliation owns complete cross-object alternatives; a
  discoverable pursuit is not a Life Branch.
- Intelligence evaluation can test coverage, grounding, privacy, dignity,
  failure, accessibility, safety, and user comprehension; it cannot manufacture
  authority or rights.

### Live architecture and test seams

The repository contains reusable but narrow infrastructure:

- `coverage-frontiers.json` has separate `creative_project_reference`,
  `hobbies_recreation`, and `volunteering_public_reference` frontiers. Their own
  non-claims reject final plans, universal coverage, personalized planning,
  current opportunities, emergency/medical advice, and release readiness.
- The recreation gold set contains three NPS public references for planning,
  responsible recreation, and health/safety topics. It is federal-park context,
  not a hobby catalog or sport-specific authority.
- The creative gold set contains Creative Commons license metadata, W3C
  standards references, and Library of Congress primary-source learning. It
  proves public-reference delivery, not a creative-practice ontology.
- The volunteering gold set contains AmeriCorps civic/volunteer-rate data and
  explicitly rejects current opportunity availability or personalized
  placement.
- Foundry already has a Wikidata entity-crosswalk adapter and a CC0 terms
  decision. Its current use is generic crosswalk discovery, not a reviewed
  hobby/life-path release.
- Native Source Atlas provides verified pack, cache, rollback, offline,
  inspection, public-only, and privacy-canary seams. Synthetic path tests prove
  behavior over supplied records, not real coverage or safety classification.

No source/test proves a stable reviewed activity identity set, family/risk
classification, alias/redirect/version handling, domain-overlay eligibility,
community-source limits, cultural/geographic coverage, accessibility facts,
safety completeness, or whether real users find the catalog invitational
rather than judgmental.

## Evidence

All external sources below were accessed on 2026-08-04.

### Broad structured discovery: Wikidata

- Wikidata's official [licensing policy](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing)
  makes structured data in the main/property/lexeme namespaces available under
  CC0; other wiki-page text uses CC BY-SA. Production ingestion must select
  structured entity data only and not copy explanatory wiki text under a CC0
  assumption.
- The official [data-access guidance](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Data_access)
  supports entity URIs, dumps, APIs, and other access methods, warns that some
  interfaces are not stable, requests responsible rate limiting/User-Agent use,
  and recommends a user-visible correction/report route.
- Wikidata is community-maintained. Entity statements can be unreferenced,
  disputed, deprecated, vandalized, merged, redirected, or revised. A source
  rank and reference improve inspectability but do not turn the graph into a
  safety, legal, rules, qualification, availability, or personal-fit authority.

Wikidata can support broad discovery identity, source labels/aliases,
source-published instance/subclass relationships, and revision-bound links for
a reviewed allowlist. It cannot approve the allowlist, decide product family or
risk, or supply trustworthy instructions merely because a statement exists.

### Cultural and creative starter material

- The Smithsonian [Open Access FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq) says
  assets explicitly designated CC0 may be used, transformed, and shared,
  including for creative projects. Non-CC0 items remain subject to their own
  conditions; third-party, publicity, privacy, trademark, cultural, and ethical
  considerations can still apply. The current Open Access page reports more
  than five million 2D/3D items.
- Smithsonian records retain collection/unit, accession/object identity,
  rights designation, media availability, source locator, and correction
  route. A CC0 metadata record does not imply every linked media asset is CC0
  or culturally appropriate for every transformation.
- The Library of Congress
  [Free to Use and Reuse](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/) portal supplies
  curated thematic sets whose item/collection rights statements must still be
  inspected. The Library's
  [copyright guidance](https://www.loc.gov/legal/understanding-copyright/)
  warns that it often does not own collection rights and that records may be
  incomplete or inaccurate; source-specific rights govern reuse.
- The Library's [MADS/RDF documentation](https://www.loc.gov/standards/mads/rdf/)
  shows that authority/subject labels are descriptions in a knowledge-
  organization system, distinct from the real-world object. This is a useful
  semantic warning: a controlled subject label does not define a hobby,
  progression, or personal identity.

Smithsonian/Library sources can provide optional, rights-qualified materials
for exploration and making. They do not decide which activity a person should
choose, what it requires, or whether an item may be transformed beyond its
exact rights/cultural constraints.

### Recreation, rules, safety, and current conditions

- NPS [Recreate Responsibly](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/recreate-responsibly.htm)
  provides federal park planning, respect, safety, and activity-topic context.
  It tells visitors to check closures, fire restrictions, weather, permits,
  rules, equipment, and alternatives. That proves a generic activity identity
  is insufficient for a real outing.
- The current NPS [Trip Planning Guide](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/healthandsafety/trip-planning-guide.htm)
  distinguishes day/overnight guidance and says multi-day/backcountry trips
  require additional planning. Generic federal guidance cannot become
  emergency advice or override site, weather, state, equipment, instructor,
  health, or activity-specific authority.
- The federal Recreation Information Database
  [RIDB](https://ridb.recreation.gov/) supplies public recreation areas,
  facilities, activities, tours, and permits, but states agency data may be
  incomplete. Its [API access agreement](https://ridb.recreation.gov/access-agreement-ridb)
  is revocable and governs API use. Current area/facility/permit availability
  therefore belongs to the fast-changing opportunity initiative, not this
  slow catalog.

NPS references may be an independent general safety/context overlay.
Safety-bearing activity recommendation eligibility still requires a separate
complete claim set from the applicable activity/site/legal/provider authorities.

### Service and community life paths

- AmeriCorps' current civic-engagement and volunteering releases use a bounded
  survey methodology and are descriptive population evidence, not a current
  opportunity directory or placement decision.
- The live repository already retains AmeriCorps volunteer-rate/civic data with
  a biennial posting context and non-claims for availability, eligibility,
  legal advice, personalized placement, and universal coverage.

Service/community pursuit identity can appear in broad discovery, but an actual
organization, role, safeguarding requirement, eligibility rule, schedule,
location, or opening belongs to current-authority/provider sources. Population
participation rates must not rank communities or pressure the user.

### Existing four-family authority pilot

The approved hobby recommendation Research already tested photography,
birdwatching, Toastmasters, and beginner sailing. Its findings remain valid:

- Scouting can own its current youth-program requirements, not an adult hobby
  taxonomy or the user's proficiency.
- Cornell can own its courses and eBird workflow, not the user's interest or a
  public-contribution decision.
- Toastmasters can own its membership program and paths, not confidence,
  friendship, or leadership outcomes.
- US Sailing, a listed provider, the Coast Guard, site authority, and weather
  sources own different parts of beginner sailing; no one generic source owns
  them all.

Those sources are evidence for claim-specific authority. Their rights,
release identity, geographic/program scope, current provider state, and safety
completeness are not sufficient for automatic inclusion in the first production
catalog.

### Completed production-shape pilot

This Research completed a record-shape pilot without redistributing source
bytes:

1. **Wikidata identity:** an entity can retain QID, revision/dump, language
   label/aliases, redirect/merge state, reviewed statements/references, and an
   Ambitions eligibility record. Unreviewed graph edges remain unavailable.
2. **Creative material:** a Smithsonian object can retain record/media rights
   separately so CC0 metadata never upgrades a restricted asset.
3. **Knowledge material:** a Library themed-set item can retain its exact
   rights statement and collection context rather than inheriting a portal-wide
   “free” label.
4. **Recreation overlay:** NPS planning guidance can attach as a general federal
   reference without completing activity/site safety.
5. **Service overlay:** an AmeriCorps population statistic can remain a
   time/geography/method-bound observation rather than a service opportunity or
   normative participation target.

The pilot rejects a universal row and a transitive graph. Identity, editorial
eligibility, materials, general safety context, civic statistics, rules,
providers, and opportunities remain independently eligible claim families.

### Coverage and quality implications

Coverage must report:

- reviewed possibility identities by family, language, geography/cultural
  scope, risk, source revision, and eligibility;
- included/rejected/ambiguous/merged/deprecated/unreviewed source entities and
  graph relations;
- starter material coverage by exact item/media rights and cultural/ethical
  review state;
- general recreation and civic overlays separately from activity completeness;
- aliases/subdisciplines and known overlaps without declaring identity;
- missing equipment, cost, space, access, season, provider, safety, legal,
  certification, and local availability facts;
- stale, conflicting, withdrawn, rights-blocked, unsupported, unsafe-to-
  generalize, and unavailable records; and
- evaluation for authority, unsafe omission, privacy, accessibility, dignity,
  cultural breadth, productivity framing, correction, offline use, and user
  comprehension.

High catalog counts do not prove usefulness, diversity, cultural breadth, or
safety. Each downstream consumer must request an exact eligible purpose.

### Privacy, sensitive inference, and dignity

- Public collection is driven by fixed source/dump/release IDs, never private
  interest, location, capability, health, relationships, or schedule.
- Viewing, dismissing, or selecting an activity must not update a hidden
  interest/personality profile or remote popularity signal.
- Family and experience facets are catalog organization, not identity labels
  for the user. A practice may overlap multiple facets; ambiguity stays visible.
- The catalog cannot infer health/disability suitability, religion, politics,
  sexuality, relationships, ethnicity, finances, age, or location from an
  activity. Sources that reveal or target protected context require separate
  purpose-specific review.
- A capability overlap does not make an activity more worthy. The user retains
  the right to be a beginner, rest, play, keep a practice private, avoid
  monetization, and reject every suggestion.
- Historical/community classifications can contain cultural and colonial bias.
  Editorial review must preserve source labels/lineage while declining harmful
  or demeaning product language; changes remain inspectable.

## Alternatives

### 1. Treat Wikidata as the universal hobby ontology

This provides broad multilingual coverage and CC0 rights. It fails because the
graph is community-maintained, transitive relations overreach, hobby membership
is subjective, and it has no claim-specific safety/rules/fit authority.

### 2. Build an Ambitions-authored universal taxonomy

Editorial control improves clarity but makes Ambitions the unearned authority
over culture, play, and the good life. A small facet system may organize the
catalog; it cannot assert universal identity or hierarchy.

### 3. Use provider and community directories as the corpus

This improves actionable breadth but mixes current commercial availability,
location, accounts, moderation, safety, and fast-changing terms into the slow
catalog. It belongs to current-opportunity and integration owners.

### 4. Launch only NPS recreation references

This reuses a verified live pack but supplies safety context rather than broad
discovery and overweights federal outdoor recreation.

### 5. Launch a reviewed source-native possibility catalog with overlays

Use a finite reviewed Wikidata identity allowlist, Ambitions editorial
eligibility, rights-qualified cultural starter materials, and independent NPS/
AmeriCorps overlays. Restrict recommendation eligibility to low-risk creative/
making and knowledge/collecting until social/current-provider and physical/
safety completeness are separately proven. This is recommended.

## Unknowns and risks

### Resolved product decisions

- No universal hobby or life-path taxonomy is created. The shared organization
  layer is a non-exclusive facet set plus source-native relations.
- Wikidata supports reviewed discovery identity only, not rules, safety,
  progression, proficiency, or personal fit.
- The first recommendation-eligible catalog remains low-risk creative/making
  and knowledge/collecting, consistent with approved v1 Scope.
- Social/community/service and physical/safety-bearing identities may be
  inspection/discovery-only until exact current-provider/safeguarding or
  safety/legal/site evidence passes their own eligibility.
- Cultural material is record/media-rights qualified; no portal-wide rights
  inheritance.
- Current opportunities, providers, prices, equipment availability, locations,
  conditions, permits, schedules, and memberships are separate faster-clock
  facts.
- A possibility record is public reference and never a user interest, Goal,
  Life Area, Capability, Proof, or Life Branch.

### Material risks

- **Editorial bias:** a finite reviewed allowlist may reflect maintainer culture,
  language, class, geography, ability, or internet visibility.
- **Graph drift/vandalism:** community entity revisions/merges can corrupt
  labels or relations; release review and LKG are mandatory.
- **False safety:** a low-risk label may miss tools, allergens, environment,
  travel, legal, age, or accessibility hazards. The catalog must limit what the
  label establishes and allow stricter per-record review.
- **Rights/cultural harm:** CC0 legal status does not settle trademark,
  publicity, privacy, cultural sensitivity, sacred/community stewardship, or
  ethical reuse.
- **Productivity pressure:** source materials, skill links, streaks, or public
  outcomes can turn play into obligation or resume work.
- **Category identity:** facets may be mistaken for personality labels or a
  hierarchy of valuable leisure.
- **Coverage illusion:** many identities with thin sources may appear more
  complete than a small well-supported catalog.
- **Currentness split:** programs, providers, rules, safety, and opportunities
  change faster than discovery identities.
- **Storage/search:** multilingual labels, source relations, and cultural
  material can exceed device budgets; starter media must remain optional and
  rights-safe.

### Dependencies

- `public-reference-knowledge-foundation` supplies public claim, rights,
  freshness, conflict, offline, inspection, and public-firewall contracts.
- `intelligence-quality-safety-evaluation` supplies grounding, unsafe omission,
  privacy, dignity/bias, accessibility, coverage, and regression evidence.
- `cross-taxonomy-relationship-authority` owns any relationship from a
  possibility to capabilities, occupations, education, or other taxonomies
  beyond the source's exact reviewed relation.
- `current-opportunity-availability-intelligence` owns providers, clubs,
  programs, events, roles, availability, cost, location, conditions, permits,
  rules, and current safety/gate facts.
- Hobby recommendations consume only eligible immutable projections and own
  private local reasons/session behavior.
- `intelligence-change-management` later owns promotion/impact policy across
  source/editorial releases, not source semantic truth.

### Evidence required before Scope

Research has enough evidence to Scope a bounded production catalog if Scope
requires:

1. a finite versioned reviewed source-entity allowlist rather than arbitrary
   graph queries;
2. exact CC0 structured-data extraction and exclusion of other wiki text/media;
3. non-exclusive editorial facets and per-record risk/eligibility/non-claims;
4. no transitive relation closure or name-similarity identity merge;
5. exact item/media rights and cultural/ethical state for starter materials;
6. independent NPS/AmeriCorps overlays that cannot complete activity safety or
   current opportunity claims;
7. low-risk creative/knowledge recommendation eligibility only;
8. fixed public refresh identities, per-claim freshness/rights, atomic
   promotion, rollback, invalidation, purge, offline, and inspection;
9. coverage/evaluation over cultural breadth, accessibility, unsafe omission,
   editorial bias, quiet failure, and productivity framing; and
10. explicit exclusion of personal profiles, current providers/opportunities,
    hard safety/instruction, recommendation, mutation, and external action.

No v1 runtime evidence is required to define the public identity/rights
contract. Social/community and safety-bearing recommendation eligibility must
wait for current-provider/safeguarding and safety-authority evidence. Direct-
user usefulness remains a recommendation evaluation obligation.

## Recommended direction

Scope a federated, source-native possibility catalog with:

- a fixed release-pinned Wikidata structured-data allowlist for identity,
  labels/aliases, reviewed direct relations, revisions, redirects, and source
  references;
- a separate Ambitions editorial record for inclusion, non-exclusive family
  facets, risk class, eligible claim families, non-claims, evidence, and change
  lineage;
- rights-qualified Smithsonian Open Access and Library of Congress starter
  materials as optional inspiration/inspection, not activity authority;
- independent existing NPS general-recreation and AmeriCorps civic-statistics
  overlays; and
- local immutable projections with explicit missing current-provider,
  opportunity, safety, equipment, cost, location, and accessibility facts.

The first consumer-eligible release should admit only reviewed low-risk
creative/making and knowledge/collecting identities. Social/community/service
and physical/safety-bearing identities can be visible as inspection-only
possibilities with incomplete claim sets, but cannot drive hobby recommendations
until their stricter evidence passes.

Use Source Atlas verification, public cache, quarantine, last-known-good,
offline, revocation, and inspection infrastructure. Public acquisition stays
independent of users. Local Planning may later compare an eligible public
projection with explicitly permitted private context, but the corpus receives
no private input or feedback.
