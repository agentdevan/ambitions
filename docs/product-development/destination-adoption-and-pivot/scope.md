## Outcome

A user can consider a possible life direction without accidentally turning it
into a Goal, and can deliberately adopt it only after seeing what commitment is
being created. When the user chooses a genuinely different destination, the
new outcome becomes a separate provisional Goal while the old Goal retains its
identity, history, Proof, and honest lifecycle meaning.

The experience makes four states unmistakable: an advisory candidate, a saved
direction held without pressure, a user-owned provisional Goal, and an active
Goal with an accepted route. Adoption crosses only the candidate-to-provisional-
Goal boundary. It never accepts a generated route, creates Steps, schedules
work, or certifies external eligibility by implication.

For a changed-destination pivot, the user sees what progress remains personally
useful, what only appears relevant, what needs review, and what a receiving
authority alone can accept. The user chooses what happens to the old Goal. The
product never edits the old outcome into the new one or describes changing
one's mind as failure.

## In scope

- Inspection of a locally produced or user-entered destination candidate,
  including its exact rationale, evidence categories, uncertainty, source and
  freshness where public facts are cited, and why it is only a possibility.
- Candidate actions to keep for later, edit the proposed outcome, dismiss the
  exact candidate, correct its rationale, or begin deliberate adoption.
- A dormant saved-direction state that creates no Goal, Goal Path, Step, Proof
  rule, placement, deadline, or execution pressure.
- Duplicate and relationship review against equivalent saved directions and
  existing provisional, active, paused, ended, or archived Goals.
- Explicit creation of one typed provisional Goal shell from a reviewed outcome,
  preserving the user's wording and the candidate-to-Goal lineage.
- A changed-destination review that preserves the old Goal and creates a
  separately identified new outcome rather than rewriting Goal identity.
- User choice for the old Goal: keep it as-is, pause it, end it through honest
  Closure, or cancel the pivot. Completion and archive remain available only
  when their existing lifecycle preconditions are independently satisfied.
- Reviewable links from old Proof, capability evidence, completed work, and
  relevant context to the new Goal without copying the source or claiming that
  a new requirement is satisfied.
- Local, offline, private, accessible cancellation, interruption recovery,
  truthful Receipts/History, and safe reversal through existing owners.

## Out of scope

- Generating a Goal Path, comparing same-outcome routes, selecting route nodes,
  creating canonical Steps, choosing Proof requirements, or scheduling work.
- Editing an existing Goal's desired outcome in place to represent a different
  destination.
- Treating a same-outcome degree, role, preparation sequence, Step order, or
  schedule change as a destination pivot.
- Automatically creating or activating a Goal from a recommendation, dormant
  direction, capability overlap, inactivity, missed work, or model score.
- Determining that the user is eligible, qualified, employable, admissible,
  licensable, medically fit, or likely to succeed.
- Automatic transfer, copying, completion credit, equivalency, or receiver
  acceptance for old Proof, capabilities, credentials, or experience.
- Destination discovery, recommendation ranking, public pathway ingestion,
  or a universal career, education, or hobby taxonomy.
- A general Life Branch or alternate-life simulator. Cross-object all-or-none
  correction belongs to Life Branch only when its stricter threshold is met.
- A new top-level destination, social profile, public aspiration graph, or
  hosted private-life recommendation service.

## Requirements

### REQ-001 — Candidates remain advisory

A destination candidate must state that it is a possibility, not a Goal or a
prediction. It must show the proposed outcome, why it appeared, the local input
categories used, uncertainty, and every cited public source's authority,
region, retrieval date, and freshness. Capability overlap may explain adjacency
but must not be presented as desire, eligibility, or selection authority.

### REQ-002 — Exploration does not require commitment

The user must be able to keep a candidate for later as a dormant direction
without creating a Goal or execution obligation. The saved direction must
remain inspectable, editable, archivable, restorable, and dismissible, and must
say that no Goal, route, Step, Proof requirement, or Time placement exists.

### REQ-003 — Candidate controls have bounded meaning

The user may edit a candidate, keep it for later, dismiss it, correct its
rationale, or start adoption. Dismissal applies only to the exact candidate,
rationale, evidence basis, and review context. A broader exclusion requires a
separate explicit choice that states its scope, remains inspectable and
resettable, and never becomes a personality or permanent-interest inference.
Editing the proposed outcome must re-evaluate its rationale, source claims,
duplicate relationships, and evidence links; unsupported lineage cannot carry
over to the edited meaning.
Unchanged dismissed content may return only after the user requests review or
materially changed outcome, rationale, or source facts create a new basis.

### REQ-004 — Adoption is an explicit Goal boundary

Before adoption, the product must show the exact outcome and Life Area that
will become user-owned, identify duplicate or related directions, and state
that only a provisional Goal shell will be created. Explicit confirmation must
create exactly one provisional Goal through the Goal owner, retain original
intent and candidate lineage, and produce truthful Receipt, History, and replay
results. Route generation and scheduling remain later choices.

### REQ-005 — Duplicates are reviewed without silent merging

If the candidate may match a saved direction or existing Goal, the user must
see the relevant identities and choose to open the existing item, relate the
candidate, refine the new outcome, or continue with a distinct provisional
Goal. Label similarity must never silently merge, replace, reactivate, or
duplicate a canonical Goal.

### REQ-006 — Changed destination creates a new identity

A changed-destination pivot occurs only when the user explicitly says the
desired outcome is changing. The old Goal must remain historically intact and
the new outcome must receive a distinct provisional Goal identity. A different
route toward the same outcome must leave this flow and return to Goal Path
generation or comparison.

### REQ-007 — The user owns the old Goal decision

The pivot review must let the user keep the old Goal unchanged, pause it, end
it through honest Closure, or cancel before creating the new Goal. The product
must show the consequence of each available choice and must not infer
completion, archive, deletion, or abandonment. Archive may be offered only as a
separate existing-owner action after an eligible completed or ended state.

### REQ-008 — Progress continuity does not overclaim transfer

The review must distinguish: retained personal evidence; apparent relevance to
the new outcome; possible support for a sourced condition; recognition by a
named policy; and formal acceptance by an external authority. Old work remains
owned by its original Goal, Step, Proof, capability, Receipt, or History Event.
The user may approve relationships to the new Goal, but adoption must not copy
evidence, mark new work complete, or assert equivalency or acceptance.

### REQ-009 — Uncertain and stale facts fail closed

Missing, stale, contradictory, protected, or authority-sensitive facts must be
shown as unknown or needing review. They cannot block the user's right to adopt
an outcome as intent, but the product must not convert them into eligibility,
readiness, requirement satisfaction, or a current route claim.

### REQ-010 — Adoption and old-Goal changes settle honestly

Each accepted mutation must use its canonical owner and expose its own result.
The product must not claim that a pivot is complete while the provisional Goal
creation or selected old-Goal lifecycle change is blocked, failed, or stale.
If the user requires several changes to succeed as one all-or-none decision,
the flow must stop and hand off to Life Branch assessment rather than perform
an opaque partial multi-owner mutation.

### REQ-011 — Cancellation, interruption, and reversal preserve truth

Cancellation before adoption creates no Goal. Interruption after candidate
review restores the exact candidate, edits, duplicate decision, transfer
choices, and review position after revalidating changed facts. Reversing a
provisional Goal creation or old-Goal lifecycle decision uses the owning
recovery action, preserves Receipt/History lineage, and never deletes genuine
later work or external consequences.

### REQ-012 — Private direction remains local

Candidates, saved directions, rationale, capability links, Goal relationships,
pivot decisions, and recommendation history are private local graph data and
must work without account or network. They must not be sent to Account, R2,
Source Atlas, hosted AI, telemetry, or an external profile. A suggestion whose
output or rationale exposes protected context must remain quiet; user-entered
protected intent may be stored locally but cannot silently broaden inference.

### REQ-013 — Language preserves agency

The experience must use calm, specific language and must not expose a
compatibility, employability, success, potential, or life-direction score. Low
overlap, dismissal, pause, ending, or non-transferability must not be described
as failure, waste, inability, or evidence that the user should not aspire.

### REQ-014 — The full decision is accessible

Candidate meaning, rationale, uncertainty, source state, duplicate
relationships, old-Goal choices, transfer distinctions, confirmation scope,
results, and recovery must have an ordered semantic representation. Every
action must have a labeled non-gesture path for VoiceOver, Voice Control,
Switch Control, and Full Keyboard Access. Dynamic Type, increased contrast,
reduced motion, and non-color indicators must preserve meaning; focus and
announcements must return to the exact changed item or recovery action.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-001 (REQ-001):** A capability-informed candidate shows why it appeared,
   its uncertainty and source status, and explicitly says it is not a Goal,
   eligibility decision, or predicted outcome.
2. **AC-002 (REQ-002):** Choosing Keep for later preserves an inspectable dormant
   direction and creates no Goal, Path, Step, Proof rule, placement, or deadline.
3. **AC-003 (REQ-003):** Dismissing a candidate suppresses only its exact basis;
   an unrelated aspiration remains available, a broader exclusion requires a
   separate scoped choice, and reset/requested review restores eligibility. An
   edited outcome loses rationale, source, or evidence lineage that no longer
   supports its meaning.
4. **AC-004 (REQ-004):** Confirming adoption creates one provisional Goal with
   the reviewed outcome, Life Area, original-intent lineage, Receipt, History,
   and replay result while route and Time remain unchanged.
5. **AC-005 (REQ-005):** An equivalent active Goal is surfaced before adoption,
   and each user choice opens, relates, refines, or creates distinctly without a
   silent merge or duplicate activation.
6. **AC-006 (REQ-006):** Changing only a preparation route exits to the Goal Path
   flow and preserves Goal identity; changing the desired outcome preserves the
   old Goal and creates a distinct provisional Goal.
7. **AC-007 (REQ-007):** Pivot review offers keep, pause, truthful Ended Closure,
   and cancel for the old Goal; it never labels the Goal completed or archived
   without satisfying the existing owner law.
8. **AC-008 (REQ-008):** Reusable Proof and capability evidence can be related to
   the new Goal while the source IDs and histories remain unchanged and no new
   requirement or completion is marked satisfied.
9. **AC-009 (REQ-009):** Stale source facts and missing personal facts appear as
   unknown/review-needed while the user may still adopt the outcome without an
   eligibility or readiness claim.
10. **AC-010 (REQ-010):** Blocked provisional creation or old-Goal mutation is
    reported at its exact boundary; the UI never reports a completed pivot, and
    an explicitly all-or-none cross-owner request is handed to Life Branch
    assessment.
11. **AC-011 (REQ-011):** Cancel, interruption/resume, retry, and safe reversal
    preserve candidate edits, canonical identities, and truthful Receipt/History
    without duplicating a later retry.
12. **AC-012 (REQ-012):** The complete flow works offline and privacy-egress
    tests show no private candidate, rationale, Goal, or relationship payload at
    a prohibited destination; protected derived suggestions remain quiet.
13. **AC-013 (REQ-013):** Low-overlap aspiration, dismissal, pause, ending, and
    irrelevant transfer fixtures contain no scoring, destiny, shame, or ability
    judgment.
14. **AC-014 (REQ-014):** Direct assistive-technology verification proves the
    ordered decision, every action, focus restoration, announcements, Dynamic
    Type, increased contrast, reduced motion, and non-color state parity.

## Dependencies

- Approved `capability-continuity-foundation` is optional input, never
  destination authority.
- `career-destination-recommendations`,
  `education-destination-recommendations`, and
  `hobby-destination-recommendations` may supply candidates only under REQ-001
  through REQ-003 after their own Scope and dependencies are approved.
- Existing Goal, Closure, Proof, History, Receipt, privacy, runtime, and
  accessibility contracts own all canonical mutations and evidence.
- `goal-path-generation` begins only after a provisional Goal exists.
- `life-branch-reconciliation` is a handoff only for a user-required complete
  cross-owner all-or-none correction; ordinary adoption and a single Goal
  lifecycle choice remain here.

## Canon impact

- Goal canon and the Goal-creation-and-activation journey should explicitly own
  candidate promotion into one provisional Goal without route acceptance.
- A canonical pre-Goal saved-direction contract is required because source-level
  North Star behavior is not yet normative; Design may choose the smallest
  owner but may not give it Goal identity or execution state.
- Goals surface canon should own candidate inspection, duplicate review, and
  changed-destination handoff while preserving same-outcome Goal Path ownership.
- Closure, Proof, History, Receipt, local-learning, privacy, and accessibility
  canon retain their existing meanings and need only the relationship and
  presentation consequences introduced here.
- This Scope creates no new root surface and does not change Goal Path, Time, or
  Life Branch ownership.

## Risks and open decisions

Resolved product decisions:

- Saved exploration is distinct from Goal creation; adoption creates only a
  provisional Goal.
- A changed outcome creates a new Goal identity. A same-outcome route change
  does not.
- The old Goal choice is explicit and never inferred as completion or failure.
- Evidence is related, not copied, and personal continuity is distinct from
  external acceptance.
- Generic candidate dismissal is exact and bounded; broader exclusions are
  deliberate, inspectable, and resettable.
- Cross-owner all-or-none requests are assessed by Life Branch rather than
  hidden inside a pivot action.

Remaining Design-level risks are candidate/Goal vocabulary comprehension,
duplicate-language ambiguity, high-consequence source staleness, sensitive
derived outputs, and interruption across separately owned mutations. Design
must make those states legible without adding implementation ceremony or
inventing a recommendation score.
