## Outcome

For an already adopted Goal, the user receives an understandable candidate
route that explains how the same desired outcome could become real. The route
shows ordered stages, dependencies, sourced requirements, assumptions,
unknowns, optional preparation, selection points, and Proof expectations
without pretending that a generated outline is accepted truth.

The generator owns creation and review of one route proposal. It does not own
the Goal, choose among multiple meaningfully different routes, activate the
canonical Goal Path, create canonical Steps, or place work in Time. If several
viable routes deserve comparison, it hands them to adaptive path comparison;
after one proposal is selected, the Goal Path owner revalidates and controls
any canonical activation.

NASA astronaut candidacy in the United States is the bounded validation case.
It tests whether Ambitions can separate current authority-backed gates,
alternative qualification routes, competitive selection, post-selection
training, and unknown personal facts. It does not create an astronaut-specific
product or establish coverage for every career, education, hobby, or license.

## In scope

- Generation of a local, side-effect-free candidate route for one provisional,
  ready, or active Goal whose desired outcome is already user-owned.
- One-route review with ordered stages, dependencies, requirements, options,
  assumptions, risks, source/freshness state, and safe next clarification.
- Explicit distinction among hard gates, prerequisites, optional strengthening,
  common preparation, official substitutions, competitive selection,
  post-selection training, continuing requirements, and user-controlled work.
- Separate presentation of public route facts, user-confirmed starting facts,
  capability/Proof evidence, resources, constraints, availability, and unknowns.
- Source-bounded and plainly generic degraded results when current authority or
  private starting context is insufficient.
- Editing, omitting, rejecting, deferring, or regenerating a candidate without
  canonical mutation.
- A handoff of meaningfully different same-outcome candidates to adaptive path
  comparison and of one reviewed proposal to the canonical Goal Path boundary.
- Offline, local, private, accessible review, stale-state recovery, and manual
  continuation.

## Out of scope

- Recommending or adopting a destination, changing the Goal outcome, or creating
  a Goal from an aspiration.
- Selecting among several meaningfully different routes; this initiative may
  identify the need for comparison but cannot choose the winner.
- Activating or replacing a canonical Goal Path, committing canonical Steps,
  accepting Proof rules, changing Goal lifecycle, or scheduling placements.
- Claiming personal eligibility, requirement satisfaction, equivalency,
  admission, licensing, hiring, medical fitness, selection, promotion, flight
  assignment, or success probability.
- Treating a common sequence, intermediate role, course, credential, or public
  reference node as a mandatory Goal or promotion ladder.
- Hosted personalized path generation or sending private Goal context to R2,
  Source Atlas, Account, telemetry, or an external model.
- Universal public-source ingestion, cross-taxonomy equivalence, real-time
  provider/opening availability, or broad domain coverage.
- A second Goal Path identity, parallel active paths, Life Branch, or general
  scenario engine.

## Requirements

### REQ-001 — Generation starts from adopted intent

The generator may run only for a stable user-owned Goal outcome. It must show
the Goal identity and exact outcome being planned and must stop for destination
adoption if the requested route would change that outcome. A provisional Goal
must survive generation failure or rejection with its original intent intact.

### REQ-002 — The output is a non-canonical proposal

Every generated route must be labeled as a candidate simulation. Generation,
editing, omission, regeneration, rejection, and deferral must leave the Goal,
current Goal Path, Steps, Proof, Closure, placements, and Time unchanged. The
candidate cannot drive execution before canonical Goal Path confirmation.

### REQ-003 — One-route review has a complete semantic outline

A candidate must show ordered stages, current or assumed starting position,
dependencies, each material requirement's kind, optional items, alternative
conditions, decision or selection points, Proof expectations, resource needs,
risks, assumptions, unknowns, and the next useful clarification. Every item
must state whether it is user-controlled, authority-controlled, or merely
informational.

### REQ-004 — Route meanings remain distinct

The product must distinguish a hard gate from preparation, a prerequisite from
optional strengthening, an official substitution from a similar capability,
a current opening from a theoretical route, an intermediate-role option from
a required promotion, competitive selection from a user milestone,
post-selection training from applicant preparation, and a proposed Step from a
canonical Step. It must never flatten alternatives into one mandatory ladder.

### REQ-005 — Sourced claims expose authority and freshness

Every externally grounded gate, substitution, availability statement, and
route distinction must identify the authority, claim, region/program, version
or effective context, retrieval date, freshness, and uncertainty. Generic
taxonomy relationships may supply discovery context but cannot impersonate a
current provider, employer, school, issuer, licensing board, or program rule.

### REQ-006 — Private starting facts remain separately confirmed

User capabilities, Proof, credentials, experience, resources, location,
constraints, and user-chosen sensitive facts must remain distinct inputs. An
absent fact is unknown, not unmet. A capability label or Proof link cannot by
itself satisfy a sourced gate, establish equivalency, or authorize a
professional determination.

### REQ-007 — Assumptions are bounded and correctable

The route may proceed with a non-material assumption only when the assumption,
reason, affected stages, and consequence of being wrong are visible and the
user can correct or reject it. Ambiguity that changes the destination, hard
gate, applicable authority, route identity, or protected context must block a
domain-backed candidate and ask one necessary neutral question.

### REQ-008 — Degraded behavior remains useful and honest

With current public route facts but insufficient private context, Ambitions may
show the sourced route envelope and ask for clarification, but must not claim a
personal gap or readiness. With a stale, contradictory, unavailable, or
inapplicable material authority claim, it must identify the missing fact and
may offer only a plainly labeled generic starter outline or manual continuation.
The degraded result must not inherit domain authority.

### REQ-009 — NASA validation preserves authority boundaries

For NASA astronaut candidacy, the route must distinguish citizenship,
qualifying education alternatives, experience or pilot-route alternatives, the
flight-physical gate, leadership/teamwork/communication statements,
application-cycle availability, competitive selection, candidate training, and
flight-assignment eligibility according to current cited NASA claims. Unknown
user facts remain unknown; selection, the physical, training, and assignment
must not become user-completable milestones or forecasts.

### REQ-010 — User review controls the candidate

The user must be able to inspect sources and assumptions, correct starting
facts, edit user-controlled wording or preparation, omit an optional proposal,
reject the route, keep it for later, request a generic/manual alternative, or
regenerate from corrected facts. Editing cannot relabel an authority-owned gate
or retain a source citation for meaning the source does not support.

### REQ-011 — Multiple meaningful routes hand off to comparison

If generation produces two or more candidates that differ in a material route
consequence rather than wording or timeslot, the generator must not designate a
best route. It must preserve each candidate's source and assumption state and
offer handoff to adaptive path comparison. One-route review may continue only
for a user-selected focus candidate without making that focus canonical.

### REQ-012 — Goal Path activation remains a separate owner boundary

A reviewed candidate may be sent to the Goal Path owner as one selected
proposal. Before any canonical version is created, that owner must revalidate
Goal identity, source and fact freshness, assumptions, dependencies, current
path revision, and confirmation scope. Route acceptance must separately state
which proposed nodes, Steps, and Proof expectations become accepted;
scheduling remains a later confirmed action.

### REQ-013 — No route or person score becomes authority

The user-facing route must not expose a compatibility, employability,
readiness, ability, success, or best-path score. Confidence and freshness must
be stated categorically and with reasons. Internal ordering must not be
presented as a judgment that the user should choose or can achieve the Goal.

### REQ-014 — Generation remains private and offline

Private Goal meaning, starting facts, capabilities, Proof, constraints,
candidate routes, corrections, and review history must remain local and work
without account or network. Public references may be obtained only without a
private payload. Protected context must not be inferred; professional or
sensitive unknowns fail closed without asking the user to expose them merely to
receive a route.

### REQ-015 — Stale and interrupted review recovers safely

A retained review must preserve candidate identity, edits, omissions, source
revisions, assumptions, review position, and focus. On resume or before handoff,
changed Goal, source, Proof, capability, resource, constraint, or Time facts
must mark affected content stale and require refresh. Failure or cancellation
preserves the Goal and last accepted Goal Path and offers retry, correction,
generic/manual continuation, or return.

### REQ-016 — Route meaning is accessible without a diagram

The full route must have an ordered nonvisual representation exposing stage,
dependency, requirement kind, source/freshness, assumption, unknown, current
position, inclusion state, authority boundary, and actions. VoiceOver, Voice
Control, Switch Control, Full Keyboard Access, Dynamic Type, increased
contrast, reduced motion, and non-color cues must preserve review, edit,
omission, source inspection, rejection, recovery, and handoff. Status changes
must be announced and focus restored to the affected stage or recovery action.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-001 (REQ-001):** Generation for a provisional astronaut-candidacy Goal
   shows that exact outcome; an attempted aerospace-engineering substitution is
   rejected as a destination change and leaves the original Goal intact.
2. **AC-002 (REQ-002):** Generate, edit, reject, defer, and regenerate fixtures
   produce no canonical Goal Path, Step, Proof, placement, or Time mutation.
3. **AC-003 (REQ-003):** A route review exposes every ordered stage,
   dependency, requirement kind, option, source, assumption, unknown, Proof
   expectation, resource need, authority owner, and next clarification.
4. **AC-004 (REQ-004):** Fixtures separately identify hard gate/preparation,
   prerequisite/optional strengthening, substitution/similarity,
   opening/theoretical route, role option/promotion, selection/milestone,
   post-selection training/preparation, and proposed/canonical Step.
5. **AC-005 (REQ-005):** Every NASA-derived claim opens to its exact authority,
   program context, retrieval date, freshness, and uncertainty; a generic pack
   cannot display NASA-backed authority.
6. **AC-006 (REQ-006):** Missing citizenship, education, experience, capability,
   and resource facts remain unknown; Proof overlap marks no gate satisfied and
   makes no equivalency or eligibility claim.
7. **AC-007 (REQ-007):** A harmless visible assumption remains correctable,
   while ambiguous destination, program, hard gate, or protected context blocks
   domain-backed output and produces one neutral clarification.
8. **AC-008 (REQ-008):** Missing private facts yield a sourced route envelope
   without personal gap claims; stale material authority yields an identified
   gap plus only generic/manual continuation with no inherited authority.
9. **AC-009 (REQ-009):** The NASA fixture preserves every named qualification
   alternative and authority-controlled state and never converts selection,
   physical, candidate training, or flight assignment into completable work.
10. **AC-010 (REQ-010):** The user can inspect, correct, edit, omit, reject,
    defer, regenerate, or choose generic/manual continuation; editing a sourced
    gate removes any unsupported source attribution.
11. **AC-011 (REQ-011):** Two materially distinct same-outcome candidates offer
    comparison with no best-route label; wording-only and timeslot-only variants
    do not create comparison candidates.
12. **AC-012 (REQ-012):** A selected proposal produces no mutation until Goal
    Path revalidation and explicit confirmation; accepted node/Step/Proof scope
    is visible and no placement is committed.
13. **AC-013 (REQ-013):** All normal, degraded, blocked, and comparison-handoff
    fixtures contain no user-facing scalar or best/success/ability judgment.
14. **AC-014 (REQ-014):** Local generation and review work offline, public
    reference requests contain no private context, and privacy-egress tests fail
    closed for every prohibited destination.
15. **AC-015 (REQ-015):** Resume after changed source or Goal facts marks only
    affected content stale, preserves edits and focus, and offers refresh,
    generic/manual continuation, or return without altering accepted state.
16. **AC-016 (REQ-016):** Direct assistive-technology verification proves
    ordered route semantics, every action, focus/announcement recovery, Dynamic
    Type, contrast, reduced motion, and non-color parity.

## Dependencies

- A user-owned Goal from existing Goal creation or
  `destination-adoption-and-pivot` is required.
- `public-reference-knowledge-foundation` is required for any authority-backed
  gate, availability, source, or freshness claim. Generic/manual degradation
  may proceed without it and must remain plainly non-authoritative.
- The public-reference foundation's first O*NET Software Developers slice does
  not supply the NASA authority required by REQ-009 and AC-009. Authoritative
  NASA validation therefore depends on a separately approved NASA/domain-corpus
  Research and Scope; until that dependency exists, only the generic/manual
  degraded path may proceed and no NASA-backed acceptance claim may pass.
- Approved capability continuity may supply local evidence but cannot satisfy a
  route requirement on its own.
- `adaptive-path-comparison` owns qualitative selection when multiple
  meaningful same-outcome candidates exist.
- The existing Goal Path owner alone revalidates and activates a selected
  proposal; scheduling remains under Time/placement owners.

## Frontend impact contract

- Surface impact: new-child
- IA/navigation: none
- Assets/iconography: system-only
- Visual language: unchanged
- Motion: unchanged
- Copy/localization: The approved requirements, acceptance criteria, and user flows own visible terminology and non-claims; implementation must localize that meaning without inventing promotional, score, authority, or outcome language.
- Accessibility: Every new child view and action must preserve the approved semantic order, Dynamic Type/reflow, assistive-input parity, non-color meaning, focus, announcements, and reduced-effects behavior.
- Visual proof: One production-intended native fixture and viewport requires owner visual approval before implementation, followed by changed-state runtime, screenshot, accessibility, and named-device evidence required by Verification.

## Canon impact

- Goal Path and Goal-creation-and-activation canon should distinguish candidate
  construction, one-route review, multi-route comparison handoff, and canonical
  activation/revalidation.
- Goal Path strategy canon should own the route semantic distinctions,
  assumption/degraded behavior, and one selected proposal handoff without
  creating a second path identity.
- Public-reference canon should own authority, region, version, retrieval,
  freshness, contradiction, and no-private-query boundaries for route facts.
- Goals surface canon should own accessible ordered review while canonical Goal,
  Step, Proof, Time, Receipt, and History owners retain mutation authority.
- NASA is a validation fixture, not a new canonical product domain or permanent
  source requirement for unrelated Goals.

## Risks and open decisions

Resolved product decisions:

- This initiative generates and reviews one proposal; comparison selects among
  multiple proposals; Goal Path activates the selected result.
- Hard claims require current authority. Missing authority degrades rather than
  fabricates.
- Unknown personal facts remain unknown and are never inferred as gaps.
- Public stages and selection points do not become canonical Steps or Goals by
  generation alone.
- NASA astronaut candidacy is the first proof fixture, not the product boundary.

Remaining Design-level risks are progressive route detail, clarification
burden, source-change presentation, generic-versus-authoritative comprehension,
and readable density. Design may resolve presentation and representation but
must not weaken the degraded-path rule or invent route-selection authority.
