# Scope — Owner Taste Design Governance

Install Owner Taste as the supreme design authority, with a machine peer,
explicit conflict law, normal canon route, compiler guardrails, iPhone-only
scope, a pre-proof taste gate, and physical-iPhone proof routing. Demote the
VC-01–VC-14 input contract and closure packages to subordinate implementation
and calibration material. Generated authority output must expose Owner Taste at
the top level and must not describe VC directions as independent authorities.

Out of scope: production surface redesign, broad frontend authorization, a
second design authority, iPad/Watch work, Figma control, requirements or proof
claims decided by taste, or process-only merge gates. Owner Taste controls all
design conflicts. Product truth, domain semantics, safety, privacy,
correctness, accessibility requirements, platform requirements, and verified
usability limits are non-design boundary conditions. They constrain feasible
implementation without granting Visual Closure or evidence a competing design
direction.
