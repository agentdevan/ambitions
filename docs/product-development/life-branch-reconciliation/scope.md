## Outcome

When one confirmed change makes several already-real parts of the user's life
plan conflict, the user can compare a small set of complete ways forward and
apply one without leaving Goals, Paths, Time, Proof conditions, or protected
commitments in a half-changed state.

Life Branch appears only after Ambitions explains why ordinary Goal, Goal Path,
Recovery Segment, destination-pivot, or Time correction cannot preserve the
user's declared invariants. It coordinates existing owners; it is not a second
private graph, alternate self, recommendation container, or general scenario
tool.

This Scope is bounded to one validation scenario: a user-confirmed permanent
relocation on a known date to assume protected caregiving, while an in-person
credential Goal, an onsite-work Goal, their accepted Paths/placements, and
external enrollment/employment obligations are already real. Candidate review
and local promotion must not claim that a provider, employer, recipient, or
external system accepted any follow-up.

## In scope

- Detection and plain-language explanation of the worked relocation conflict
  from current canonical facts explicitly confirmed by the user.
- A mandatory simpler-owner assessment before Life Branch escalation.
- Two or three bounded, complete, policy-distinct correction candidates when
  current facts support them, plus inspectable blocked or omitted options.
- Review of what each candidate protects, changes, sacrifices, assumes, leaves
  unresolved, and assigns to local, recipient, joint, or external authority.
- Current viability status, source/fact freshness, conflict reasons, and the
  exact facts that would invalidate or repair each candidate.
- Edit, lighter-correction, defer, keep-conflict, reject-all, refresh, and
  explicit selection behaviors.
- Revalidation and one coordinated local promotion for a currently valid
  selected candidate, with external effects kept separate.
- Atomic failure behavior, truthful Receipt/History/replay, bounded rollback or
  compensation, offline privacy, and accessible nonvisual review.

## Out of scope

- A general scenario builder, alternate-life dashboard, copied life graph,
  prediction engine, or always-visible branch workspace.
- Destination discovery, dormant aspirations, recommendation candidates, or a
  single changed Goal outcome that destination adoption/pivot can own.
- Same-outcome route generation/comparison, one Goal Path edit, ordinary
  Recovery Segment, or timeslot-only schedule reflow.
- Creating a branch merely because more than one object is visible or may
  eventually change.
- Hidden ranking, best-life selection, success prediction, or a user score.
- Letting generative AI certify viability, decide authority, authorize a
  mutation, or receive private branch context.
- Claiming remote-program availability, employer agreement, tuition outcome,
  recipient consent, calendar write, admission, hiring, licensing, or any other
  external result.
- Generalizing the relocation validation case to health, finance, legal,
  relationship, or other sensitive domains without separately approved scope.

## Requirements

### REQ-001 — Reconciliation starts from confirmed changed reality

The entry must bind one explicit user-confirmed trigger, current graph revision,
bounded horizon, affected existing objects, protected/fixed conditions,
authority facts, and current conflict. A forecast, inferred life event, model
suggestion, or unconfirmed external claim cannot trigger a branch or mutate
state.

### REQ-002 — Simpler owners are tested first

Before offering Life Branch, Ambitions must inspect whether Goal edit, Goal Path
revision, Recovery Segment, destination adoption/pivot, or Time reflow can
restore an honest graph while preserving declared invariants. It must show the
user which mechanisms were considered and why each is sufficient or
insufficient. If one simpler owner can solve the conflict, that owner receives
the handoff and no Life Branch candidate is created.

### REQ-003 — The Life Branch threshold is conjunctive

Escalation is permitted only when one confirmed trigger creates a revision-bound
conflict; its acceptable correction requires coordinated material changes
across multiple canonical owners; the user declares those changes one all-or-
none confirmation scope; and no independently committable simpler-owner repair
preserves the protected conditions. Merely changing multiple objects or
preferring a grouped preview is insufficient.

### REQ-004 — The worked scenario remains the product boundary

The first experience must apply only to the confirmed relocation, caregiving,
in-person credential, onsite-work, protected-Time scenario described above. It
must preserve separate provider and employer authority facts. If an official
remote-work or remote-program fact permits a simpler honest repair, the branch
threshold fails and the user returns to the owning Goal Path or Time flow.

### REQ-005 — Candidates are complete and bounded

The main review may show two or three policy-distinct complete candidates. Each
must account for every affected Goal, current Goal Path, accepted Step/placement,
protected-Time rule, Proof condition, resource or obligation, unresolved fact,
and external follow-up within the bounded horizon. A candidate that omits one
material affected consequence is blocked, not partially viable. Additional
blocked or consequence-equivalent options remain inspectable with suppression
reasons and cannot silently displace a materially distinct candidate.

### REQ-006 — Every candidate explains protection and sacrifice

Each candidate must state in plain language what changed fact it accepts, what
it protects, changes, defers, ends, sacrifices, assumes, costs, leaves
unresolved, and makes reversible or irreversible. The product must not collapse
these value conflicts into a scalar, confidence percentage, or best-branch
label.

### REQ-007 — Authority remains partitioned

Every effect must identify whether the user, another recipient, both jointly,
or an external system owns its outcome. Only user-owned local changes may enter
promotion. Provider application, tuition cancellation, employer agreement,
recipient response, and external calendar operations remain separately labeled
proposals or post-local intents. No branch status or local success may imply
their completion.

### REQ-008 — Viability is current, inspectable, and fail-closed

Each candidate must expose whether it is currently valid, fragile, blocked,
invalid, or stale; the governing graph/source/policy revisions; protected and
relaxable conditions; unresolved questions; and invalidation facts. Only a
current valid candidate may be selected for promotion. Fragile or blocked
candidates may explain repairs but cannot be presented as feasible success.

### REQ-009 — Candidate review remains simulation

Materialization, comparison, editing, refresh, correction, defer, keep-conflict,
reject-all, and selection must be local side-effect-free simulation. Candidate
identity or recovery durability must not duplicate Goals, Paths, Steps,
placements, Proof, Receipts, History, or the private graph and must not create
canonical mutation authority.

### REQ-010 — The user controls review and selection

The user must be able to inspect the current conflict, inspect or edit a
candidate within declared relaxable conditions, choose a lighter correction,
defer, keep the conflict, reject all, refresh facts, or select exactly one valid
candidate. Editing a protected condition requires a separate explicit change
through its owner and recertification; it cannot be silently relaxed inside the
branch.

### REQ-011 — Promotion revalidates and commits locally as one scope

Before confirmation, Ambitions must revalidate the selected candidate against
current graph, source, policy, authority, protected-boundary, materiality, and
viability facts and show the complete local mutation scope plus external intents.
Explicit confirmation may produce only one coordinated local all-or-none
result. It must update existing canonical owners, preserve their identities,
and create truthful Receipt, History, replay, and recovery lineage.

### REQ-012 — Failure never leaves a mixed local state

Stale review, changed authority, invalid candidate, command rejection, conflict,
crash, or interruption before local settlement must preserve the last honest
local graph and report no branch success. Recovery must offer inspect, refresh,
choose another correction, defer, retry safely, or return. External failure
after local success remains a separate visible outcome and cannot roll back or
falsify settled local truth automatically.

### REQ-013 — Rollback respects later reality

Where a typed reversal remains safe, rollback must restore the prior local
branch and affected owners while retaining the selected branch, decision basis,
Receipt, History, and attempted external effects. If subsequent work, external
effects, or another person's decision makes exact reversal unsafe, the product
must offer an explicit compensating recovery and must not pretend the branch
never happened.

### REQ-014 — Revision changes invalidate only declared dependents

When an affected Goal, Path, Step, placement, source, authority, capacity,
policy, or protected rule changes, only candidates and consequences that depend
on that revision should become stale unless completeness itself can no longer
be established. The review must show what changed, what remains valid, and why
revalidation expanded or stayed bounded.

### REQ-015 — Reconciliation remains private and local

Triggers, branches, conflicts, candidate effects, caregiving, location,
employment, education, schedule, Proof, authority, decisions, and recovery
history are private local graph data and must work without account or network.
Public-reference refresh may send no personalized context. Account, R2, Source
Atlas, hosted AI, telemetry, and external systems must not receive a private
branch payload; unknown or unavailable public facts fail closed.

### REQ-016 — The experience recedes when not needed

Life Branch must appear contextually only for a conflict that passes REQ-001
through REQ-004. It must not create a root surface, standing score, hypothetical-
self collection, engagement loop, or pressure to optimize. After resolution,
the affected canonical objects remain the normal places to inspect current
truth; Trust/History may retain decision lineage.

### REQ-017 — Complete alternatives are accessible without a branch diagram

Every candidate must have an ordered semantic representation naming trigger,
state, affected objects, protected and sacrificed conditions, assumptions,
unknowns, authority partitions, local consequence, external intent,
confirmation scope, and recovery. VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control,
Full Keyboard Access, Dynamic Type, increased contrast, reduced motion, RTL,
and non-color cues must support all actions. Focus and announcements must return
to the changed candidate, stale fact, commit result, or recovery action.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-001 (REQ-001):** Only an explicitly confirmed relocation/caregiving
   fact at a known graph revision opens assessment; forecasts and unconfirmed
   provider/employer facts create no branch or mutation.
2. **AC-002 (REQ-002):** Fixtures show Goal, Goal Path, Recovery Segment,
   destination-pivot, and Time checks with reasons; an official remote-work
   repair exits to the simpler owner and creates no branch candidate.
3. **AC-003 (REQ-003):** The worked all-or-none education/work/Time conflict
   passes all four threshold clauses; multi-object but independently repairable
   and grouped-preview-only cases fail the threshold.
4. **AC-004 (REQ-004):** The flow accepts only the bounded relocation scenario
   and refuses to generalize a new sensitive-domain case without separate
   approved scope.
5. **AC-005 (REQ-005):** Review shows two or three complete candidates; omitting
   one Goal, placement, protected rule, obligation, or external follow-up marks
   a candidate blocked, and equivalent/extra options retain visible reasons.
6. **AC-006 (REQ-006):** Every option states protected, changed, deferred,
   ended, sacrificed, assumed, costly, unresolved, and reversible consequences
   without a score, percentage, or best-branch verdict.
7. **AC-007 (REQ-007):** Local promotion cannot report employer, provider,
   tuition, recipient, or external-calendar success; each remains a separately
   owned pending, succeeded, failed, or cancelled result.
8. **AC-008 (REQ-008):** Only a current valid result enables selection;
   fragile, blocked, invalid, stale, and superseded fixtures expose reasons and
   repair but cannot present feasibility or promotion.
9. **AC-009 (REQ-009):** Every pre-confirmation action leaves all canonical
   object bytes unchanged and candidate recovery creates no duplicate owner.
10. **AC-010 (REQ-010):** Inspect, edit-relaxable, lighter correction, defer,
    keep conflict, reject all, refresh, and select-one actions are available;
    protected changes require their owner and recertification.
11. **AC-011 (REQ-011):** A valid selection receives fresh revalidation, exact
    local/external consequence preview, explicit confirmation, one all-or-none
    local result, and Receipt/History/replay lineage across existing IDs.
12. **AC-012 (REQ-012):** Validation failure and crash-at-each-phase fixtures
    preserve the prior local graph with no false success; later external failure
    remains distinct from the settled local result.
13. **AC-013 (REQ-013):** Safe rollback restores prior local state and retains
    lineage; a fixture with later work or external effect offers compensation
    instead of destructive reversal.
14. **AC-014 (REQ-014):** Changing one dependency stales only its impact cone,
    while loss of completeness conservatively stales the whole candidate with
    an inspectable reason.
15. **AC-015 (REQ-015):** Review, selection, local commit, replay, and recovery
    work offline; privacy-egress tests prove no private branch payload reaches a
    prohibited destination or public-reference request.
16. **AC-016 (REQ-016):** A passing conflict offers contextual reconciliation;
    a non-qualifying or resolved conflict shows no persistent branch workspace,
    root destination, score, or engagement prompt.
17. **AC-017 (REQ-017):** Direct assistive-technology verification proves the
    full ordered alternative, every action, focus/announcement recovery,
    Dynamic Type, contrast, reduced motion, RTL, and non-color parity.

## Dependencies

- Existing normative Life Branch, Branch Viability Certificate, certified
  reconciliation, runtime transaction, Goal, Goal Path, Step, placement, Proof,
  History, Receipt, scheduling, privacy, and accessibility contracts govern.
- Current source has no complete Life Branch implementation; this Scope creates
  no implementation-completeness claim.
- `destination-adoption-and-pivot`, `goal-path-generation`, and
  `adaptive-path-comparison` remain the simpler owners for their exact cases.
- Fresh provider/employer facts are conditional inputs. Their absence produces
  fragile/blocked candidates, never inferred external truth.

## Canon impact

- Existing Life Branch object, Branch Viability Certificate, certified
  reconciliation system, and Life Branch journey already own most normative
  behavior; implementation should refine them with the bounded necessity test
  and worked-scenario product language rather than create another branch type.
- Goal, Goal Path, Step, Time/placement, Proof, Receipt, and History canon retain
  identity and mutation authority; Life Branch coordinates typed changes only.
- Goals, Today, Time, and You/Trust surface canon should own contextual entry,
  consequence presentation, current results, and lineage without a fifth root.
- Privacy and accessibility canon require direct proof for the sensitive,
  multi-owner review and recovery states.

## Risks and open decisions

Resolved product decisions:

- Life Branch uses the conjunctive necessity threshold, not a multi-object count.
- The first product boundary is the confirmed relocation/work/education/
  caregiving case, not a general scenario engine.
- Review shows two or three complete policy-distinct candidates and keeps
  blocked/equivalent omissions inspectable.
- Only current valid candidates may promote; no model certifies or chooses.
- Promotion is one local all-or-none result; external outcomes remain separate.
- Exact rollback yields to compensation when later reality makes reversal unsafe.

Remaining Design-level risks are explaining completeness and viability without
diagnostic theater, showing dense cross-object consequences accessibly, bounding
candidate generation, and preserving calm recovery during stale or failed
promotion. Design may resolve presentation and representation but cannot relax
the threshold, authority partition, or atomic local consequence.
