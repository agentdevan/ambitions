Status: **awaiting owner decision**

**Approve the recommended nine-capability set.**

- **Approve with named edits:** provide exact identity/name changes; aliases and source provenance remain preserved.

## Decision 2 — Skill Transference scope

### Question

Should Skill Transference remain a standalone flagship capability or become a named Local Learning subcapability?

### Recommendation

**Standalone flagship capability.**

This keeps the person-facing promise visible and independently traceable. Local Learning remains the enabling system and does not absorb the product identity.

### Alternative consequence

A Local Learning subcapability produces a smaller atlas but risks allowing transfer to disappear into implementation behavior.

---

## Decision 3 — Simulation breadth

### Question

Should the initial simulation capability remain career-specific or cover broader alternate life paths?

### Recommendation

**Career-specific initial scope with expandable architecture.**

Life Branch can remain domain-capable, but career-specific scope bounds reference data, safety, uncertainty, comparison dimensions, and proof.

### Alternative consequence

A general life-path simulator is more expansive but requires substantially more domain and safety law before implementation.

---

## Decision 4 — Reflow capability level

### Question

Should Adaptive Step Placement Reflow be standalone or a named subcapability of broader Schedule Reflow?

### Recommendation

**Standalone visible capability backed by Scheduling and Capacity.**

The owner-originated promise is specifically about what happens when a new Step enters the plan. Keeping it visible prevents the experience from collapsing into an invisible engine feature.

### Alternative consequence

A Schedule Reflow subcapability is ontologically compact but less independently traceable.

---

## Decision 5 — Appearance Studio depth

### Question

What initial authoring depth is required for Appearance Studio to qualify as first-class?

### Recommendation

**Bounded multidimensional studio with live preview.**

Initial approved dimensions:

- Appearance mode
- Approved accent/palette expression
- Surface/material intensity
- Supported density or emphasis choices
- Compatible motion-expression preferences

Every dimension remains constrained by semantic roles, accessibility, native platform behavior, and the selected Ambitions visual system.

### Alternative consequence

Mode and accent only is smaller but is more likely to remain a settings page than a first-class Studio.

---

## Decision 6 — Share Studio formats

### Question

Which initial output formats establish Content Share Studio as first-class?

### Recommendation

**Native payloads plus visual and document exports.**

All formats share one audience, field-selection, redaction, provenance, exact-preview, and Receipt contract.

### Alternatives

- Native payloads only: smaller, but risks becoming a refined share-sheet action.
- Visual exports first: strong expressive artifact, but structured/document use cases remain deferred.

---

## Decision 7 — Files and Knowledge scope

### Question

Should the initial experience remain Goal-attached or be object-wide from launch?

### Recommendation

**Goal-first experience with object-capable architecture.**

This gives the product a coherent initial use case while preserving stable Attachment identity and relationships for later expansion.

### Alternative consequences

- Object-wide launch materially expands every surface, lifecycle, Search, deletion, performance, and accessibility obligation.
- Goal-only architecture creates unnecessary future migration constraints.
