# Ambitions Capability Reconciliation

Status: **non-normative Phase D proposal**

Phase B produced 13 qualified candidate records. Those records are not automatically 13 distinct capabilities. Some are owner seeds, some are composed journeys, some are cross-surface projection contracts, and some are surface-specific expressions of one deeper promise.

This reconciliation proposes **nine stable capability identities** while preserving every source candidate and its disposition.

All 13 qualified candidate IDs have exactly one proposed disposition. The 298 ambiguous records remain preserved and are not silently merged, rejected, or promoted.

## Merge law applied

### Journey variants are expressions, not duplicate capabilities

`Search Find Act Inspect` and `Search Find Ask Act Inspect` both express the same global Search promise. They become required journey variants beneath `CAP-SEARCH-001`; they do not create two additional top-level capabilities.

### Projection contracts remain with the canonical object capability

The canonical Goal Path's projection into Today, Time, Motion, Search, and Trust is essential behavior of contextual goal pathing. Making projection a separate capability would create competing ownership and weaken the invariant that one Goal Path remains canonical.

### Degraded-state expressions may share one resilience capability

Offline Today and offline Search differ operationally, but their durable promise is the same: valid local work remains usable, external limitations are disclosed, and repair is deterministic. They therefore reconcile into `CAP-RESILIENCE-001` with domain-specific expressions.

### User-facing names should not center implementation mechanisms

“Goal-Attached File Storage” is preserved as owner wording and an alias. The proposed name “Goal-Attached Files and Knowledge” centers the person's durable relationship to information rather than the storage engine.

## Proposed identities

### `CAP-CONTEXT-001` — Skill Transference

Preserve as an explicit, inspectable capability. Reducing it to an invisible local-learning mechanism would lose the defining promise that a person can deliberately reuse a demonstrated skill, strategy, or pattern in another domain.

Additional owner decision: standalone flagship capability versus named subcapability of local learning. Recommendation: standalone.

### `CAP-PATH-001` — Contextual Generative Goal Pathing

One capability owns personalized, revisable path generation and the canonical Goal Path's projections across the product. Personal context, learned behavior, time fit, constraints, dependencies, and uncertainty inform the path; they do not create competing copies.

No additional scope decision is required before the general owner approval of the proposed identity.

### `CAP-SIMULATION-001` — Alternate Career Path Simulation

Preserve the owner's career-specific promise as the initial bounded scope. The architecture may allow future expansion, but a broad “life simulation” identity would currently exceed the evidence and create an unbounded prediction promise.

Additional owner decision: career-specific scope versus broader alternate-life-path scope. Recommendation: career-specific initial capability with expandable architecture.

### `CAP-TIME-001` — Adaptive Step Placement Reflow

Preserve the visible user promise separately from the scheduling engine. The person should preview and understand what moves when a step is placed, why it moves, and how the consequence can be repaired or undone.

Additional owner decision: standalone capability versus named subcapability of broader schedule reflow. Recommendation: standalone visible capability backed by broader scheduling infrastructure.

### `CAP-SEARCH-001` — Ambitions Native Search and Command

One global native capability owns retrieval, grounded asking, contextual inspection, command invocation, and receipt-aware consequence. “Raycast-like” remains inspiration and alias evidence, not canonical product naming.

The direct-action and grounded-ask journeys remain distinct journey contracts beneath the same capability.

### `CAP-APPEARANCE-001` — Appearance Studio

Preserve the first-class studio promise while removing “first-class” from the stable product name. First-class status is demonstrated through scope, depth, preview, accessibility, coherence, and product integration—not by retaining the adjective in the title.

Additional owner decision: the minimum authoring depth required beyond color and appearance mode. Recommendation: a bounded multidimensional studio with live preview.

### `CAP-SHARING-001` — Content Share Studio

Preserve as deliberate external composition rather than a raw system share-sheet action. The capability must support audience-bound transformation, privacy review, exact preview, and source-object preservation.

Additional owner decision: initial supported output formats. Recommendation: native share payloads plus visual and document exports.

### `CAP-KNOWLEDGE-001` — Goal-Attached Files and Knowledge

Preserve the explicit goal-attached promise while centering the person's files, references, and knowledge rather than storage implementation. The capability is not a generic file manager or cloud-drive replacement.

Additional owner decision: remain goal-attached or expand to all first-class objects. Recommendation: goal-first product scope with object-capable architecture.

### `CAP-RESILIENCE-001` — Local-First Operational Continuity

Create one repository-derived identity for the cross-product promise that committed local truth remains useful during degraded conditions. Today and Search become validated expressions; future domains qualify only when they meet the same disclosure, repair, and restoration law.

No additional scope decision is required before the general owner approval of the proposed identity.

## Owner decisions deferred to the owner gate

Six scope decisions cannot be resolved deterministically:

1. Skill Transference: standalone capability or local-learning subcapability.
2. Alternate Career Path Simulation: career-specific or broader life-path scope.
3. Adaptive Step Placement Reflow: standalone capability or broader reflow subcapability.
4. Appearance Studio: minimum first-class authoring depth.
5. Content Share Studio: initial output formats.
6. Goal-Attached Files and Knowledge: goal-first or object-wide scope.

The proposed defaults allow Phase E product-promise drafting to proceed without treating those defaults as approved decisions.
