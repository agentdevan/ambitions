# Ambitions Draft Capability Gap Report

Status: **non-normative Phase G analysis**

Baseline: `5670f24fb82adefd27cffee5ddf8fb70676ed049`

This report identifies what prevents each proposed capability from becoming canonical, implementation-ready, verified, or shippable. It distinguishes:

- **Missing:** no adequate contract or evidence exists.
- **Partial:** relevant authority or implementation exists but does not close the capability.
- **Unverified:** intended behavior exists but has not been proven at capability level.
- **Deferred:** an owner or later-gate decision intentionally remains open.
- **Prohibited:** the shortcut conflicts with current privacy, authority, safety, or product law.

The machine-readable register is `draft-capability-gaps.json`.

## Executive result

The repository supports a credible nine-capability atlas, but **none of the nine is yet entitled to capability-level implemented, verified, shippable, or marketed status**.

Three capabilities have substantial canon, source, and focused test evidence:

1. Contextual Generative Goal Pathing
2. Adaptive Step Placement Reflow
3. Ambitions Native Search and Command

The remaining six have strong adjacent foundations, design intent, or platform primitives, but lack one or more defining contracts, journeys, runtime integrations, or proof packages.

No proposed capability is inherently impossible under current product law. Several shortcuts are prohibited, especially any design that sends the private life graph to hosted AI or an Ambitions backend to perform core personalization, pathing, simulation, sharing, or learning.

## Portfolio blockers

### 1. No canonical capability authority

All nine identities remain proposed. Canon installation is blocked until the owner approves each identity and resolves six additional scope decisions.

### 2. No compiler-backed Product Genome

The current canon compiler does not consume capability authority or enforce links from capability to requirement, object, system, surface, test, proof, and retirement state.

### 3. No capability is fully verified

Focused source, test, audit, and visual evidence exists, but no proposed capability has complete end-to-end proof spanning privacy, accessibility, degraded operation, replay, performance, and final person-facing experience.

### 4. Implementation maturity remains separate and unassessed

The discovery program deliberately did not promote capability maturity because similarly named code or tests exist. Capability-specific verification must occur after owner approval and stable scope.

### 5. Ambiguous discovery evidence remains

The 298 ambiguous records remain preserved. They should receive a bounded second semantic pass after owner decisions stabilize the identity boundaries; they are not a reason to inflate the current top-level capability count.

## Capability-by-capability gaps

### `CAP-CONTEXT-001` — Skill Transference

**Current posture:** owner seed with adjacent local-learning and privacy law.

**Blocking gaps:**

- No normative cross-domain transfer identity, evidence model, confidence model, lifecycle, or correction contract.
- No stable representation separates demonstrated source capability from destination applicability.
- No approved person-facing discovery, explanation, comparison, acceptance, correction, or dismissal journey.
- No tests prove plausible versus implausible transfers, sensitive-domain boundaries, sparse evidence, deletion, or no silent consequential action.
- Owner must decide whether it is standalone or a named Local Learning subcapability.

**Recommended closeout:** retain the visible product promise, add a bounded transfer-proposal contract beneath Local Learning, and require explicit person review before any accepted transfer influences a consequential path.

### `CAP-PATH-001` — Contextual Generative Goal Pathing

**Current posture:** strong canonical object, system, surface, source, and focused test foundation.

**Blocking gaps:**

- No measurable acceptance criteria establish contextual uniqueness, usefulness, calibration, or non-template behavior.
- The typed boundary between Local Learning influences and Goal Path generation is incomplete.
- No single suite proves generation through review, acceptance, projection, adaptation, Receipt, replay, rollback, privacy, and accessibility.
- Representative generation, adaptation, memory, energy, cancellation, and large-path performance budgets are not calibrated.

**Recommended closeout:** create a golden capability suite over multiple contrasting personal contexts and require explainable path differences, stable canonical identity, consequence review, and cross-surface parity.

### `CAP-SIMULATION-001` — Alternate Career Path Simulation

**Current posture:** strong normative branch architecture, explicitly limited to design intent.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Owner must choose career-specific or broader life-path scope.
- No career-specific journey defines inputs, public references, comparison dimensions, uncertainty, experiments, adoption, or abandonment.
- Life Branch and Branch Viability Certificate specifications explicitly state that runtime implementation is not proven.
- No approved reference-data model covers occupations, training, compensation, geography, freshness, provenance, or uncertainty.
- No executable simulator, certificate, invalidation, comparison, promotion, or rollback proof exists.

**Recommended closeout:** begin with a career-specific scope over the existing branch architecture; treat every result as a hypothetical, inspectable scenario and never as a predicted future.

### `CAP-TIME-001` — Adaptive Step Placement Reflow

**Current posture:** strong reflow law and substantial source/test evidence.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Owner must decide standalone capability versus named schedule-reflow subcapability.
- The exact new-Step placement and selective acceptance journey is incomplete.
- Atomic multi-object rollback and external-write reconciliation are not proven.
- Named non-drag accessibility parity and focus restoration remain unverified at capability level.
- No final device evidence proves comprehension under typical, dense, conflicted, recurring, and externally constrained schedules.

**Recommended closeout:** prove one golden new-Step placement journey from preview through partial acceptance, canonical commit, external result, Receipt, replay, and rollback.

### `CAP-SEARCH-001` — Ambitions Native Search and Command

**Current posture:** strongest source, test, and audit evidence in the proposed atlas.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Global invocation, focus, dismissal, resume, external-keyboard behavior, and Raycast-grade interaction quality are not finally proven.
- Grounded Ask needs a complete supported-question, refusal, citation, stale-evidence, no-answer, and privacy evaluation suite.
- App-wide canonical action and owner-handoff coverage is incomplete.
- Query, ranking, rebuild, index-size, memory, energy, and latency budgets are not calibrated on supported devices.
- Existing audits do not certify final frontend reconstruction quality or complete shippability.

**Recommended closeout:** generate an object/action coverage matrix and run one final native Search acceptance program after the frontend rebuild.

### `CAP-APPEARANCE-001` — Appearance Studio

**Current posture:** owner seed with partial You/settings and design-token infrastructure.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Owner must define the authoring dimensions required for first-class status.
- No normative Studio identity, state model, preview, commit, migration, reset, or failure contract exists.
- Person-selectable semantic tokens and safe composition rules are undefined.
- No closed studio anatomy or product-wide live-preview experience exists.
- No matrix proves allowed choices across light/dark, accessibility, content stress, motion reduction, persistence, migration, reset, and restoration.

**Recommended closeout:** adopt a bounded multidimensional studio with live preview, semantic token constraints, complete accessibility equivalence, and product-wide coherent application.

### `CAP-SHARING-001` — Content Share Studio

**Current posture:** owner seed with privacy, export, Attachment, and Trust primitives.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Owner must choose initial formats.
- No normative Studio identity, audience model, field selection, transformation, exact preview, destination, or Receipt contract exists.
- No approved local multi-format rendering architecture exists.
- No field-level redaction and relationship-traversal policy maps private objects into exact output payloads.
- No end-to-end proof validates bytes, visuals, metadata leakage, accessibility, destination failure, large payloads, cancellation, Receipts, and source immutability.

**Prohibited shortcut:** core rendering may not send the private life graph to an Ambitions backend or hosted AI. A named external integration may receive only explicitly selected minimum fields through separate approval.

**Recommended closeout:** start with native payloads plus visual and document exports rendered locally under one fail-closed audience/redaction/preview contract.

### `CAP-KNOWLEDGE-001` — Goal-Attached Files and Knowledge

**Current posture:** strong Attachment object law and partial storage evidence.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Owner must choose goal-first versus object-wide product scope.
- Goal-specific Attachment roles, ordering, shared links, and deletion consequences are incomplete.
- Apple file importer, security-scoped resources, bookmark lifecycle, copy-versus-link policy, permission loss, and recovery are not proven.
- Attachment metadata and permitted derived-content Search indexing is incomplete.
- File type, size, count, streaming, storage-pressure, thumbnail/OCR, memory, energy, and cleanup budgets are undefined.
- No final Goal files/knowledge experience or device proof exists.

**Recommended closeout:** keep the first product experience goal-first while retaining object-capable Attachment relationships and strict distinction between unlinking and deleting shared content.

### `CAP-RESILIENCE-001` — Local-First Operational Continuity

**Current posture:** strong cross-app degraded-state and offline law with partial runtime/audit evidence.

**Blocking gaps:**

- Every root, focused object, Capture path, mutation, import/export flow, and external effect is not yet mapped to one failure-class/recovery-owner matrix.
- Interruption, relaunch, checkpoint, stale preview, and partial-result behavior are not app-wide proven.
- Backup, restore, repair, index rebuild, and data-loss prevention are not proven together from a clean device state.
- Local-success versus external-failure reconciliation is not proven across all adapters.
- Failure class, unchanged data, affected scope, and recovery are not app-wide proven nonvisually.

**Recommended closeout:** execute destructive recovery drills and cross-app accessibility scenarios after producing the complete continuity coverage matrix.

## Unsafe or prohibited shortcuts

Current law prohibits:

1. Sending private life-graph context to hosted AI or an Ambitions backend to perform core pathing, simulation, transfer learning, sharing, or personalization.
2. Presenting probabilistic or generative output as deterministic truth, professional qualification, guaranteed career outcome, or a valid branch certificate.
3. Committing material path, schedule, scenario, share, deletion, or external-effect changes outside the owning confirmation and Receipt boundary.
4. Allowing appearance choices to remove semantic meaning, required state, contrast, accessibility, or product authority.

## Recommended execution order

1. Resolve the nine identity approvals and six scope decisions.
2. Install approved capability authority and compiler traceability.
3. Close missing specifications for Skill Transference, Appearance Studio, Content Share Studio, and career-specific simulation.
4. Complete capability-level proof for Goal Pathing, Reflow, Search, Goal Attachments, and Operational Continuity.
5. Run a bounded second-pass reconciliation over the 298 ambiguous records.

Phase G is complete when these gaps are explicit and prioritized—not when the repository pretends they are already repaired.
