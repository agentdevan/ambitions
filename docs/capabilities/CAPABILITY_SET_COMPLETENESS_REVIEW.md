# Ambitions Capability Set Completeness Review

Status: **initial nine approved; full-set approval deferred**

## Owner decision recorded

The owner approved the nine proposed identities as **approvable initial capabilities**, while explicitly asking whether additional capabilities should be added or reviewed before the set becomes canonical.

This means:

- The nine identities are accepted as valid product capabilities.
- They are not treated as an exclusive or complete list.
- Canon installation remains blocked until the full capability landscape is reviewed.
- The six scope decisions in the existing owner packet remain pending unless separately selected.

## Why another review is required

The Phase C taxonomy defines **14 product-meaning domains**, but the current nine-capability proposal has primary capability coverage in only **9 domains**.

Five domains have no first-class capability identity:

| Uncovered domain | Missing person-facing promise |
|---|---|
| Present Execution and Adaptation | Understand what is executable now, act, adapt, and recover |
| Identity, Privacy, and Trust | Control personal identity, local data, privacy, explanations, and history |
| Capture and Interpretation | Express and preserve something before its meaning or destination is final |
| Reflection, Proof, and Closure | Preserve evidence, understand progress, reflect, and close honestly |
| Apple Ecosystem and Automation | Reach and act through Apple system surfaces without weakening authority or privacy |

The preserved ambiguity register also contains **298 records**. Most are not capabilities, but some may contain aliases, missing baseline promises, or evidence for additional capability identities.

## Strong additional capability candidates

### 1. Provisional Capture and Interpretation

**Confidence:** High

**Domain:** Capture and Interpretation

Ambitions already promises global provisional intake, durable preservation, interpretation review, owner-routed placement, and recovery. This is a person-facing outcome, not merely the Capture surface.

Likely promise:

> Let a person express something quickly, preserve it safely, understand Ambitions’ interpretation, and decide what it becomes without premature commitment.

### 2. Present Execution and Adaptive Recovery

**Confidence:** High

**Domain:** Present Execution and Adaptation

Today owns immediate execution, narrow contextual action, changed-reality response, and missed-step recovery. Those behaviors need one product capability identity.

Likely promise:

> Help a person understand what is genuinely executable now, act with context, adapt when reality changes, and recover without becoming a task list.

### 3. Personal Data, Privacy, and Identity Control

**Confidence:** High

**Domain:** Identity, Privacy, and Trust

You, Privacy, Local Learning, deletion, export, reset, account separation, and local-data controls establish a broader promise than Appearance Studio.

Likely promise:

> Let the person know what Ambitions stores and infers, understand how it is used, and control identity, privacy, retention, export, reset, and deletion.

### 4. Trust Inspection and Reversible History

**Confidence:** High

**Domain:** Identity, Privacy, and Trust

Receipts, History, provenance, explanations, inspection, Undo, rollback, and repair form a distinct capability. Search may invoke this capability but should not own it.

Likely promise:

> Let a person inspect why something happened, what changed, where information came from, and how a supported change can be reversed or repaired.

### 5. Proof, Reflection, and Closure

**Confidence:** High

**Domain:** Reflection, Proof, and Closure

Proof, progress interpretation, reflection, closure, pause, pivot, and retained history are central Ambitions outcomes and currently lack a capability identity.

Likely promise:

> Help a person preserve meaningful evidence, understand progress, reflect on outcomes, and close or change a pursuit without losing truth.

### 6. Native Apple Reach and Automation

**Confidence:** Medium

**Domain:** Apple Ecosystem and Automation

App Intents, widgets, notifications, EventKit, deep links, share intake, and other Apple system surfaces may form one umbrella capability or several subcapabilities.

Likely promise:

> Extend Ambitions through native Apple surfaces and person-authorized automation while preserving canonical ownership, privacy, confirmation, accessibility, and local-first behavior.

## Covered-domain breadth checks

The nine approved capabilities are valid, but several are narrower than their entire domain:

1. **Goals:** Contextual Goal Pathing may not cover ordinary Goal and Life Area creation, management, pause, pivot, accomplishment, and closure.
2. **Time:** Step Placement Reflow does not cover the whole Time promise: chronology, events, recurrence, protected time, calendar views, and import review.
3. **Learning:** Skill Transference does not cover the whole Local Learning and adaptive-guidance promise.
4. **Knowledge:** Goal-attached files may not cover general notes, references, provenance, collection, synthesis, and retrieval.
5. **Resilience:** Operational Continuity may need named backup, restore, repair, portability, or continuity subcapabilities.

These require explicit merge/split decisions rather than silent expansion of the nine approved promises.

## What is not being approved

The following are not hidden capability approvals:

- **14 taxonomy domains:** organizational categories only.
- **52 gaps:** missing law, architecture, UI, implementation, tests, or proof.
- **298 ambiguous records:** preserved evidence requiring review.
- **Objects and systems:** implementation or domain owners, not automatically product capabilities.
- **Six recommended scope choices:** still pending explicit owner selection.
