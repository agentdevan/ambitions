## Excluded from the projection

Research-only hypotheses remain outside the capability projection until validated. The rejected `Inspectable Influence Graph` framing remains excluded; its lawful underlying controls stay represented through Trust, You, privacy, learning, Receipts, and History.

## Authority boundary

A derived label may improve strategy, roadmap communication, and completeness reporting. It cannot create an object, lifecycle, root, mutation authority, specification requirement, or implementation claim.
