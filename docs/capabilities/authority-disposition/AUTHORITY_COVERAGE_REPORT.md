# Authority Coverage Report

Status: **non-normative audit result**

## Executive finding

The 112-item draft substantially over-counted Ambitions product capabilities by treating object lifecycles, projections, integration seams, algorithms, horizontal guarantees, future-gated behaviors, and research hypotheses as equivalent top-level capabilities.

Current canon already owns most of the proposed behavior. The correct result is not a new 112-item authority layer; it is a smaller derived projection, targeted specification amendments for real gaps, and explicit activation posture for gated features.

## Major findings

1. **Foundation coverage is already strong.** Life Areas, Goals, Steps, Goal Paths, Today execution, Time, scheduling, Capture, Search, Proof, closure, privacy, persistence, and Apple handoffs already have manifest-controlled owners.
2. **Most apparent breadth gaps are projection problems.** Several draft labels combine multiple authoritative objects and journeys into useful strategic language but do not justify new authority.
3. **The real gaps are narrow.** The twelve specification gaps concentrate in skill transfer, resource resurfacing, polished sharing, Appearance Studio depth, recovery preferences, Capture calibration/suggestions, and review cadence.
4. **Activation posture matters.** Backup/restore, continuity, Capture attachments, durable draft recovery, Search Ask, Share intake, and some Apple surfaces cannot be presented as current shipping capabilities merely because safety contracts exist.
5. **No draft item independently requires a constitutional amendment.** The only constitutional question is strategic naming: whether `MISSION-NAMING-001` should eventually remain centered on generative goal pathing with schedule reflow or adopt a broader flagship label.
6. **One framing is rejected.** `Inspectable Influence Graph` conflicts with the constitutional and You-surface prohibition on an exposed knowledge/memory dossier. Plain learning, privacy, Receipt, History, correction, reset, and deletion controls remain authoritative.
