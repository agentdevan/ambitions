# Compounding Review Pass 1 — Semantic and Authority Boundaries

Status: **completed non-normative review**

## Question

Did the first disposition still mistake dependent controls, internal models, or integration seams for independent capability gaps?

## Method

Every owner-decision record was retested against four questions:

1. Does it produce a distinct person-facing outcome?
2. Does it require an independent lifecycle and authority owner?
3. Is the missing contract absent, or merely distributed across existing owners?
4. Would approving it separately duplicate a parent capability or internal mechanism?

## Findings

### Appearance collapsed from four gaps to one

`Material and Surface Intensity`, `Density and Emphasis`, and `Private Appearance and Shared Artifact Separation` are valid subcapabilities or boundary rules. They do not justify separate product authorities. The genuine gap is the approved parent outcome: **Appearance Studio**.

### Skill Transference is the outcome; the model is internal

`Cross-Domain Capability and Resource Model` describes semantic support for Local Learning and Life Capital. It is not independently understandable to the user and should not become a graph, object family, or destination. **Skill and Strategy Transfer Intelligence** remains the genuine person-facing gap.

### Adaptive Capture collapsed from two gaps to one

`Contextual Capture Suggestions` is one typed output of **Capture Routing Calibration**. Both need the same evidence, uncertainty, expiry, suppression, quiet fallback, accessibility, and authority rules. Splitting them would create duplicate policy.

### Resource resurfacing is fragmented authority

Planning may already compose public references locally, Search retrieves sources, canonical relationships attach resources to objects, and Local Learning may influence declared decisions. The missing work is a bounded integration contract, not a new capability owner.

### Review rhythm is fragmented authority

You already carries review cadence, Notifications owns return rules, Closure owns honest outcomes, and Local Learning may observe timing. The missing work is cross-owner reconciliation.

### Recovery and restoration were improperly combined

Non-destructive recovery option ordering may use preferences. Backup restore, repair, reset, conflict resolution, and destructive recovery cannot be personalized into standing behavior; they remain exact-state, evidence-bound, freshly confirmed operations.
