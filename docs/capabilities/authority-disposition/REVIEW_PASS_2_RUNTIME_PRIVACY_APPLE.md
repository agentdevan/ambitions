# Compounding Review Pass 2 — Runtime, Privacy, Apple, and Activation

Status: **completed non-normative review**

## Question

Would the remaining recommendations preserve the Private Life Runtime, privacy firewall, native Apple boundaries, accessibility floors, and honest activation posture?

## Findings

### Reuse Local Learning; do not invent a personalization framework

Local Learning already owns versioned evidence-linked influences, uncertainty, expiry, correction, disablement, reset, deletion, deterministic rebuild, and declared decision use. New adaptive behaviors must be typed use-site policies under existing owners. No new generic “personalized operational rules” object, engine, graph, or settings center is justified.

### Skill Transference needs sensitive-domain isolation

Transfer may use only eligible local evidence. The contract must distinguish explicit facts from inferred patterns, expose structural similarities and material differences, preserve unknowns, and fail quiet. Sensitive evidence cannot cross domains merely because the system detects similarity. Material Path or Time effects remain proposals.

### Recovery preference is not restoration authority

A preference may rank options such as keep current, defer, split, or review later. It must not preauthorize backup restore, data repair, reset, continuity conflict resolution, source replacement, or destructive recovery. Those operations require current evidence and fresh exact-scope confirmation.

### Content Share Studio must be future-gated

The Studio may compose a local export artifact from selected canonical sources, but it cannot create a live shared graph, collaboration system, automatic publication channel, or private backend. Activation requires exact source-object scope, local draft lifecycle, field selection, redaction, preview, file-format accessibility, egress settlement, interruption recovery, and destination proof.

### Appearance Studio cannot weaken product semantics

Presets may alter presentation within approved ranges only. They cannot change canonical state, hide required information, reduce Dynamic Type support, alter semantic warning/destructive/proposed/accepted meaning, or leak private accessibility and device preferences into shared artifacts. Adaptive presets remain research-only.

### Apple reach remains per-surface and proof-gated

A category label does not activate Spotlight, Share intake, widgets, App Intents, notification actions, or extensions. Each consumes minimized projections or canonical handoffs and requires framework, entitlement, privacy, accessibility, interruption, and device proof.

### Search requires authority cleanup

The no-chatbot product decision conflicts with future conversational `Ask` language. Preserve deterministic Find, bounded evidence-backed Understand, owner-routed Act, and Inspect. Remove or narrow conversational wording without activating any gated command.

3. **P2 bounded expression/adaptation:** Appearance Studio and Capture Routing Calibration.
4. **P3 external expression:** Content Share Studio.
5. Fragmented integrations are repaired only when their owning implementations or specifications are active work.
