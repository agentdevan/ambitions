## Decision 6 — Appearance Studio

Covers `CAP2-APPEAR-007` Appearance Studio.

**Recommendation: approve one bounded Appearance Studio under You.**

One parent specification owns approved appearance modes and accents, material-intensity presets, density/emphasis presets, live preview, persistence/reset, migration, performance/energy, accessibility floors, and private-versus-shared style separation.

Material intensity, density/emphasis, and private/shared styling are subcapabilities—not independent gaps. The Studio cannot override semantic states, hide required information/actions, reduce Dynamic Type/input access, weaken contrast, or become arbitrary per-surface theming.

**Priority:** P2 expression specification.
