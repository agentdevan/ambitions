## What Is Settled

The following are now settled canon unless superseded by a later explicit product decision:

- Ambitions is a life organization system and personal life OS / external brain.
- The locked top-level shell is `Today / Goals / Capture / Plan / You`.
- Ambitions should stay deep, not wide.
- Today prioritizes one best next action and explains the current execution context.
- Goals protects direction and avoids top-level project-management-board behavior.
- Plan shapes believable days/weeks and may become calendar-aware only after user action.
- Capture is a Quiet Command Sheet with Smart Attachment and a Needs a Place fallback.
- You is the personal system center; Profile is legacy compatibility terminology during migration only.
- Intelligence explains, suggests, and prepares; it does not chat first or silently decide for the user.
- Suggestions must be explainable, correctable, dismissible, and calm.
- Accessibility and Focus Support are core product quality, not late compliance.
- External surfaces provide calm continuity and must not spam, expose private details, or replace app context.
- Data posture is local-first; no account is required at launch; no launch sync.
- Export should exist before cloud sync and should not feel hostage.
- Monetization should feel premium but accessible, with no ads and no manipulative tactics.
- Launch should prove one meaningful goal can become organized, believable, and actionable.
- Roadmap governance should prevent drift and optimize for coherent loops.
