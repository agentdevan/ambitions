# AmbitionsOS Safety Legality Feasibility Triage

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

Status: Future source truth. No crisis tooling, professional advice, legal/privacy signoff, or safety runtime implementation is claimed.

## Triage Scope

LDI screens illegal goals, violence or harm to others, self-harm or crisis-coded input, exploitation or coercion, stalking or harassment, fraud or evasion, academic/workplace dishonesty, dangerous physical goals, regulated/professional-boundary domains, impossible/fantasy goals, delusion/paranoia-coded goals, minors/age-sensitive goals, privacy-sensitive goals, and source-sensitive goals.

## Safety Rules

- Illegal/harmful input: do not operationalize; create `unsafe_blocked` and safe redirect handling.
- Self-harm/crisis-coded input: do not route into normal productivity; create `crisis_support` with calm support UX and no harmful steps.
- Harm-to-others: block revenge, stalking, intimidation, violence, harassment, doxxing, coercion, or manipulation plans. Redirect to lawful, safe, restorative alternatives.
- Professional-boundary domains: legal, medical, financial, immigration, licensing, public office, aviation, high-risk physical activities, and similar domains must use source states and verification boundaries.
- Fantasy/impossible: use `north_star_extraction`.
- Delusion/paranoia-coded: do not validate delusions. Offer grounding, concrete evidence logging, and safe support.
- Minors: use age-sensitive handling, guardian/legal/safety context, local law source checks, and crisis support if danger.
- Manipulation/exploitation: block coercive plans; redirect to ethical communication/community-building.
- Academic/workplace dishonesty: block cheating/fraud; redirect to legitimate recovery/study/skill-proof paths.
- Dangerous health/fitness: safety review and medical/professional boundary where relevant.
- Impossible timelines: reality check, aggressive-but-plausible path, realistic path, and first proof step.
- Privacy-sensitive goals: default discreet/local privacy behavior.

## Professional Boundary

Ambitions may provide planning scaffolds, source states, and verification boundaries. Ambitions must not provide legal, medical, financial, immigration, or regulated professional advice.
