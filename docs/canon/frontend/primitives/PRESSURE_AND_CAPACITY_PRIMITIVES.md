# Pressure And Capacity Primitives

Status: Active primitive role set

## Protected Block

- carved-out time or space that must not be silently taken

## Pressure Cluster

- a visible compression of commitments or demand

## Current-State Crown

- the current-state header or orientation line for the surface

## Preview Delta

- before/after difference for a reflow or change

## Recovery Basin

- a low-pressure area for recovery and narrowing

## Holding Basin

- a safe holding area that is not shameful backlog

## Focus Narrowing Mask

- reduces visible density while preserving escape paths

## Capacity Confidence

- the system's confidence in available capacity, never a score

## Accessibility Concern

Pressure must be legible in shape and spacing, not alarm color alone.
