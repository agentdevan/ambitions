## Purpose

Ambitions preserves useful history without letting history steer the current product by accident.

This policy defines where historical migration evidence may remain and how active docs should label older material.

---

## Historical Evidence Rule

Historical material may remain when it is clearly one of:

- archived evidence under `docs/archive/`;
- batch or audit evidence under `docs/audits/` or `docs/codex/`;
- still-binding older canon where Ambitions 3.0 does not replace the domain;
- compatibility evidence for route, deep-link, App Intent, widget, Live Activity, share extension, or stored-value safety.

Historical material must not be presented as current product direction.

---

## What Must Be Labeled

Label older references when they mention:

- old launch names;
- old top-level tabs;
- old shell models;
- old roadmap waves or batches;
- Ambitions 2.0/v2 as current product direction;
- migration-only terminology;
- superseded visual/component names.

Labels should be plain:

- `historical evidence`;
- `supporting context`;
- `compatibility seam`;
- `archived / superseded`;
- `binding only where Ambitions 3.0 does not replace this domain`.

---

## Deletion And Archive Rules

Do not delete useful history just to make scans quiet.

Deletion or movement is allowed only when:

- the file is generated junk;
- the file has no future value and no evidence value;
- archive policy says the history is preserved elsewhere;
- links and indexes are updated;
- the batch report names what moved or was deleted.

Useful history should usually be labeled or moved, not erased.

---

## Active Doc Rule

Active docs should describe the current Ambitions product directly.

If an active doc needs older history to make sense, it should be rewritten, linked to a current baseline doc, or moved behind an archive/supporting label.
