## Purpose

This document defines the reusable design primitives for Ambitions 3.0.

Ambitions should feel premium, native, calm, and distinct without becoming decorative or dashboard-heavy.

---

## Core Visual Grammar

Ambitions 3.0 uses:

- rails
- nodes
- sheets
- peeks
- receipts
- proof marks
- context chips
- compact headers
- signature objects
- grouped navigation rows
- privacy-safe projections
- calm empty states

---

## Rails

Rails represent sequence, progression, navigation, or proof over time.

Allowed rails:

- Today Reality Rail
- Ambition Meridian
- Goal Path Rail
- Proof Rail
- Life Shape Map rail/arc only when meaningful

Do not use rails as decoration.

---

## Nodes

Node grammar:

- circle = step
- diamond = closure or decision
- document mark = proof or receipt
- muted node = waiting/protected/unavailable
- aperture = capture

Nodes are visual indicators. Rows or cards carry touch targets.

---

## Sheets

Use sheets for compact, reversible decisions:

- Why this?
- Close the loop
- Place it
- Change this
- Receipt peek
- Memory confirmation
- Reflow preview

Sheets must not become long dashboards.

---

## Full Screens

Use full screens for deep work:

- Step Session
- Goal Detail
- Plan Life Suite room
- What Ambitions Knows
- Receipts & History
- Reviews

---

## Peeks

Peeks are small expandable explanations.

Use for:

- Why this?
- receipt preview
- proof saved
- source facts
- privacy explanation

Peeks should answer one question and route deeper if needed.

---

## Receipts

Receipt presentations:

- toast
- peek
- trail
- search result
- export item where implemented

Receipt copy follows:

```text
[Result] · [Object] · [Destination or consequence]
```

---

## Proof Marks

Proof marks show evidence saved.

Use restrained motion and quiet visual treatment.

Do not use trophies, confetti, badges, or streak symbols.

---

## Context Chips

Context chips show:

- source label
- duration source
- readiness
- privacy state
- protected/away state
- goal/life area

Chips must be readable and not color-only.

---

## Compact Headers

Headers should be contextual, not large repeated page titles.

Examples:

- Today · Work until 5
- Plan · Week Shape
- Goals · Creative active
- You · Guided

Accessibility/navigation titles may still exist separately.

---

## Grouped Navigation Rows

Use grouped rows for You, Planning Setup, Trust, and deep settings-like lists.

Rows should include:

- title
- value statement
- status when relevant
- chevron
- optional privacy/trust indicator

---

## Empty States

Premium empty states are intentional.

Examples:

- Nothing needs you yet.
- Nothing needs a place.
- No proof saved yet.
- This week has no decisions blocking it.

Every empty state needs a useful next action.

---

## Color And Material

Use:

- graphite depth
- restrained warm gold
- quiet glass/material layering
- strong contrast
- semantic color sparingly

Warm gold is reserved for:

- recommended step
- proof saved
- active rail node
- primary user-approved action

---

## Motion

Motion clarifies:

- proof saved
- plan adjusted
- placement confirmed
- closure completed
- shell transition
- reflow preview

Motion must have Reduce Motion equivalent.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

A design system primitive is mature when:

- it has a specific job
- it maps to a product state
- it has accessibility behavior
- it is reusable
- it avoids decoration-only use
- it preserves top-level density rules
