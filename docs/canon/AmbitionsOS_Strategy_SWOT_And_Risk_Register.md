Status: Future strategy canon under Beyond 3.0; not current app implementation truth

AmbitionsOS supports Ambitions becoming a local-first life path system. It is not generic productivity, a chatbot planner, task app, habit tracker, calendar clone, or analytics dashboard.

## Opportunities

- Define a new category: life path system.
- Become the app for life-defining goals.
- Own not starting over through progress transfer.
- Make honesty a brand advantage.
- Support high school-to-adult 5-20 year paths.
- Use celestial navigation as a restrained identity system.
- Support career, education, and life pivots.
- Make source truth and proof a premium differentiator.
- Create a non-chatbot intelligence layer.
- Become the decision layer above Calendar/Reminders over time without cloning them.
- Turn maintainability extraction, compatibility retirement, and Codex OS continuity into build-quality advantages.

## Threats

Threats include legal/medical/regulated misinformation, stale official requirements, hallucination, sycophantic recommendations, cognitive overload, privacy exposure, app widening, generic positioning, source crawler overreach, discouragement from hard blockers, maintenance burden, battery degradation, unsupported platform claims, large-file regression, hidden extraction behavior changes, broken legacy routes/imports/widgets/App Intents, Codex train drift, and missing validation logs. Each threat routes to an owner kernel, required test, and stop condition in the AOS gate matrix.

## Consolidation Decisions

AmbitionsOS, Goal Intelligence, and Life Commitments are not new top-level app tabs. Path lives inside Goal Detail / Mission Control. Immediate next actions surface in Today. Requirement timing and feasibility surface in Plan. Assumptions and source truth surface in You / Trust. Raw dreams start in Capture. Path changes create receipts. Celestial theme remains a visual/language system. Calendar/Reminders bridge is optional and permissioned. Maintainability extraction, compatibility seam retirement, and Codex OS continuity are future train systems, not automatic work.
