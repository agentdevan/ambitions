HPS11 defines how Ambitions may later expand into education, career, workforce,
coaching, family, proof economy, source packs, API/platform licensing, and
strategic buyer-fit narratives without widening the current app or claiming a
commercial outcome.

pricing decision, legal approval, acquisition claim, buyer-interest claim, App
Store claim, TestFlight claim, release claim, or professional-advice approval.

Current Ambitions remains the private operating system for verified human
progress. The visible product remains Today, Goals, Capture, Plan, and You.

HPS11 does not approve:

- education product launch
- career product launch

- coaching product launch
- family, parent, teacher, counselor, or school product launch

## Strategy Thesis

Ambitions should expand vertically only after the consumer wedge proves the
same core loop in a person's real life:

> Capture -> Place -> Plan -> Do Today -> Close / Recover -> Save Proof.

Vertical strategy must start from Ambitions' owned primitives:

- Human Progress Graph
- Verified Proof Ledger
- Source Truth / Requirement Graph
- Commitment Memory
- Searchable Life Recall
- Recommendation Quality
- Option Value / Pivot Preservation
- Living Dream Compiler
- Privacy / Memory Permission Kernel
- AI Governance / Evaluation Lab

The first-order product remains personal life organization. Vertical ideas are
future overlays on verified progress, source truth, proof, and reviewable
planning. They must not become separate apps inside the app.

## Revenue Lanes

HPS11 preserves future business optionality without implementing revenue.

| Lane | Future paid value | Required later proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium personal tier | Deeper planning, reviews, memory depth, personalization, and advanced continuity. | PFC21-PFC23 reopened with product ids, entitlement rules, tests, copy review, and legal/business approval. |
| Education support | User-owned planning around learning paths and requirements. | Minor/student-data review, source truth fixtures, no admissions guarantee, no school-service claim. |
| Career support | User-owned planning around career transitions, portfolios, skills, and proof. | Professional-boundary review, source-state fixtures, no employment/earnings/credential guarantee. |
| Coaching support | User-owned packet and review flow for qualified-human discussion. | Human-role permissions, privacy review, no coach marketplace or professional service launch. |
| Family support | User-owned shared planning or support packets where explicitly approved. | Consent, age, privacy, external-surface, and delete/export review before any role model. |
| Proof portability | User-exported proof packets and receipts. | Export/delete/correction proof, redaction proof, no public credential network claim. |
| Source packs | Reviewed requirement/source bundles for user planning. | Source freshness, review, rollback, conflict, and legal/professional boundary proof. |
| API/platform licensing | Partner-readable architecture or future contracts. | API design, privacy/legal/security review, account/backend decision, support model, and no user-data overclaim. |

No lane may paywall trust, privacy, delete, correction, export basics, or the
user's ability to understand what Ambitions knows.

## No-Build Gates

Before any future vertical or revenue lane can become implementation scope, a
later batch must prove all of the following:

- named owner and source-truth stack
- exact user value and non-sprawl rationale
- five-tab placement or owned secondary surface
- privacy class and redaction rules
- source truth, freshness, uncertainty, and correction rules
- proof ownership and portability rules
- professional-boundary and minors/student-data review where relevant
- free-tier and paid-tier boundary where relevant
- export/delete/correction behavior
- external-surface behavior
- validation fixtures and regression oracle
- legal/business/human review stops
- rollback path

If a lane needs accounts, sync, backend, hosted AI, public sharing, partner
access, school use, family roles, employer roles, commerce, or external source
services, that dependency must be its own explicit PFC/AOS/LDI/HPS batch before
implementation.

## Surface Placement Rules

Vertical and revenue ideas must remain invisible until a later batch proves a
specific user-facing object. If surfaced later:

- Today may show only what matters now.
- Goals may show path/proof/source implications for user-owned goals.

- Plan may show capacity and timing without becoming a calendar or admin tool.
- You may expose trust, memory, proof, export, correction, and review controls.
