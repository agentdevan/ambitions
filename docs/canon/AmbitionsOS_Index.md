Status: Future canon under Ambitions Beyond 3.0; not current app implementation truth

## What AmbitionsOS Is

AmbitionsOS is the parent future architecture for a local-first life intelligence operating system inside Ambitions. It is built from typed kernels, event-sourced life graph state, source-bound claims, starting-position awareness, alternate path intelligence, commitment/time reasoning, proof and receipt trust, performance-energy governance, privacy-safe projections, and optional future on-device language assistance.

Core law: AmbitionsOS should understand the shape of a user's ambition, where they are starting, what paths may exist, what still counts, what must be verified, what today can hold, when to interrupt, and when not to pretend.

## What AmbitionsOS Is Not

AmbitionsOS is not a chatbot, bundled LLM, generic productivity engine, Calendar clone, Reminders clone, analytics dashboard, source-certification authority, backend strategy, cloud dependency, or permission to add a top-level tab.

## Relationship To PXOS

PXOS, the Product Experience OS, is the future user-facing product experience canon that sits beside AmbitionsOS. AmbitionsOS owns internal intelligence/runtime/source-truth/recommendation/proof/privacy architecture. PXOS owns the future expression of that architecture through screens, surface hierarchy, copy, navigation, interaction, recovery, trust UI, proof UI, receipts, visual design, accessibility, and release-safe product messaging.

AmbitionsOS does not make PXOS implemented, and PXOS does not make AmbitionsOS implemented. Both remain future canon until explicit implementation trains produce evidence.
