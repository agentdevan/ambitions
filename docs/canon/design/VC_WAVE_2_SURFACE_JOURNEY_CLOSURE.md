## 5. VC-07 — Today Closure Family

Selected synthesis:

`D02 + D05 + D06 — Balanced Semantic Execution Day`

Applies within:

`AVF-TODAY-S10-R00 — Start Here Contextual Command`

### Typical first viewport

1. Compact Today crown and date
2. One dominant Start Here projection
3. Concise current truth
4. Material why-now consequence
5. One primary execution action
6. Zero or one earned Also Fits Now projection
7. Beginning of Today’s Timeline
8. Direct `See Full Day` entry

Start Here dominates without consuming the entire viewport. Timeline awareness
begins before the fold. The remaining day continues through natural scrolling.

### Quiet or empty day

- No manufactured priority, recommendation, score, or filler object.
- Open time remains visible truth.
- A reflective or protective action appears only when grounded.
- Also Fits Now may be absent.
- The root may remain intentionally calm.

### Stress, conflict, or interruption

- Start Here remains dominant.
- One root-level qualification appears only when the whole day materially
  changed.
- Current truth, consequence, protection, conflict, and valid action become
  explicit.
- Timeline objects carry local changed, protected, stale, conflict, blocked, or
  failed state.
- Dense evidence moves deeper.
- Alert stacks, universal warning treatment, and color-only meaning are
  prohibited.

### Ownership

- Today shows immediate execution projections, not duplicate canonical objects.
- Goals owns pursuit progression.
- Time owns exact chronology and temporal mutation.
- Editing and consequential action transfer to the canonical owner.
- Also Fits Now is earned, optional, and limited to one projection.
- Today does not own broad schedule reshaping.

### Rejected Today patterns

Three-priority layouts, `Top Priorities`, ranking, scores, completion circles,
task-list anatomy, full-calendar roots, dashboard modules, recommendation feeds,
generic AI summaries, large warning banners, pill collections, decorative
category color, card grids, and full-hierarchy compression are rejected.

## 6. VC-08 — Goals Closure Family

Locked closure:

`VC08-GOALS-S07-R00 — Singular Living Pursuit Passage`

Applies within:

`AVF-GOALS-S08-R00 — Life Area Linked Goal Lens`

Synthesis inheritance:

- D02 — Singular Life Area Passage
- D03 — Living Life Area Field atmosphere
- D05 — Relational Life Area Seam when consequential
- D06 — Explicit Adaptive Pursuit Passage under stress

Core progression:

```text
Life Area index
-> one selected Life Area
-> readable Goal identities
-> one selected Goal
-> inline Linked Goal Lens
-> focused pursuit timeline
-> settlement and integrated history
```

### Typical root composition

The first viewport contains approximately:

1. Compact Goals crown
2. Two or three Life Area identities
3. One selected Life Area expanded inline
4. Two or three Goal identities
5. One selected Goal
6. Only the opening portion of its Linked Goal Lens near the bottom fold

The lower lens, remaining Goals, and additional Life Areas continue through
normal scrolling.

### Life Area and Goal behavior

- Life Areas are persistent root-level organizing identities.
- Only one Life Area is materially expanded at a time.
- Selection does not imply ranking or importance.
- Unselected areas remain compact and recognizable.
- Editing, reorder, disclosure, and navigation remain native where supported.
- Goal identity appears before progress, dates, metrics, or state.
- No Life Area score, completion percentage, or decorative category color is
  introduced.

### Linked Goal Lens

One selected Goal may own the inline lens. Initial root disclosure normally
shows current truth and material consequence. Further scrolling may show one
consequential relationship, compact continuity, an active-step preview, and
entry to focused pursuit depth.

The lens remains visually attached to its Goal. It is not a detached card,
inspector, or dossier.

Only one materially consequential relationship may expand at root. It must name
the related Goal, owning Life Area, relationship meaning, practical consequence,
and route to the owner. Geometry reinforces meaning but never explains it alone.

### Focused pursuit depth

Focused depth distinguishes completed, active, planned, conditional or
tentative, conflict, recovery, settlement, and historical progression. These
are pursuit transitions, not ordinary task rows.

Preview breadth, repeated metadata, and secondary evidence compress before
identity or type size. Under accessibility pressure, the structure becomes an
explicit Life Area → Goal → truth → consequence → state → relationship →
progression → action → return passage.

### Rejected Goals patterns

Goal-card grids, portfolio dashboards, progress gauges, completion circles,
persistent connector maps, several expanded Life Areas, full lens anatomy before
the fold, tiny typography, decorative category color, boxed accordion behavior,
and full pursuit timelines at root are rejected.

## 7. VC-09 — Time Closure Family

Locked closure:

`VC09-TIME-S07-R00 — Adaptive Dual-Truth Period Passage`

Applies within:

`AVF-TIME-S07-R01 — Integrated Period Atlas, Capability-Bounded`

Synthesis inheritance:

- D02 — Adaptive Week Passage
- D03 — restrained Continuous Temporal Canvas character
- D04 — Dual-Truth Week Ledger for exact operation
- D06 — Explicit Adaptive Period Passage under stress

Core progression:

```text
active period orientation
-> selective chronology
-> focused day or Event
-> current/proposed dual-truth preview
-> truthful settlement
-> return to originating temporal context
```

### Default scale and first viewport

- Week is the first-use scale.
- Later entry may restore the last-used implemented supported scale.
- Unsupported scales are absent.
- Week remains distinct from Today.

The typical first viewport contains a compact Time crown, active week identity,
present relationship, one lived temporal truth, one protection, one material
pressure/opening/conflict, the beginning of chronology, and direct current-day
entry. The complete period continues through natural scrolling.

### Temporal truth and meaning

Time distinguishes accepted placement, proposed placement, external observation,
stale external truth, unknown current truth, and historical chronology.
Suggested timing never receives accepted treatment without authority.

Time may express protection, personal usability, transition friction, recovery,
over-capacity, and conflict. Open calendar time alone does not imply personal
usability or recommendation. Over-capacity is aggregate practical demand;
conflict requires identifiable valid participants that cannot coexist.

### Exact operation and settlement

Focused day and Event depth use native calendar mechanics where sufficient. A
consequential preview shows current exact truth, proposed exact truth, lived
consequence, protection, transition, usability, conflict, uncertainty, and
Commit/Cancel. Current accepted truth remains authoritative until settlement.

After commit, exact chronology and lived meaning update truthfully. Failed,
unchanged, deferred, and unresolved scopes remain explicit. Receipt and Undo
remain capability-gated. The originating period, day, Event, and focus context
restore where supported.

### Rejected Time patterns

Calendar-grid-first identity, AI briefing above a calendar, capacity dashboards,
availability heat maps, decorative waveforms, protection as a colored Event, red
overlap as sufficient conflict explanation, automatic recommendations from open
time, compressed full-week viewports, tiny type, and duplicate Today anatomy are
rejected.

## 8. VC-10 — Capture and Search Geometry Closure

Capture closure:

`VC10-CAPTURE-S01 — Full-Screen Adaptive Meaning Passage`

Applies within:

`AVF-CAPTURE-S07-R01 — Bounded Adaptive Meaning Fold`

Search closure:

`VC10-SEARCH-S01 — Full-Screen Semantic Command Passage`

Applies within:

`AVF-SEARCH-D07-R01 — Owner-Routed Semantic Command Field`

### Shared global geometry

Capture and Search are dock-invoked, temporary full-screen iPhone
presentations. They are not roots or tabs. Root crown and dock are hidden during
active use. Native dismissal, keyboard, focus, and safe-area behavior remain.
Both show a compact origin relationship and transfer domain mutation to the
canonical owner.

### Capture progression

```text
Express
-> Meaning
-> Clarify only when necessary
-> Review when consequential
-> Commit or owner transfer
-> Settlement
-> Return
```

Express is natural-language-first. No schema, command, object-type, or
destination picker precedes expression. Long content expands naturally.
Dictation and attachments remain absent until capability proof exists.

Interpretation is bounded, provisional, typography-led, and retains the original
expression. Clarification asks one targeted question only when safe continuation
is impossible. Capture does not become a chat transcript.

Consequential review identifies original intent, owner, current truth, proposed
meaning, change, consequence, protected meaning, uncertainty, Commit, and Cancel
or revise. When Capture cannot commit, it prepares and transfers; the owner
revalidates before settlement.

### Search progression

```text
Query
-> interpretation when needed
-> Find, Understand, or Prepare Action
-> owner transfer when required
-> Return
```

Find, Understand, and Act are semantic outcomes, not tabs. The query and Cancel
remain stable above the keyboard. Results are compact semantic rows with
identity, owner, current truth, material state, and match reason only when
non-obvious.

Understand is object-backed and may explain current truth, earlier or compared
truth, change, relationship, consequence, provenance/freshness, and limits.
Search does not become an AI answer feed.

Search may prepare a change and transfer to the owner. It may directly perform
only non-mutating operations such as Open, Navigate, Inspect, or Compare.

No result, read failure, unavailable index, stale index, permission limitation,
unsupported query, ambiguous object, and partial coverage remain distinct.

### Rejected global-system patterns

Chat bubbles, assistant avatars, detached command palettes, confidence
percentages, suggested-prompt filler, structure-first Capture, Search-owned
mutation, one-screen journey compression, generic AI gradients, glow, glass
result cards, root embedding, persistent dock during active presentation, and
bottom navigation are rejected.

## 9. VC-11 — You and Appearance Studio Closure

Selected root structure:

`VC11-YOU-D02 — Personal Control Passage`

Native substrate:

`VC11-YOU-D01 — Native Grouped Control List`

Required transformation:

`VC11-YOU-D06 — Explicit Adaptive Control Passage`

Applies within:

`AVF-YOU-D07-R02 — Local Personal Control Index`

### Root composition

The ordinary first viewport contains the compact You crown, Identity & Local
Data, Personalization, Privacy & Data, Appearance, and the beginning of
Notifications & Attention. Natural scrolling reveals Connections & Permissions,
Accessibility & Interaction, App Behavior, and About Ambitions.

The root does not compress every domain into one viewport.

### Current local/no-account truth

The current baseline has no Ambitions account. Do not show sign-in, account
recovery, cloud sync, device management, subscription, sign-out, or account
deletion.

A domain row may contain an SF Symbol, domain identity, one concise current-state
summary, material state when required, and native disclosure. Rows do not show
scores, dashboards, recommendations, security grades, privacy grades, or profile
completion.

Personalization distinguishes person-entered, confirmed, suggested, inferred,
uncertain, historical, superseded, and removed meaning. It is not an AI memory
list or personality analysis.

Privacy and permissions remain capability-based. Approved permission families
are Calendar, Reminders, Notifications, and local authentication where used.
Granted, Limited, Denied, Not requested, Restricted, Requires Settings, and
Unavailable remain distinct.

### Appearance Studio

Appearance is a focused You depth. Active customization is limited to System,
Light, Dark, approved action accents, and restrained violet-indigo as default.
One controlled live specimen may preview representative Ambitions anatomy. It is
not a theme gallery.

Appearance cannot alter root ownership, navigation, semantic colors, component
anatomy, material hierarchy, accessibility, motion grammar, or cross-root
coherence. Typography, density, material, atmosphere families, root-specific
themes, motion overrides, and cross-device appearance remain inactive.

Selection never reveals sensitive values. Reveal and rehide are deliberate, and
VoiceOver never announces hidden content.

### Rejected You patterns

Account heroes, avatars, social-profile layout, account or subscription portal,
`What Ambitions Knows`, AI memory lists, privacy scores, data dashboards,
settings-card grids, theme marketplaces, swatch walls, disabled speculative
controls, unsupported data actions, and all-settings compression are rejected.

## 10. VC-12 — Resilience Combined-State Closure

Locked closure:

`VC12-RESILIENCE-S01 — Contextual Combined-State Passage`

Applies within:

- `AVF-RECOVERY-S07-R01 — Contextual Truth and Settlement`
- `VC05-STATE-D04 — Semantic State Covenant`
- `VC05-STATE-D06 — Explicit Stress Scaffold`
- `AVF-A11Y-S07-R00 — Adaptive Semantic Continuity`

Synthesis inheritance:

- D03 — Contextual State Seam
- D04 — Dual-Layer Truth Passage
- D05 — Recovery Continuity Passage
- D06 — Explicit Adaptive State Stack
- D01 — native inline state for simple local conditions
- D02 — root qualification only for genuinely root-wide conditions

Core progression:

```text
preserve accepted truth
-> qualify narrowest truthful scope
-> explain consequence
-> retain valid operation
-> expose owned next action
-> settle truthfully
-> restore coherent context
```

### State dimensions and reading order

Interaction posture, truth status, and operational status remain independent.
Combined states read as canonical identity, current accepted truth, truth
qualification, operational status, consequence, preserved/restricted capability,
available action, owner/source, and settlement/return.

State expression occurs at the narrowest truthful scope: object, section, root,
or global. Root qualification is permitted only when a meaningful portion of the
root is affected, displayed truth could be misread, and local annotations are
insufficient. Only one root qualification dominates at a time.

### Contextual State Seam

The standard local geometry sits between identity and affected truth, accepted
and proposed state, current truth and source qualification, conflicting
participants, failed operation and retry context, or settlement and remaining
uncertainty. It uses concise language, spacing, restrained rule, optional SF
Symbol, controlled semantic color, and native progress only when active. It is
not a pill, badge, warning card, glow, or thick border.

### Truth, offline, conflict, and settlement

Accepted truth remains authoritative during preview, saving, pending, failure,
and recovery. Proposed truth remains provisional. A spinner never replaces the
accepted state.

Local-first operation continues normally when fully local. Where external truth
cannot be verified, last verified truth may remain with explicit source and
verification state; current external status is unknown or unverified. Queueing
or `will sync later` appears only when a real durable queue exists.

Conflict identifies all valid participants, owners, incompatibility,
consequence, protected meaning, resolution authority, and what Ambitions cannot
decide. No participant is declared the winner. Over-capacity remains distinct.

Saving stays local. Pending requires real persisted operation identity. Failure
preserves original intent, object, accepted truth, entered content, proposal,
unaffected scopes, correction or safe retry, owner, and return. Recovered is a
restrained transient qualification, not a celebration.

Partial settlement is permitted only for formally independent typed scopes.
Settlement reports Changed, Unchanged, Blocked, Failed, Still uncertain, or
typed Partial settlement. Receipt requires durable accountability. Undo requires
a real executable inverse.

Dominance follows semantic consequence: safety/privacy/integrity, conflict,
mutation affecting accepted truth, freshness/source/verification, recoverable
failure, interaction posture, then supporting evidence.

Recovery preserves the deepest truthful supported context. When exact
restoration is unavailable, return to the nearest stable owner and state what
was not preserved.

### Rejected resilience patterns

Global error themes, warning centers, status dashboards, root banners for local
failures, warning-card stacks, pill collections, toast cascades, spinner-only
mutation, generic `Something went wrong`, color-only conflict, offline as
universal failure, fabricated queues, premature accepted styling, ambiguous
partial success, unsupported Undo, success takeover, and context loss are
rejected.

- Today remains execution-oriented without becoming a task list or calendar.
- Goals remains Life-Area-led with Goal-owned pursuit depth.
- Time remains exact chronology and calendar ownership without duplicating
  Today.
- You remains settings-first, local, and no-account.

- Search remains retrieval-first and owner-routed.
- Resilience remains contextual, scope-accurate, and truth-preserving.

## 14. Architecture-sensitive assumptions

- One shell owner supplies four independent root paths, crown context, dock
  posture, global presentation, restoration, focus return, and external origin.
- Canonical identity and one mutation owner survive every projection.
- Today projections, Goal relationships, temporal meanings, Search results, and
  Capture interpretations are backed by canonical truth before display.
- Life Areas, Goals, Steps, Events, periods, settings domains, proposals, and
  recovery operations have stable identity where required.
- Owner transfer revalidates before mutation.
- All depicted truth and operation states exist as distinct runtime contracts.
- Calendar scales, recurrence, external calendars, notifications, permissions,
  Appearance choices, Receipt, Undo, partial settlement, and restoration remain
  capability-bounded.
- Accessibility transformations can recompose geometry without removing meaning.
- Direct-device proof remains required for keyboard, safe area, navigation,
  gesture competition, focus, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, RTL, localization,
  reduced effects, and reach.

These assumptions are not evidence that current source implements the target.
