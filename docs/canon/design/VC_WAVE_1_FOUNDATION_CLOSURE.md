## 4. VC-01 — Typography and Semantic Voices

Selected closure study: `VC01-TYPE-STUDY-D — Semantic Cadence`.

San Francisco remains the sole core Ambitions interface family.

### Semantic voices

- **Root identity:** SF Pro Display, Semibold, in a native large-title or title
  relationship. It is compact, left aligned, and never a ceremonial hero.
- **Focused-object identity:** SF Pro Display, Semibold, multiline before
  truncation, and more prominent than state, chronology, or action.
- **Object identity:** SF Pro Text, Medium or Semibold, stable across the
  canonical owner and its projections.
- **Current truth:** SF Pro Text, Regular, in a body relationship. It is the
  primary changing semantic voice.
- **Consequence:** SF Pro Text, Regular or Medium, in a body or callout
  relationship. Spacing provides the primary separation, and consequence is
  present only when material.
- **Exact chronology:** SF Pro Text with monospaced or tabular digits. It is
  exact without visually dominating object identity.
- **State and uncertainty:** SF Pro Text in a subheadline or footnote
  relationship, expressed as explicit language attached to what it qualifies.
  It never becomes a detached status-pill vocabulary.
- **Supporting evidence:** secondary semantic styles disclosed in proportion to
  consequence and compressed before identity, truth, state, consequence, or
  action.
- **Actions:** native control typography; no branded button typeface.

### Semantic cadence

The ordinary sequence is:

```text
identity
-> current truth
-> consequence
-> qualifying state
-> evidence
-> action or return
```

This is semantic ordering, not a mandatory fixed row template.

### Compression order

Compress in this order:

1. Repeated context
2. Repeated ownership
3. Secondary symbols
4. Supporting provenance
5. Secondary chronology
6. Preview breadth
7. Secondary relationships

Never compress away Place, Identity, Current truth, Applicable state,
Consequence, Required action, Conflict, or Return.

### Typography rejections and deferrals

Serif accents, New York, oversized editorial root titles, instrument
typography as a universal character, permanent accessibility scaffolding,
all-regular low-contrast hierarchy, custom tracking as brand identity,
microcopy-dependent layouts, and uppercase state systems are rejected as
active authority.

Exact sizes, line heights, tracking, final named Figma text styles, and exact
Dynamic Type mappings remain deferred.

## 5. VC-02 — Dual-Appearance System and Action Accent

Selected synthesis: `Mineral Relief Continuum`.

This reconciles `AVF-APPEARANCE-D01-R00 — Mineral Graphite Continuum` and
`AVF-APPEARANCE-D04-R00 — Sculpted Neutral Relief`. The synthesis name is a VC
closure label, not an active AVF direction ID.

The system uses D01's calm, matte, continuous environmental character and D04's
slightly stronger but localized articulated relief. It permits no theatrical
material behavior.

### Light appearance

- Mineral-neutral environmental plane, not stark productivity white.
- Deep graphite text and restrained dividers.
- Functional chrome is slightly clearer than content.
- Small tonal and structural articulation provides localized relief.
- No warm editorial parchment character.

### Dark appearance

- Deep graphite rather than pure black.
- Controlled luminance, with no glowing edges or violet environmental wash.
- Functional chrome is slightly clearer than content.
- Tonal transitions and structural edges provide localized relief.

Light and Dark preserve identical object anatomy, hierarchy, state meaning,
ownership, and accessibility transformation, with comparable relief intensity.

### Accent and material

Restrained violet-indigo is the default action accent for primary actionable
tint, selected-control emphasis, active input focus where native behavior
permits, current timeline position, proposed action path, and links.

The accent does not identify roots, carry semantic status by itself, color the
ambient page, create glow, or form decorative gradients.

Primary content remains opaque and matte. Relief is visible but restrained.
Functional material is limited to justified chrome and transient interaction,
and Reduce Transparency receives an authored opaque equivalent.

### Appearance rejections and deferrals

Purely flat D01 calibration, strongly sculpted D04 calibration, OLED Dark,
atmosphere families, photography-derived appearance, decorative gradients,
glow, glass content panels, and appearance-dependent hierarchy are rejected as
active authority.

Exact Light canvas values, Dark graphite values, violet-indigo values, relief
intensity, shadows, divider contrast, and opaque material substitutions remain
deferred.

## 6. VC-03 — Crown State Family

Selected primary study: `VC03-CROWN-D04 — Compact Semantic Stack`.

Required transformation: `VC03-CROWN-D06 — Adaptive Semantic Passage`.

### Root-browsing anatomy

The crown contains root identity, at most one concise contextual line when
materially useful, and native contextual actions at the trailing edge. Search
and Capture never appear in the crown.

The root crown is compact and left aligned in SF Pro Display Semibold. It has no
centered hero, large decorative root icon, metrics, recommendations, or status
collection.

### Postures

- **Scroll:** native inline title; secondary context collapses first; root
  identity remains explicit; contextual actions remain in native placement or
  overflow; no duplicate floating crown.
- **Focused object:** framework Back remains available; focused canonical
  identity becomes primary; navigation continuity communicates the root; one
  material state may appear only when it changes interpretation; the object
  owner supplies actions.
- **Editing:** focused identity, native Cancel, native Done or Save, and only a
  concise blocked or unsaved state when material. Consequential change moves to
  a separate review.
- **Consequential review:** crown identifies the affected object or operation,
  review posture, and native cancel or return. Detailed consequences remain in
  content.
- **Conflict:** object or context identity plus at most one concise conflict
  qualification. Full participants remain in content.
- **Recovery:** at most one material recovery qualification. Local failures
  remain local; a system-wide unsafe state uses a dedicated shell gate.

### Accessibility transformation

At large Dynamic Type sizes, remove the decorative symbol, remove repeated
context, move secondary actions to overflow, wrap identity, increase height,
stack context beneath identity, and preserve Back, Cancel, Save, or the required
action, in that order.

Native direction handling, Back ownership, identity/context/action reading
order, reachable editing and dismissal actions, and unobscured active input are
required. The dock adapts independently. The crown is functional separation,
not a content card.

Exact height, title/context spacing, toolbar spacing, tonal separation, and
transition duration remain deferred.

## 7. VC-04 — Edge Dock Posture Family

Selected primary study: `VC04-DOCK-D04 — Articulated Edge Tray`.

Required transformation: `VC04-DOCK-D06 — Adaptive Navigation Passage`.

The state family is Hidden, Peek, Expanded, and Adaptive labelled equivalent.

### Hidden and Peek

Hidden applies to full-screen Search, full-screen Capture, immersive focused
depth, unsafe gesture competition, and unsafe keyboard overlap. Commands remain
available only through truthful native accessibility or keyboard equivalents
where supported.

Peek is a narrow visible Material Seam with at least a 44-point interaction
envelope. It communicates availability, not repeated root identity, and is the
ordinary eligible root-browsing posture.

### Expanded

Expanded uses one compact Articulated Edge Tray: one coherent right-edge
surface that is neither full height, a sidebar, nor detached buttons. It
overlays instead of reserving a layout column and preserves most underlying
content.

The order is Today, Goals, Time, You, Search, Capture. The four roots form the
upper semantic group; Search and Capture form a separate lower group. Symbols
are always visible, labels are selectively compact, the selected root receives
localized articulated relief, violet-indigo may reinforce selection, and
selection never depends on color alone.

### Adaptive Navigation Passage

The required equivalent is used for large Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Voice
Control, Switch Control, Full Keyboard Access, long localization, RTL, keyboard
pressure, comprehension requirements, and lower-reach requirements.

It is wider, fully labelled, opaque, and uses larger targets while preserving
the same six commands, order, grouping, ownership, and single-dock identity.
It is not a second navigation system.

Symbol-only rail as the primary Expanded state, permanent labelled drawer,
full-height sidebar, detached floating controls, bottom tabs as fallback,
gesture-only access, and Search or Capture outside the dock are rejected.

Exact tray width, passage width, visible seam width, vertical position, control
spacing, expansion duration, inactivity timeout, and lower-reach geometry
remain deferred.

## 8. VC-05 — Shared Interaction-State Grammar

Selected primary study: `VC05-STATE-D04 — Semantic State Covenant`.

Required stress transformation: `VC05-STATE-D06 — Explicit Stress Scaffold`.

### State model and expression

Interaction posture, truth status, and operational status remain separate. A
selected object may also be stale, a focused object may also be pending, and an
editor may also contain conflict.

Use up to explicit language, typography, local geometry, and supporting color
or material, in that order. Language and structure remain sufficient when
color, depth, transparency, and motion are removed.

### Interaction postures

- **Resting:** integrated into the matte plane, no elevation by default, full
  identity and current truth readable.
- **Selected:** localized relief or structural edge, slightly stronger
  identity/truth hierarchy, context retained, optional restrained
  violet-indigo reinforcement, and no implication of editing or mutation
  ownership.
- **Focused:** primary spatial attention, focused title relationship, expanded
  supporting meaning, explicit origin and return.
- **Editing:** native field focus, native Cancel and Save or Done, accepted
  truth kept distinct, and separate review for consequential edits.
- **Sensitive reveal:** hidden state is explicit; reveal or authentication is
  deliberate; rehide remains available; selection alone never reveals.

### Mutation postures and outcomes

Preview shows current truth, proposed truth, change, consequence, protected
meaning, uncertainty or external effect, Commit, and Cancel in that order.

Saving identifies the operation and keeps current accepted truth authoritative
until commit publication. Pending appears only with durable operation identity
and a proven owner, persistence, and recovery contract.

- **Settled changed:** new truth becomes primary; relief recedes; Receipt and
  Undo appear only when capability-gated; no celebration.
- **Settled unchanged:** existing truth remains and the no-change outcome is
  explicit; no fake success.
- **Blocked:** no authority commit; current truth unchanged; exact prerequisite
  attached locally; only valid resolution actions.
- **Failed:** disclose whether truth changed and distinguish pre-commit from
  post-commit failure; preserve work where supported; provide one truthful
  recovery action.
- **Irreversible:** disclose exact scope before commit; imply no Undo.
- **Undo available:** place near the affected result or object, name the
  reversal, execute a new typed mutation, and retain history.

External, Stale, Unknown, and Historical remain explicit truth qualifiers.
Conflict uses a Coherence Seam with both claims visible, owner and invariant
explicit, no implied winner, and no warning-color-only expression. Recovery
keeps available truth and repair scope visible and promotes recovered truth
only after verification, without celebration.

### Combined-state order and stress transformation

Combined states read as identity; current authoritative truth; genuine
conflict; unknown or partial authority; failed or blocked operation; pending or
recovering operation; permission or authentication limitation; stale,
external, or offline qualification; historical context; supporting evidence.

Under accessibility and content pressure, relief becomes rule, spacing, or
explicit labelled structure; conflict becomes ordered comparison; preview
becomes a vertical current/proposed sequence; settlement becomes explicit
result grouping; recovery becomes an ordered passage; and required actions move
into reachable native regions.

Detached status pills, universal progress spinner, success celebration,
warning-card grid, toast cascade, color-only state, error replacement
container, universal Undo, and pending without ownership are removed.

Relief intensity, divider weight, state type metrics, semantic color values,
progress-indicator implementation, and exact transition timing remain deferred.

## 9. VC-06 — Foundational Visual Grammar

Selected primary study: `VC06-GRAMMAR-D04 — Articulated Native Grammar`.

Native substrate: `VC06-GRAMMAR-D01 — Native Minimal Substrate`.

Required stress transformation:
`VC06-GRAMMAR-D06 — Explicit Accessible Structure`.

### Material roles

- **Role 0 — Semantic Plane:** opaque, matte, continuous, stable,
  typography-led, and independent of blur or shadow. It is the primary-content
  default.
- **Role 1 — Local Articulation:** selection, focus, proposal, protection,
  pending, recovery, current timeline position, selected dock command, and
  sensitive boundary may use a small tonal shift, structural edge, local
  relief, controlled recess, divider emphasis, or increased spacing. It remains
  part of the surrounding plane and flattens under pressure.
- **Role 2 — Functional Transient Material:** crown, dock, Search, Capture,
  menus, popovers, sheets, consequence review, and temporary editing controls
  may use justified native material with an authored opaque Reduce
  Transparency equivalent.

### Shape, dividers, relief, and icons

Native controls retain native shapes. Routine content has no rounded container;
spacing, alignment, typography, and dividers come first. A boundary exists only
for an independent interaction or atomic operation. There is no universal
brand radius. Pills are reserved for native compact controls and true binary
choices.

Dividers express sequence, ownership boundary, temporal separation, state
transition, or meaningful grouping. A Coherence Seam remains distinct from a
standard divider.

Resting has no relief by default. Selected state receives slight local
articulation; focused state uses spatial and tonal separation; proposed truth
stays distinct from accepted truth; active chrome may use restrained elevation;
conflict is a discontinuity rather than warning elevation; and settlement
causes articulation to recede.

SF Symbols are the default. Monochrome or hierarchical rendering is preferred.
Fill or weight may support selection, but label, trait, and structure also
express it. A custom symbol requires a genuine canonical semantic gap.

### Color, motion, haptics, and spatial rhythm

Appearance environment, violet-indigo action accent, and immutable semantic
colors remain separate. Roots have no assigned colors, and every semantic
state has language, structure, and a non-color equivalent.

Allowed motion categories are micro feedback, local state transition, spatial
continuity, and sparse meaning transformation. Motion explains continuity or
causality. Pulsing, breathing glow, continuous floating, decorative parallax,
celebration, error shaking, long cinematic transitions, and motion on every
update are rejected.

Reduce Motion uses direct replacement or a short crossfade, no required scale
effect, static current/proposed relationships, static conflict interruption,
and no semantic loss.

Haptics are limited to selection, consequential confirmation, warning
threshold, and genuine interaction error. Decorative or continuous haptics are
rejected.

Spatial rhythm uses a compact progression primarily based on 4, 8, 12, 16, 20,
24, and 32. Larger separation may use 40, 48, and 64. This is a philosophy, not
a final token set.

The minimum interaction target is locked at 44 by 44 points.

Native control radius, compact functional radius, and focused
transient-surface radius define the radius philosophy without exact radii.
Shadow is optional and limited to floating chrome or temporary presentation;
tonal separation and structural edge come first.

Exact type metrics, colors, violet-indigo values, semantic colors, blur or
opacity, divider weights, radii, shadows, motion duration or easing, haptic
APIs, Figma variables, and component tokens or APIs remain deferred.

  Today, Search, Capture, and cross-root views.
