## Typography

`VC01-TYPE-STUDY-D — Semantic Cadence` is closed and active. San Francisco is
the sole core Ambitions interface family.

- Root identity uses compact, left-aligned SF Pro Display Semibold in a native
  large-title or title relationship and never becomes a ceremonial hero.
- Focused-object identity uses SF Pro Display Semibold and wraps before
  truncation.
- Canonical object identity uses SF Pro Text Medium or Semibold and remains
  stable across owners and projections.
- Current truth uses SF Pro Text Regular in a body relationship.
- Material consequence uses SF Pro Text Regular or Medium in a body or callout
  relationship, separated primarily by spacing.
- Exact chronology uses SF Pro Text with monospaced or tabular digits without
  outranking identity.
- State and uncertainty use attached, explicit subheadline or footnote
  language, never a detached status-pill vocabulary.
- Supporting evidence compresses before identity, truth, state, consequence,
  action, or return.
- Actions retain native control typography.

The ordinary semantic cadence is identity, current truth, consequence,
qualifying state, evidence, then action or return. It is ordering, not a fixed
row template. Compression removes repeated context and ownership, secondary
symbols, provenance, chronology, preview breadth, and secondary relationships
before it removes Place, Identity, Current truth, Applicable state,
Consequence, Required action, Conflict, or Return.

New York and every other serif are absent from the active baseline. Historical
serif explorations remain `SUPERSEDED`. Exact sizes, line heights, tracking,
named Figma text styles, and Dynamic Type mappings remain deferred. No custom
tracking, all-regular low-contrast hierarchy, uppercase state system, branded
button typeface, or microcopy-dependent hierarchy is active.

## Appearance and accent

`Mineral Relief Continuum` is the closed VC-02 synthesis. It combines the calm,
matte, continuous environment of `AVF-APPEARANCE-D01-R00` with the slightly
stronger localized articulation of `AVF-APPEARANCE-D04-R00`, without theatrical
material behavior. This name is a VC synthesis label, not an active AVF ID.

The only active appearance choices are System, Light, and Dark. System follows
the operating-system Light or Dark selection; it is not a third palette. Light
uses a mineral-neutral plane rather than stark productivity white, deep
graphite text, restrained dividers, clearer functional chrome, and small tonal
or structural articulation. Dark uses deep graphite rather than pure black,
controlled luminance, no glowing edge or violet environmental wash, clearer
functional chrome, and localized tonal or structural relief.

Light and Dark preserve identical object anatomy, hierarchy, state meaning,
ownership, and accessibility transformation with comparable relief intensity.
OLED Dark, atmosphere families, photo-derived atmosphere, sensory themes,
root-specific themes, text-comfort modes, density controls, typography controls,
material controls, and cross-device appearance are not active.

Restrained violet-indigo is the default action accent. It may support primary
actionable tint, selected-control emphasis, active input focus where native
behavior permits, current timeline position, a proposed action path, and links.
It never identifies roots, replaces a semantic color, communicates state alone,
colors the ambient page, creates glow, or creates a decorative gradient.
Semantic colors are immutable and independent from appearance accents. Existing
accent candidates remain `DEFERRED_CANDIDATE_REQUIRING_OWNER_REVIEW`; exact
violet-indigo and candidate values are not fixed by this contract.

Purely flat D01 calibration, strongly sculpted D04 calibration, OLED Dark,
atmosphere families, photography-derived appearance, decorative gradients,
glow, glass content panels, and appearance-dependent hierarchy are rejected as
active authority.

## Crown state family

`VC03-CROWN-D04 — Compact Semantic Stack` is the closed primary study and
`VC03-CROWN-D06 — Adaptive Semantic Passage` is its required transformation.

At root depth, the crown contains root identity, at most one concise contextual
line when materially useful, and native trailing contextual actions. Search and
Capture never appear in the crown. The crown is compact, left aligned, and free
of centered heroes, decorative root icons, metrics, recommendations, and status
collections.

On scroll, context collapses before identity and secondary actions move to
native overflow. At focused depth, framework Back remains available, canonical
object identity becomes primary, and the object owner supplies actions. Editing
preserves native Cancel and Save or Done. Consequential review leaves detailed
consequence in content. Conflict and recovery add at most one concise material
qualification.

Under large text or content pressure, decorative symbol and repeated context
leave first, secondary actions move to overflow, identity wraps, the crown grows
and stacks, and Back, Cancel, Save, or the required action survives. Native
direction, reading order, keyboard avoidance, and focus ownership remain. The
crown is restrained functional separation, never a content card. Exact crown
geometry, spacing, tone, and timing remain deferred.

## Edge dock posture family

`VC04-DOCK-D04 — Articulated Edge Tray` is the closed primary study and
`VC04-DOCK-D06 — Adaptive Navigation Passage` is its required equivalent
transformation.

The state family is Hidden, Peek, Expanded, and Adaptive labelled equivalent.
Peek is the ordinary eligible root-browsing posture and exposes a narrow
Material Seam with at least a 44-point interaction envelope. Expanded uses one
compact right-edge Articulated Edge Tray that overlays rather than reserving a
column. It preserves this exact order and grouping: Today, Goals, Time, You as
the upper root group; Search and Capture as the lower global group. Symbols stay
visible, labels are selectively compact, and localized relief plus optional
violet-indigo reinforces a selection that never depends on color alone.

The Adaptive Navigation Passage becomes wider, fully labelled, opaque, and
larger-targeted under Dynamic Type, assistive input, localization, RTL,
keyboard, comprehension, or lower-reach pressure. It preserves the same six
commands, order, grouping, ownership, and single-dock identity.

Symbol-only primary expansion, a permanent drawer, full-height sidebar,
detached controls, bottom-tab fallback, gesture-only access, and Search or
Capture outside the dock are rejected. Exact tray, passage, seam, vertical,
spacing, reach, and timing calibration remains deferred.

## Shared interaction-state grammar

`VC05-STATE-D04 — Semantic State Covenant` is the closed primary study and
`VC05-STATE-D06 — Explicit Stress Scaffold` is its required stress
transformation.

Interaction posture, truth status, and operational status remain separate. Use
explicit language, typography, local geometry, then supporting color or
material. Meaning remains sufficient without color, depth, transparency, or
motion.

Resting, Selected, Focused, Editing, and Sensitive reveal have distinct
postures without transferring mutation ownership. Preview orders current truth,
proposed truth, change, consequence, protected meaning, uncertainty or external
effect, Commit, then Cancel. Saving leaves accepted truth authoritative until
publication. Pending requires durable operation identity and proven owner,
persistence, and recovery.

Changed, unchanged, blocked, failed, irreversible, and Undo-available outcomes
remain explicit and non-celebratory. External, Stale, Unknown, and Historical
remain distinct qualifiers. Conflict keeps both claims, owner, and invariant
visible without implying a winner; recovery promotes repaired truth only after
verification.

Under stress, relief becomes rule, spacing, or labelled structure; conflict
becomes ordered comparison; preview becomes a vertical current/proposed
sequence; settlement becomes an explicit result group; recovery becomes an
ordered passage; and required actions move into reachable native regions.
Detached status pills, universal spinners, celebration, warning-card grids,
toast cascades, color-only state, replacement error containers, universal Undo,
and pending without ownership are rejected. Exact state metrics and calibration
remain deferred.

## Material, spacing, and motion

`VC06-GRAMMAR-D04 — Articulated Native Grammar` is the closed primary study.
It retains `VC06-GRAMMAR-D01 — Native Minimal Substrate` and requires
`VC06-GRAMMAR-D06 — Explicit Accessible Structure` under stress.

Role 0 is an opaque, matte, continuous, stable, typography-led Semantic Plane
with no blur or shadow dependency. Role 1 is Local Articulation for selection,
focus, proposal, protection, pending, recovery, current timeline position,
selected dock command, and sensitive boundary; it remains part of the plane and
flattens under pressure. Role 2 is Functional Transient Material for crown,
dock, Search, Capture, menus, popovers, sheets, consequence review, and
temporary editing controls. Reduce Transparency receives an authored opaque
equivalent.

Native controls retain native shapes. Routine content has no rounded container;
spacing, alignment, typography, and dividers come first. There is no universal
brand radius. Pills are limited to native compact controls and true binary
choices. Dividers express sequence, ownership, time, transition, or meaningful
grouping; the Coherence Seam remains distinct. Resting content has no default
relief, selected and focused states receive local articulation, proposed truth
stays distinct, conflict is discontinuity rather than warning elevation, and
settlement recedes.

SF Symbols are the default, preferably monochrome or hierarchical. Selection
also requires label, trait, and structure. Custom symbols require a genuine
canonical semantic gap. Appearance environment, violet-indigo action accent,
and immutable semantic colors remain separate; roots receive no colors and
semantic state always has a non-color equivalent.

The spatial-rhythm philosophy uses 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, and 32 as its compact
progression, with 40, 48, and 64 available for larger separation. It is not a
final token set. The minimum interaction target is locked at 44 by 44 points.
Radii remain native-control, compact-functional, or focused-transient roles
without exact values. Shadows are optional and limited to floating chrome or
temporary presentation; tonal separation and structural edge come first.

Motion is limited to micro feedback, local state transition, spatial
continuity, and sparse meaning transformation that explains continuity or
causality. Reduce Motion uses direct replacement or a short crossfade and
preserves static current/proposed and conflict meaning. Pulsing, breathing glow,
continuous floating, decorative parallax, celebration, error shaking, long
cinematic transitions, and motion on every update are rejected. Haptics are
limited to selection, consequential confirmation, warning threshold, and
genuine interaction error; decorative and continuous haptics are rejected.

Exact type metrics, colors, accent and semantic values, blur, opacity, divider
weights, radii, shadows, motion values, haptic APIs, Figma variables, component
tokens, and component APIs remain deferred.

## Capability and architecture boundary

Visual closure may specify the approved target architecture before runtime
implementation, but it must label target-only behavior and may not depict it as
current functionality. The following remain architecture-sensitive:

- one shell owner, independent paths, dock posture, gesture arbitration,
  restoration tiers, stale-target fallback, focus return, and external origin;
- stable Life Area, Event, Schedule Placement, Today admission, and Receipt
  identity;
- current/proposed/accepted/external/stale truth separation;
- canonical-owner mutation, settlement, Receipt, and executable Undo;
- Search/Capture owner transfer;
- operation-specific pending/recovery data;
- system accessibility, focus, localization, RTL, and device proof.

Unsupported capability is omitted or shown only as a clearly labelled target,
unavailable state, or capability gate. A placeholder, type, fixture, historical
frame, or disabled row is not capability evidence.
