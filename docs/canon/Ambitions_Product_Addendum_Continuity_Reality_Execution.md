## Doctrine

### Top-Level Surface Questions

Each top-level surface must answer one dominant question:

- `Today`: What matters now?
- `Goals`: Where am I headed?
- `Plan`: How does this week hold together?
- `Insights`: What am I learning?
- `Profile / Trust`: How is my system configured?

This is doctrine, not sample copy.

### Mobile Provenance

On mobile, provenance should feel calm and legible rather than breadcrumb-heavy.

Primary provenance patterns:

- `Origin Chip`
- `Context Ribbon`
- `Return Stack Memory`

These are product-context signals, not desktop-style navigation chrome.
