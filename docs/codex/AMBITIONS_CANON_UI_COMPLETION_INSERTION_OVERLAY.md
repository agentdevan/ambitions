1. Mission Control becomes top-level.
2. A sixth top-level tab is added.
3. Plan returns as a top-level destination.
4. Capture becomes feed, inbox, chatbot, category board, or plus-tab UI.
5. Today becomes generic task list or calendar timeline.
6. Time becomes calendar clone, agenda, or schedule dashboard.
7. Goals becomes KPI dashboard, habit tracker, astrology, or ranked life score.
8. You becomes social profile, family hub, or admin console.
9. Continuity signals appear outside Context Crown, Meridian Edge, Continuity Dock, or Trust Seam.
10. Trust becomes chatbot/AI coach.
11. Automation exceeds Manual / Suggest / Preview Reflow without later explicit canon and proof.
12. Primary object depends on visual-only meaning.
13. Reduced Motion removes essential meaning.
14. Exact token values are claimed final without visual QA.
15. Visual Green/flagship/award-caliber claim is made without screenshot or rendered-preview proof.
16. Release, public accessibility, physical-device, TestFlight, App Store, legal/privacy, or production readiness is claimed without matching evidence.
