## Purpose

Motion and haptics must make Ambitions feel native and understandable, not decorative or gimmicky.

## Gate Rules

- Motion must orient, confirm, or reduce uncertainty.
- Motion must not be decorative.
- Haptics must be native, optional, user-initiated, and meaningful.
- Transitions should preserve object continuity.
- Reduce Motion must be handled with static/non-motion equivalents.
- Celestial restraint means orientation and atmosphere, not spectacle.
- No fake AI glow, dopamine loop, or gamified reward motion.

## Required Evidence

Reports must name the motion trigger, purpose, fallback, accessibility implication, haptic class if any, and rendered evidence status.

## Red

Decorative motion, unexplained motion, missing Reduce Motion equivalent, haptic reward patterns, or motion that hides state changes.
