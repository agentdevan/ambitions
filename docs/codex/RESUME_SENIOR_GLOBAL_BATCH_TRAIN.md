## Senior First Duty

Before selecting ordinary next-batch work, inspect `docs/codex/MODEL_TIER_DEFERRAL_LEDGER.md`.

Resolve open deferrals first when they are:

- blocking
- release-relevant
- required for truthful closeout
- required before a final visual, accessibility, legal/privacy, device, handoff, or release-claim gate
- attached to the same batch family as the next eligible batch

Senior may leave a non-blocking deferral open only when the ledger records why continuation remains safe.

## Senior Actions For Deferrals

For each deferral, Senior must choose one:

- Close: perform the batch or proof and append evidence
- Accept deferred state: record why it remains non-blocking and when it must return
- Split: create Mini-safe implementation sub-batches plus one senior judgment gate
- Supersede: cite newer repo evidence that makes the deferral obsolete
- Red: stop when the deferral reveals unresolved architecture/product/privacy/legal/release/device risk

Senior may not erase Mini deferral history.

## Senior-Only Gate Ownership

Senior owns:

- source-truth conflict resolution not decidable from repo evidence
- roadmap/order reconciliation
- founder acceptance or founder objection handling
- final visual QA and coherence review
- final release-claim safety review
- public accessibility conformance wording
- legal/privacy compliance wording
- App Store, TestFlight, signed archive, provisioning, export, or device readiness wording
- route/raw-value compatibility retirement
- persistence/schema migration or data-loss risk judgment
- sync/cloud/account/backend runtime judgment
- hosted AI/model runtime/tool-bus judgment
- safety, privacy, memory, source, or professional-boundary ambiguity
- performance/battery safety claim evaluation
- deletion, rename, or cleanup with incomplete owner proof
- repeated same-root failure after scoped Mini attempts

## Per-Batch Execution Contract

For each Senior batch:

1. confirm repo state
2. read source truth and deferral ledger
3. name exact allowed and forbidden files before edits
4. resolve blocking deferrals first
5. execute the smallest safe slice or stop Red
6. run focused validation first
7. run broader proof checks where scoped
8. update reports, registry, context, current-state, and ledger as needed
9. record model tier and senior gates in the report
10. commit with a batch-specific or deferral-resolution message
11. select the next eligible batch from repo evidence
12. continue until complete, accepted Yellow, or Red

## No-Claim Boundary

Senior may judge more than Mini, but it still cannot claim physical-device proof, public accessibility conformance, App Store/TestFlight readiness, legal/privacy approval, signed release readiness, or human approval without matching evidence.
