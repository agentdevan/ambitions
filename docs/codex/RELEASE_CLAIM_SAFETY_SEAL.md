Status: Active claim boundary
Date: 2026-05-03

This seal defines what Ambitions may and may not claim after the DAV closeout
and global train reliability additions.

## Allowed Claims

- DAV01-DAV15 completed with accepted Yellow.
- Preview fixture inventory exists.
- DAV/PXEQ/SIG/Transformative Motion evidence reports exist.
- Listed validation commands passed when the report names the exact command.
- Build passed only for the specific command and date recorded in evidence.

## Not Allowed Unless Separately Proven

- Fully visually regression tested on device.
- Battery safe under Instruments.
- VoiceOver fully verified by human review.
- Full accessibility compliance.
- Production release ready.
- TestFlight ready.
- App Store ready.
- Apple Design Award status or any factual award-caliber claim.
- Privacy certified or legal signoff.

## Proof Requirements

Human/device/screenshot proof must name the device or tool, OS when relevant,
scenario, output path or reviewer evidence, and result. Source evidence,
simulator builds, previews, or future-canon docs do not substitute for that
proof.
