# Batch 37 — Post-2.0 Hardening 03 / Secondary Surface Productization

## Status

Active

## Goal

Clarify and strengthen the product roles of secondary surfaces so Plan, Insights, Profile, and any other truthfully lagging shell areas move closer to the maturity bar of Today, Goals, and Goal Detail.

This batch is the third step of the post-2.0 whole-repo/app hardening wave. It follows shell stabilization and external/trust validation so weaker surfaces can be productized on top of settled truth rather than shifting structure.

## In Scope

- product-role clarification for Plan, Insights, and Profile
- productization of lagging shell surfaces that truthfully belong here
- maturity-gap reduction between flagship surfaces and weaker top-level surfaces
- shell-surface coherence needed before a later UI/UX excellence wave

## Out Of Scope

- shell IA correction that belongs in Batch 35
- external/trust validation that belongs in Batch 36
- broad visual redesign
- speculative new tabs or new product programs
- release-readiness consolidation work that belongs in Batch 38

## Dependency Rules

- do not start this batch until Batch 35 and Batch 36 are stable
- productize weaker shell surfaces only after shell truth and external truth are settled
- keep the work focused on product-role maturity rather than net-new intelligence capability
- keep the later UI/UX excellence wave out of this batch

## Exit Criteria

- Plan, Insights, and Profile have clear and truthful product roles
- lagging shell surfaces no longer feel merely implemented relative to Today, Goals, and Goal Detail
- shell-wide product-role confusion is materially reduced
- the app is structurally ready for a later UI/UX excellence wave without mixing redesign into this hardening batch

## Validation

- docs/control-file truth checks for touched planning files
- targeted shell-surface verification appropriate to the eventual implementation scope
- do not mark this batch completed until secondary-surface roles are validated truthfully

## Completion Rule

Batch 37 is complete only when the app's secondary surfaces have truthful, stronger product roles and the shell no longer depends on a few flagship surfaces carrying the product alone.
