Define the Weekly Life Sweep ritual as Found Life source truth. The ritual
helps the user become found again without shame, dashboards, scores, feeds, or
silent automation.

- Skipping, parking, and recovery are non-shaming.

  TestFlight, physical-device, or public accessibility claim.
