# Ambitions Beta Feedback Forms

Status: Draft
Purpose: Structured qualitative beta evaluation

## First-session form

### Product comprehension

1. What do you think Ambitions is for?
2. Did you understand what to do first?
3. Did onboarding make sense?
4. Did the app feel different from a normal productivity app?

### Goal setup

5. Was it easy to create your first goal?
6. Did your goal feel connected to the rest of the app?

### Start Here

7. Did Start Here feel believable?
8. Did the recommendation feel grounded in your real life?
9. Did you understand why the step was recommended?

### Trust

10. Did the app feel trustworthy enough for personal life information?
11. Did privacy language feel understandable?
12. Did anything feel manipulative or artificial?

### Overall

13. What confused you most?
14. What felt most valuable?
15. Would you open Ambitions again tomorrow?

## Day-three form

1. Did Ambitions continue to feel useful?
2. Did recommendations still make sense?
3. Did the app adapt when your day changed?
4. Did recovery behavior feel supportive?
5. Did you trust the system more or less over time?
6. What made you hesitate to return?

## Week-one form

1. Did Ambitions help your goals feel more achievable?
2. Did the app help connect goals to daily execution?
3. Did Start Here remain useful?
4. Did the app feel distinct from other productivity tools?
5. Would you pay for this product?
6. What would stop you from continuing to use it?

## Internal evaluation guidance

Red if:

- users do not understand the product
- users describe it as generic productivity software
- recommendations feel random
- trust feels weak
- onboarding fails repeatedly

Yellow if:

- users mostly understand the product but wording needs refinement
- recommendations are mixed but improving
- trust exists but privacy messaging needs clarity

Green if:

- users quickly understand the system
- recommendations feel believable
- users describe the app as distinct
- users trust the product enough to continue
- users want to return tomorrow
