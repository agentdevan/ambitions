# Ambitions App Store Preview Video Script

Status: Draft
Purpose: App Store preview narrative planning

## Video direction

The preview video should feel:

- calm
- premium
- grounded
- native to iPhone
- emotionally believable

Avoid:

- aggressive editing
- hype energy
- productivity clichés
- flashy AI visuals
- dashboard montages

## Opening frame

Dark graphite iPhone surface.

Reality Meridian visible.

Start Here subtly illuminated.

## Opening text

Finally know how to accomplish your goals.

## Sequence 1 — The problem

Show:

- fragmented planning
- overloaded schedules
- scattered goals
- unclear priorities

Transition calmly into Ambitions.

## Sequence 2 — Start Here

Show:

- Today
- Reality Meridian
- Start Here
- grounded recommendation
- connected context

Text:

Know what matters now.

## Sequence 3 — Goals become steps

Show:

- Constellation Atlas
- meaningful goal
- connected grounded steps
- continuity structure

Text:

Goals become grounded daily steps.

## Sequence 4 — Built around real life

Show:

- LifeShape Field
- protected time
- changing availability
- adaptive shaping

Text:

Built around real life.

## Sequence 5 — Recovery

Show:

- closure states
- adaptive recovery
- continuity preserved

Text:

Real life changes. Ambitions adapts.

## Sequence 6 — Trust

Show:

- User System Profile
- privacy/trust settings
- calm explanations

Text:

Private by design.

## Final frame

Return to Today.

Subtle movement around Reality Meridian.

Text:

Start turning your goals into daily progress.

## Production rules

- Use real UI only
- Preserve native iPhone realism
- Avoid fake prototype visuals
- Use restrained motion
- Prioritize readability
- Preserve canonical terminology
- Avoid voiceover hype narration
- Keep pacing calm and confident
