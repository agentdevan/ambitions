# Ambitions Support Site Outline

Status: Draft
Purpose: Launch support-site structure and trust surface planning

## Support-site goals

The support site should:

- help users understand Ambitions
- reinforce trust
- explain onboarding and setup
- reduce App Review confusion
- provide privacy clarity
- support beta and launch users

The support experience should feel calm, premium, and native to the product.

## Primary site sections

1. Homepage
2. Getting Started
3. Privacy and Trust
4. FAQ
5. Contact Support
6. Export and Delete
7. Release Notes

## Homepage

### Headline

Finally know how to accomplish your goals.

### Supporting body

Ambitions helps turn goals into grounded daily steps that fit real life.

### Core sections

- What Ambitions is
- How Start Here works
- Goal-to-step continuity
- Time shaping
- Recovery-aware execution
- Private-by-design trust

## Getting Started

### Purpose

Help first-time users understand:

- what Ambitions does
- how setup works
- why goals come first
- how recommendations work
- how time shaping works
- how recovery works

### Required guides

- Creating your first goal
- Understanding Start Here
- Setting availability and schedule context
- Closing and recovering steps
- Understanding recommendations

## Privacy and Trust

### Purpose

Communicate trust plainly and clearly.

### Required sections

- Privacy overview
- Data handling explanation
- Permission explanations
- Export and deletion guidance
- App Privacy disclosure alignment
- Local-first trust language

### Required tone

- plain-English
- calm
- premium
- understandable

Avoid:

- fear marketing
- security theater
- aggressive anti-AI rhetoric

## FAQ

### Suggested questions

- What is Ambitions?
- How is this different from a productivity app?
- What is Start Here?
- How do goals become daily steps?
- Does Ambitions track habits or streaks?
- What happens when plans change?
- What permissions does Ambitions use?
- How private is my information?
- Can I export or delete my data?
