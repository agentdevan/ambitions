# Ambitions Launch Checklist

Status: Draft
Purpose: Pre-submission launch coordination and release preparation

## Product readiness

Before launch verify:

- Today feels grounded and understandable
- Goals connect clearly to daily steps
- Start Here feels believable
- Time shaping feels useful
- Recovery behavior preserves continuity
- The app does not feel generic
- Product language is canonical and consistent

## Visual readiness

Verify:

- premium native iPhone feel
- graphite/OLED visual cohesion
- Dynamic Type support
- VoiceOver order
- Reduce Motion support
- safe-area correctness
- visual consistency across destinations
- no placeholder states
- no broken empty states
- no inconsistent naming

## Privacy and trust readiness

Verify:

- Privacy Policy exists
- App Privacy disclosures match implementation
- permission prompts have rationale copy
- export/delete flows exist or are clearly documented
- no unsupported privacy claims exist
- trust settings are understandable

## App Review readiness

Verify:

- App Review notes are prepared
- screenshots reflect real UI
- onboarding matches functionality
- category positioning is understandable
- no unsupported claims exist
- permission explanations are clear

## Screenshot readiness

Verify:

- screenshots use real UI
- visual hierarchy is consistent
- typography is readable
- screenshot messaging is coherent
- screenshots feel premium and calm
- screenshots avoid generic productivity patterns

## TestFlight readiness

Verify:

- onboarding is understandable
- first goal setup works
- Start Here appears correctly
- recommendation explanations exist where needed
- recovery flows function
- major crashes are resolved
- beta feedback collection is operational

## Support readiness

Verify:

- support site exists
- privacy page exists
- onboarding help exists
- FAQ exists
- contact path exists
- export/delete instructions exist

## Release governance

Red if:

- users do not understand the product
- recommendations feel random
- privacy language is misleading
- the app feels generic
- onboarding fails
- major crashes remain
- accessibility regressions exist
- App Review risk is unresolved

Yellow if:

- launch quality is close but incomplete
- wording refinement is still needed
- visual polish is near-final
- onboarding clarity still needs iteration
- some recommendation quality gaps remain

Green only if:

- product positioning is coherent
- onboarding is understandable
- goal-to-step continuity feels believable
- trust feels credible
- visual quality feels premium
- accessibility is acceptable
- TestFlight feedback supports launch confidence
- App Review package is complete

## Final launch rule

Do not launch because the app is functional.

Launch only when the experience feels trustworthy, coherent, differentiated, premium, and emotionally stable.
