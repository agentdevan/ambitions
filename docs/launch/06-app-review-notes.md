# Ambitions App Review Notes

Status: Draft
Purpose: App Store Review preparation and reviewer clarity

## Reviewer summary

Ambitions is a Personal Life Operating System focused on helping users organize goals, schedules, commitments, and grounded daily execution.

The app helps users understand what they should work on today based on their goals, schedules, and changing life conditions.

Recommendations are user-controlled and intended for personal planning and organization.

## Product category explanation

Ambitions should not be interpreted as:

- medical advice
- therapy
- mental health treatment
- financial advice
- legal advice
- emergency guidance
- a chatbot companion

The app is a personal planning and execution system.

## Privacy explanation

Ambitions is designed for personal life information including goals, schedules, and daily execution.

The app should communicate privacy behavior clearly and keep App Privacy disclosures aligned with implementation truth.

## Permissions explanation

### Calendar access

Used to help shape recommendations around schedule commitments.

### Notifications

Used to support continuity and grounded daily execution.

### Reminders

Used to help users connect existing commitments into the Ambitions system.

## Reviewer testing path

Recommended reviewer path:

1. Create a goal
2. Add schedule or availability context
3. Open Today
4. Review Start Here
5. Open a recommended step
6. Test completion and closure behavior
7. Review privacy and trust settings

## Important product concepts

### Start Here

A recommendation surface intended to help users understand what fits now and why.

### Reality Meridian

The primary daily orientation surface.

### Recovery-aware behavior

The app is designed so progress can continue even when plans change.

## Trust and safety boundaries

Ambitions should not:

- imply guaranteed outcomes
- pressure users through streak systems
- create manipulative urgency
- present itself as therapy or counseling
- imply human coaching exists when it does not

## Reviewer clarification guidance

If reviewers are confused about category positioning, clarify that:

- Ambitions is a personal planning and execution product
- the app focuses on grounded daily organization
- recommendations are informational and user-controlled
- the app is not a chatbot or conversational AI product
