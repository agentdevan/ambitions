# Ambitions Privacy Policy Draft

Status: Early draft
Purpose: Plain-English privacy foundation

> This document is not final legal advice. Final public policy language should be reviewed against actual implementation behavior and applicable legal requirements before release.

## Introduction

Ambitions is designed for deeply personal life information including goals, schedules, commitments, daily planning, and progress.

Trust is foundational to the product.

This policy explains the categories of information Ambitions may handle, how the product should communicate privacy behavior, and the principles guiding the experience.

## Core privacy philosophy

Private by design.

Ambitions should be built to keep personal life information understandable, controllable, and aligned with user expectations.

## Information categories

Depending on enabled features and permissions, Ambitions may handle:

- goals
- schedules
- reminders
- daily steps
- availability information
- closure and recovery history
- preferences and settings
- notification preferences

## Permissions

Ambitions may request permission for:

### Calendar access

To help shape recommendations around schedule commitments.

### Notifications

To support continuity and grounded daily execution.

### Reminders

To help connect existing commitments into the Ambitions system.

## Data control

Ambitions should support:

- export paths
- deletion paths
- reset paths
- understandable privacy explanations

## Trust boundaries

Ambitions should not:

- sell personal life information
- create manipulative urgency systems
- misrepresent recommendation logic
- imply therapy or medical treatment
- overstate privacy guarantees

## App Privacy alignment

Public privacy disclosures, onboarding language, support documentation, and App Store metadata must remain aligned with actual implementation behavior.

## Future sections required before public launch

Before App Store submission, this policy should eventually include:

- legal entity information
- contact information
- retention explanations
- CloudKit/iCloud behavior if enabled
- third-party dependency disclosures
- regional legal compliance sections
- deletion timing expectations
- export instructions
- children/minimum age language
- policy update process
