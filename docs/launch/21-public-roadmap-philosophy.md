# Ambitions Public Roadmap Philosophy

Status: Draft
Purpose: Public roadmap guardrails

## Philosophy

Ambitions should communicate product direction carefully and coherently.

The roadmap should reinforce trust, quality, continuity, and long-term product identity.

## The roadmap should not become

- a feature-voting board
- a startup hype feed
- a productivity feature dump
- an AI wishlist
- a pressure mechanism

## Public themes

Roadmap communication should reinforce:

- grounded execution
- goal-to-day continuity
- real-life adaptation
- recovery-aware systems
- local-first trust
- premium iPhone-native quality

## Communication tone

Preferred:

- calm
- premium
- deliberate
- realistic

Avoid:

- exaggerated promises
- shipping hype
- speculative AI claims
- roadmap inflation

## Suggested roadmap structure

### Now

Current launch-quality priorities.

### Next

Near-term grounded improvements.

### Exploring

Thoughtful future directions without promises.

## Strategic emphasis

Before aggressive feature expansion prioritize:

- recommendation quality
- onboarding clarity
- trust
- accessibility
- stability
- category comprehension

## Final roadmap rule

The roadmap should make Ambitions feel more coherent and trustworthy, not larger or noisier.
