# Ambitions TestFlight Recruitment Plan

Status: Draft
Purpose: Recruit beta testers who can validate category comprehension, goal-to-step believability, trust, and return intent.

## Primary recruitment target

Overwhelmed goal-setters.

These are people who have meaningful goals but struggle to translate them into realistic daily action.

## Ideal tester profile

Recruit people who:

- have at least one meaningful personal or professional goal
- have tried planners, calendars, reminders, or productivity tools before
- struggle with consistency after life changes
- care about privacy and trust
- use iPhone as a primary device
- can give specific feedback instead of only general impressions

## Avoid over-indexing on

- people who only want feature quantity
- people who expect a chatbot
- people who only evaluate visual polish
- people who want habit streaks or gamification
- users unwilling to complete onboarding

## Recruitment message

Ambitions is a new iPhone-first Personal Life Operating System designed to help people turn meaningful goals into grounded daily steps that fit real life.

I’m looking for beta testers who have real goals, messy schedules, and honest feedback.

The first beta will test whether Ambitions helps users understand what to do today, trust the system, and believe their goals are more achievable.

## Short invite

I’m beta testing Ambitions, a private iPhone life system that helps turn goals into realistic daily progress. I’m looking for feedback from people with real goals and imperfect days.

## Tester expectations

Ask testers to:

- complete onboarding
- create one meaningful goal
- use Start Here
- open or close a recommended step
- return after at least one real-life interruption
- submit first-session and day-three feedback

## Cohort structure

### Cohort A — Close trusted testers

Purpose: severe confusion, crash, and language detection.

Size: 5–10 people.

### Cohort B — Goal-driven users

Purpose: goal-to-step believability and return intent.

Size: 15–25 people.

### Cohort C — Broader iPhone users

Purpose: onboarding clarity, App Store positioning, trust, and visual polish.

Size: 25–50 people.

## Recruitment screening questions

1. What is one goal you are actively trying to accomplish?
2. What tools have you used to manage goals or daily planning?
3. What usually causes your plans to break down?
4. Would you be willing to give feedback after the first session and after three days?
5. What iPhone and iOS version are you using?

## Selection priority

Prioritize testers who can articulate:

- goal friction
- planning breakdowns
- trust concerns
- product confusion
- return intent

## Red flags from testers

Do not over-weight feedback from testers who only want:

- more widgets
- more dashboards
- streak systems
- chatbot features
- generic task management
- automation without trust controls

## Recruitment success criteria

Recruit enough testers to evaluate:

- comprehension
- trust
- recommendation believability
- onboarding clarity
- return intent
- iPhone-native polish
