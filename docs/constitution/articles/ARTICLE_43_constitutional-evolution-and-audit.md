# Article 43 — Constitutional evolution and audit

## EVOLUTION-001 — Amendment process

A constitutional amendment states:

- problem,
- current law,
- proposed law,
- rationale,
- superseded laws,
- affected source/projects/tests,
- migration impact,
- privacy/accessibility/performance impact,
- owner approval.

## EVOLUTION-002 — Stable IDs

Law and opportunity IDs are never reused. Removed laws remain in a supersession ledger.

## EVOLUTION-003 — Annual and pre-release audit

At minimum annually and before first App Store release, audit:

- law completeness,
- registry integrity,
- source-owner drift,
- test/proof coverage,
- dependency/security posture,
- accessibility,
- performance,
- privacy,
- stale or contradictory truth.

## EVOLUTION-004 — Evidence-driven amendment

Implementation difficulty alone is not grounds to weaken law. A change requires product, safety, and evidence reasoning.
