# Article 38 — Dependencies and supply chain

## SUPPLY-001 — Admission criteria

A dependency requires named owner, product need, native-alternative review, license, privacy/security review, maintenance health, version policy, test strategy, and removal plan.

## SUPPLY-002 — Minimal dependency posture

Do not add a dependency for trivial convenience or architecture theater. Binary dependencies require explicit approval.

## SUPPLY-003 — SBOM and vulnerability response

Release evidence includes dependency inventory/SBOM where supported and a process for vulnerability triage, patching, and removal.

## SUPPLY-004 — Egress boundary

No dependency may bypass network-egress, privacy, logging, analytics, or data-classification law.
