# Article 39 — Entitlements and monetization

## ENTITLEMENT-001 — Offline-core guarantee

Loss, expiration, or uncertainty of premium entitlement may not delete, corrupt, or make existing local private data inaccessible.

## ENTITLEMENT-002 — State model

Define active, trial, grace, billing retry, expired, revoked, restored, family-shared where supported, offline-cached, account mismatch, and unknown states.

## ENTITLEMENT-003 — Purchase integrity

Purchase, restore, and downgrade flows are accessible, non-coercive, App Store compliant, and independently testable.

## ENTITLEMENT-004 — Paywall ethics

No dark patterns, fake urgency, shame, blocked account deletion, or misleading comparison. Core offline product law remains explicit.
