# Article 37 — Localization and temporal culture

## LOCALIZATION-001 — Localizable construction

No user-facing string concatenation that prevents grammatical localization. Use pluralization, localized formatting, and semantic arguments.

## LOCALIZATION-002 — Temporal locale

Time and recurrence honor locale, calendar, first weekday, 12/24-hour preference, time zone, DST, and region. Unsupported non-Gregorian behavior is explicitly documented and tested.

## LOCALIZATION-003 — Layout resilience

Support text expansion, right-to-left layout, pseudolocalization, long names, and localized VoiceOver without hiding critical actions.

## LOCALIZATION-004 — Store localization

Localized metadata and screenshots follow the same product, privacy, and accessibility truth as the app.
