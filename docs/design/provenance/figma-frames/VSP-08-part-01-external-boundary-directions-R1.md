## Options

- `A Local First Membrane`: private local runtime is the dominant object; optional account and public packs orbit outside it.
- `B Airlock Review Gate`: every external attempt becomes a visible review gate before anything can leave the device.
- `C Reference Weather Layer`: Source Atlas freshness appears as ambient weather over local planning, not as a destination.
- `D Consent Ledger Field`: account, permissions, shares, shortcuts, and notifications become receipt-backed decisions.
- `E Quiet Degraded Mode`: offline, stale, revoked, unavailable, and no-account states are calm local modes.
- `F Boundary Constellation`: compact whole-system map of allowed, optional, degraded, and blocked external edges.

Owner selected direction: `B Airlock Review Gate`.

Selection record: `docs/design/provenance/README.md`.

`B Airlock Review Gate` is the selected implementation direction because it turns boundary law into an inspectable interaction, keeps the private runtime local, makes blocked egress honest, and avoids making Source Atlas, account, widgets, notifications, or external tools into root product surfaces.

## Product-Law Checklist

- Today / Goals / Time / You remain the only persistent surfaces.
- Capture remains global composer, not a tab/root surface.
- Motion remains behavior, not a root destination.
- Proof / Source / Privacy / History / Receipts remain inspection details.
- VSP-08 requires the approved VSP-01 shell and may not mutate shell, dock, crown, Capture, Search, route depth, or root IA.
- Offline core value remains usable without an account.
- Ambitions Account remains optional identity, entitlement, recovery/support, and future approved account capability.
- R2 / Source Atlas remains public/reference/freshness infrastructure only.
- Source Atlas remains invisible by default and must not become marketplace browsing or a product center.
- Private life graph, goals, captures, schedule, proof, receipts, behavior, inferred priorities, and private user context must not leave the device for R2 or Source Atlas.
- External/cloud LLMs are not core runtime architecture.
