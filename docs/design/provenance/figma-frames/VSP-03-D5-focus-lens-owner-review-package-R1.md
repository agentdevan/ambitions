## Design Finding

D5 moves VSP-03 from direction selection into a complete owner-review candidate package. The Goals object is a full-bleed Life Area Atlas with one selected life area expanded through a focus lens while neighboring life areas remain spatially present and subordinate. The base behavior is not a dashboard, project board, scorecard, habit tracker, or ranked goals list.

The package intentionally keeps the active focus lens dominant. Inactive `People` and `Home` labels are de-emphasized beneath the action row so they remain spatial context rather than competing controls.

## State Coverage

- calm/default selected life area;
- constrained pressure;
- protected recovery;
- cross-area conflict;
- waiting/blocked;
- moved/still-counts;
- open-field relation without Capture-tab drift;
- reduced-motion static proof.

## Accessibility And Motion Notes

- Dynamic Type: the lens must grow vertically; secondary territory labels collapse before truncation.
- VoiceOver: selected life area, recommendation, affected goal paths, controls, then proof receipt; decorative stars/weather are hidden.
- Reduce Motion: glass reflow becomes static proof step: before, decision, after.
- Reduce Transparency: material glow resolves to solid tonal surfaces with explicit boundaries.
- Increase Contrast: text must not rely on blur-only contrast.
- Haptics: visible/static confirmation is primary; haptics are reinforcement only.
