## Green Requirements

- explicit owner approval tied to frame IDs and screenshot proof;
- live SwiftUI render proof tied to current source and SHA;
- accessibility proof for Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, and haptics where relevant;
- validation command proof;
- VSP-08 privacy/offline/account/R2 boundary proof;
- no product-law or shell-authority violation.

## Rejected Interpretations

- Capture as tab, inbox, chatbot, root destination, or persistent floating button.
- Motion as tab, root destination, analytics feed, score, streak, or dashboard.
- Proof, Source, Privacy, History, or Receipts as root surfaces.
- Plan, Profile, Habits, or Insights as top-level IA.
- Figma candidate screenshot or source existence as Green proof.
