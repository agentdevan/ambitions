# Goals Native Pursuit Continuum NATIVE-R02 Design

Status: `APPROVED_OWNER_REVISION_CONTRACT`

Direction:

`AVF-GOALS-S08-INT-D09-NATIVE-R02 — Flagship Anatomy Restoration`

## Thesis

Preserve D09's native Goals → Life Area → Goal hierarchy and make the canonical
Goal visible as one embodied pursuit at compact and focused resolutions. The
same anchor, accepted-truth foundation, recorded support, present movement, and
decreasing future certainty must remain recognizable without becoming a card,
document, stepper, graph, or timeline.

## Root

The Goals root remains a strict Life Area index. Each Life Area is an open,
row-wide native drilldown with stable identity, one area-level truth, and an
authored posture signal. Home communicates active construction, Relationships
communicates protected balance, and Career communicates contained work through
different neutral geometry and rhythm—not child Goal names, scores, category
color, or repeated title/subtitle rows.

## Home

Every Home Goal receives the compact canonical anatomy: an open pursuit anchor,
identity, accepted truth, and a state foundation. The selected Goal adds
embedded Proof provenance and a decisive current movement because the fixture
supports those meanings. The whole selected passage remains the native Goal
navigation target. Peer Goals remain non-mutating inspection evidence; they do
not acquire fabricated actions.

## Focused Goal

The title and Home ownership establish identity without consuming the viewport.
Accepted truth is open content resting on three visible recorded supports. Proof
expands from that foundation. The current movement is the strongest operational
region and opens Goal Path through the existing route. Future certainty uses
three material postures: present movement is fully formed, possible-later is
partially formed, and conditional future is only bounded where a protected
relationship permits it. Relationship inspection keeps its existing native
push route.

## Visual grammar

- System typography with larger state and action roles; metadata stays readable.
- Matte deep graphite and mineral Light canvases; content remains opaque.
- Violet-indigo marks the active movement and selected pursuit only.
- Proof uses neutral structural supports, not pills, tags, or bullet-list identity.
- Partial seams and tonal foundations communicate state without full cards.
- SF Symbols remain restricted to familiar shell and disclosure actions.
- The Crowned Edge Dock is unchanged and remains provisional.

## Accessibility and adaptivity

At accessibility sizes, the anchor becomes a compact semantic header, support
marks become a vertical proof foundation, and certainty regions stack in native
reading order. The Goal identity, accepted truth, Proof, current movement,
future posture, relationship, actions, and Back behavior remain present. All
interactive targets remain at least 44 points. Reduce Motion removes continuity
animation; Reduce Transparency affects shell chrome only.

## Boundaries

No fixture identity or truth changes. No route or state-model changes. No new
screen. No runtime, production mutation, app-entry, canon, token, component API,
or dependency work. Screenshots remain evaluation evidence and
`production_baseline = false`.
