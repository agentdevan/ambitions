# Cross-Packet Contradiction Register

## Executive verdict

The provisional system is not uniformly incompatible with Ambitions, but it is contradicted in several structural areas. The highest-order conflicts are the selected six-control right-edge dock versus current four-root bottom-navigation law; the provisional Goals and Today root compositions versus current canonical surface contracts; universal cross-root identity versus missing canonical Life Area/Event/Schedule Placement records; and visual promises of exact restoration, multi-scope settlement, durable pending work, and complete accessibility without matching runtime representation or direct proof.

“Survives” below means the protected intent can remain for reconciliation. It does not approve a visual, authorize implementation, or declare current support.

## Conflict classification

- **Target-versus-live implementation gaps:** X-02, X-04, X-05, X-08, X-10–X-16, X-18–X-21.
- **Canon-versus-live-source drift:** X-06 (editable Life Areas), X-17 (“What Ambitions Knows”), and parts of X-08.

No product winner is selected. Where higher-authority canon and protected provisional intent conflict, the register routes the question to the named authority and preserves both facts.
