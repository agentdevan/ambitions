# Goals R03 — Bounded Codex Design Brief

## Objective

Produce one owner-review visual architecture for Goals that delivers high information value, low reading burden, and adult flagship sophistication.

## Protect

- Life Area-led ownership
- Goal identity before status
- accepted/current/adaptable pursuit continuity
- current Step(s), route, next movement, Proof, schedule fit, and one Linked Goal Lens
- native navigation, framework Back, root-path preservation, and owner handoff
- no dashboard, scores, streaks, or decorative constellation

## Correct

- R01: remove specification prose, repeated explanation, and serialized card depth.
- R02: reject extreme subtraction, juvenile scale, giant symbols/actions, low informational richness, and context erasure.

## Required composition law

> One dominant focus. Multiple legible layers. One unmistakable next action.

## Stop conditions

Stop before native implementation if the first viewport cannot show Life Area direction, Goal identity/trajectory, a material condition, and a next movement without a paragraph. Stop if the candidate resembles a children’s tracker, generic dashboard, card stack, or one-button landing page.

## Proof sequence

1. Text anatomy and compact wire composition.
2. Owner-fit review.
3. One native typical-dark candidate only.
4. Owner review.
5. Only after survival: Light, dense, accessibility, RTL, interaction, and full proof.

No production integration, canon change, or owner approval is implied.
