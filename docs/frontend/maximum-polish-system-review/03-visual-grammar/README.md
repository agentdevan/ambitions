# 03 — Empirical Ambitions Flagship Visual Grammar

## Evidence boundary

This grammar is derived from owner-approved Shell and Time evidence, the ledger-backed Today direction, and current canon. Goals R01/R02 and Global Object Language R01 are not positive authority.

## Invariants

1. **System-native foundation, authored composition.** Use native navigation, controls, focus, safe areas, selection, scrolling, and presentation; differentiation comes from object relationships, hierarchy, continuity, and semantic expression.
2. **Identity before state; truth before command.** A command never becomes the hero until identity, current truth, and consequence make it intelligible.
3. **One dominant field, layered context.** The first viewport has a clear center of gravity but may contain subordinate contextual layers; it is not restricted to one visible object.
4. **Matte operational content; material is localized.** Routine content belongs on quiet semantic planes. Glass/relief is justified by shell/transient function, not universal card styling.
5. **Meaning survives effect removal.** Color, depth, transparency, and motion reinforce structure; they do not carry the only signal.
6. **SF typography with semantic amplitude.** Title roles identify context/object; compact text roles carry truth, evidence, consequence, and action. No ornamental display family is required.
7. **Restrained shape language.** Native controls keep native anatomy. Routine content does not acquire a branded rounded rectangle by default.
8. **Dense information may be visually calm.** Time R02 demonstrates that exact chronology, multiple states, and scale switching can remain legible through alignment, rhythm, local emphasis, and stable semantics.
9. **Root identity comes from content ownership, not color coding.** Today, Goals, Time, and You differ by their canonical object and composition.
10. **Accessibility is a semantic recomposition.** At pressure, preserve identity, accepted truth, consequence, primary action, owner route, and return; move supporting detail deeper.

## Preferred ranges, not frozen tokens

- Spacing cadence: 4/8/12/16/20/24/32 with larger separation only for true composition boundaries.
- Interactive target: at least 44×44 points.
- First viewport: one primary composition plus approximately two to four subordinate information layers; not one-object-only and not a dashboard.
- Accent: one governed action accent, with semantic status distinct from action. **Conflict:** current canon names restrained violet-indigo, while several Maximum Polish boards use SeaGlass/teal. This is unresolved until FR-3.
- Material: opaque/matte content as default; functional transient material and shell glass only where behavior justifies it.

## Root-specific exceptions

- Shell may use bounded custom dock expression over native navigation, pending device proof.
- Time may use higher information density and grid/chronology geometry because exact temporal truth is its owned instrument.
- Today may use a living field/continuum because day admission and current movement are primary.
- Goals must express pursuit continuity and relationships without becoming a metrics dashboard or a single oversized icon/action.

## Prohibited misreadings

- “One dominant field” does not mean one visible object.
- “Low cognitive load” does not mean low informational richness.
- “Native” does not mean a stock settings/list template.
- “Premium” does not mean more glass, gradients, cards, or animation.
- “Calm” does not mean empty.
