# Workstream Boundaries

A material finding has exactly one owning workstream and one `MP-XXX` identifier. Other workstreams cite that identifier. Shared ledgers contain summaries, never duplicate findings.

| # | Workstream | Owns | Explicitly does not own |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Canonical state | Status, chronology, supersession, next action | Visual quality, process causality, proof validity |
| 02 | Owner decision model | Demonstrated owner-fit criteria and review outcome protocol | Shared visual grammar or regression detection |
| 03 | Visual grammar | Positive static visual laws derived from approved evidence | Cross-root journeys, owner taste, motion |
| 04 | Anti-patterns | Named bad-output patterns and detection signals | Why the process generated them |
| 05 | Goals R03 | Goals-specific recovery architecture and bounded brief | Rendering or implementation |
| 06 | Cross-root coherence | Relationships and contradictions among whole experiences | Static token system or production mapping |
| 07 | Process autopsy | Causal defects in Maximum Polish method and corrected controls | Candidate visual design |
| 08 | Proof integrity | Validity and ceiling of evidence claims | Whether a visual direction is desirable |
| 09 | Packet factory | Context-minimized instructions for approved future jobs | Stage economics or owner approval |
| 10 | FR-2 handoff | Design-synthesis entry dossier and reopening boundaries | Production translation |
| 11 | Golden masters | Regression baselines and delta classification | Intrinsic quality of new directions |
| 12 | State completeness | Coverage of product states and transitions | Accessibility transform rules |
| 13 | Cognitive ergonomics | Information consumption and copy-compression rules | Visual styling |
| 14 | Accessibility | Structural accessibility transformations and proof needs | Localization/RTL |
| 15 | Localization/RTL/temporal | Language, direction, format, and calendar resilience | Assistive technology |
| 16 | Truth language | Epistemic-state semantics | Persistence implementation |
| 17 | Motion/haptics | Time-based experience behavior and proof | Static layout |
| 18 | FR-3 traceability | Design-to-production ownership and risk mapping | FR-2 direction selection |
| 19 | Iteration economics | When expensive work is permitted; stop-loss | Per-job context format |
| 20 | Signature defensibility | Ownable product experiences and anti-commodity doctrine | General coherence or visual tokens |

## Routing rules

- A bad screen characteristic routes to **04**; the causal gate/prompt defect routes to **07**.
- A preference demonstrated by Devan routes to **02**; a repeatable approved static composition law routes to **03**.
- A contradiction between roots routes to **06**; a contradiction between design and production architecture routes to **18**.
- Missing product states route to **12**; accessibility transformations route to **14**; locale/RTL transformations route to **15**.
- Job packet size routes to **09**; whether the job should be allowed to reach expensive proof routes to **19**.
- FR-2 receives design authority from **10**; FR-3 receives production traceability from **18**.
