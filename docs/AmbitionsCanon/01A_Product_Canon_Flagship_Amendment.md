# 01A — Product Canon Flagship Amendment

Status: active amendment, docs-only.

This amendment updates `01_Product_Canon.md` for the Ambitions Flagship Interface canon without deleting the older document history.

## Supersession Rule

Where `01_Product_Canon.md` says the fourth top-level tab is Plan, that wording is superseded by the active flagship canon.

The active top-level IA is:

```text
Today
Goals
Capture
Time
You
```

Plan is no longer a top-level destination in active flagship canon. Plan remains valid only as an action or contextual noun, such as Adjust plan, Shape week, or Review pressure.

## Active Product Hierarchy

```text
Goals → Time → Today
Capture feeds all three.
You governs trust, privacy, defaults, and automation.
```

Goals define direction.
Time shapes capacity.
Today starts what fits now.
Capture holds what has no place yet.
You controls trust, privacy, and defaults.

## Active Copy Amendments

- Use `Time`, not `Plan`, as the top-level tab.
- Use `Shape Time` as the Time screen title.
- Use `Shape week`, `Review pressure`, and `Adjust plan` as actions.
- Use `Trust & Automation`, not `Automation & Trust`, as preferred user-facing label.
- Use `Receipts & History` for the receipt archive inside You.

## Active Hard Reds

Stop and repair if:

1. Plan returns as a top-level tab.
2. Mission Control becomes top-level.
3. Time becomes a calendar clone, agenda, or schedule dashboard.
4. Today becomes a task list or calendar timeline.
5. Goals becomes KPI dashboard, habit tracker, astrology, or ranked life score.
6. Capture becomes feed, inbox, chatbot, category board, or plus-tab UI.
7. You becomes social profile, family hub, admin console, or AI settings wall.
8. Compatibility names appear as active user-facing language.
9. Visual Green/flagship/award-caliber is claimed without screenshots or rendered previews.
10. Release, public accessibility, performance, TestFlight, App Store, or production readiness is claimed without matching proof.
